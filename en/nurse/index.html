A registered nurse who can run the clinical side of the day: patient preparation, procedures, records and aftercare.

  • Active DHA registered nurse licence, or readiness to obtain it
  • Nursing degree and at least 2 years of recent experience
  • Outpatient, aesthetic, dental, pediatric or home care background
  • Accurate records and infection control
  • English; Arabic, Russian or Tagalog as a plus depending on your patients

Licensing for nurses (DHA)

Nurses need a DHA licence to work in Dubai. Candidates from abroad go through verification and the DHA nursing exam.

1
DataFlow (PSV): verification of nursing degree, licence and experience.
2
DHA exam: the registered nurse assessment (Prometric).
3
DHA licence: issued once the file and exam are cleared.
4
Facility linking: the licence is activated under your clinic.

Nurses already licensed in Dubai can transfer quickly. We tell you which route each candidate needs before the interview.
