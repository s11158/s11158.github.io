Who we place

A therapist who assesses precisely, sets clear goals and works with parents between sessions.

  • DHA licence as speech and language therapist, or eligibility
  • Degree in speech and language therapy or pathology
  • Children, adults, autism, stuttering or swallowing
  • Assessment reports and progress tracking
  • English; Arabic or Russian is in high demand

Licensing (DHA)

Speech and language therapists in clinical settings are licensed allied health professionals in Dubai. Therapists working in schools may follow a different regulator.

1
DataFlow (PSV): verification of degree, licence and experience.
2
DHA assessment: an exam or interview for the category, where required.
3
DHA licence: issued once the file and assessment are cleared.
4
Facility linking: the licence is activated under your clinic.

Bilingual therapists are rare, so for Russian or Arabic speakers we also search outside the UAE.
