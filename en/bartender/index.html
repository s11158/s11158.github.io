Who we help you find

A bartender who makes cocktails fast and consistently, keeps the bar in order, sells well and knows the alcohol service rules in Dubai.

  • Cocktails: IBA classics, signature recipes, non-alcoholic cocktails that Dubai guests order more and more
  • Speed: peak service at brunch and weekends, ticket order, teamwork with waiters
  • Selling: recommendations, upselling premium items, wine and spirits knowledge
  • Rules: alcohol served only under the venue licence, checking guest age, refusing an intoxicated guest
  • Control: inventory, losses, prep, POS and cash discipline
  • Hygiene and service: a clean bar, ice and syrups to Dubai Municipality standards, guest rapport

What to check before you hire

Flair videos do not show speed at peak or honesty with the till. The check happens behind the bar, and your bar manager decides.

1
Bar check: the candidate makes five or six cocktails from your menu and the classics. We look at recipe accuracy, speed, presentation and cleanliness.
2
Practical check at peak: a skills check during peak hours: handling the ticket flow, talking to guests, leaving the bar in order at the end.
3
Guest situations: an intoxicated guest wants another drink, a guest asks for a non-alcoholic option, a VIP table is waiting. How the candidate acts.
4
References: calls with bar managers: reliability, inventory results, relations with the team.

Typical mistake: hiring a bartender for the show and followers without checking speed and consistency at peak. Another one: not checking honesty and stock control, because bar losses quickly eat the margin when inventory does not match.

FAQ

How much does a bartender earn in Dubai?

In 2026 a barback in Dubai earns about AED 2,500 - 3,500 per month, a bartender AED 3,500 - 6,000 and a senior or head bartender AED 6,000 - 12,000. Tips and service charge add noticeably to income.

What should a bartender know about alcohol service in Dubai?

Alcohol is served only in licensed venues, guests must be at least 21, and a bartender must be able to politely refuse an intoxicated guest. The bartender needs to know and follow these rules. We test this with situational questions during selection.
