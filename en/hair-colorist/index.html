Hair Colorist Recruitment in Dubai

Hair colorists for salons in Dubai. Color is where salons earn the most per visit and lose clients fastest, so we check real work on a model.

Hire a hair coloristLooking for a job?

Who we place

A colorist who delivers predictable blondes and corrections, and whose clients rebook every 6-8 weeks.

  • Blonding, balayage, airtouch and toning
  • Color correction
  • Brands they work with and formulation knowledge
  • Consultation and aftercare advice
  • English; Russian or Arabic is a plus

What to check before hiring

We check the things that predict repeat clients.

1
Trial: a model color service in your salon.
2
Portfolio: before and after in daylight, not filtered.
3
Retention: how many clients rebook and how often.
4
Health card: the occupational health card required for salon staff in Dubai.

The employer sponsors the work permit and residence visa through MOHRE.

Cost of hiring a hair colorist in Dubai

Besides the salary, the employer covers the work permit and residence visa, medical test, Emirates ID and mandatory health insurance. By UAE law these costs cannot be deducted from the employee's pay.
