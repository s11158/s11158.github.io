FAQ.

Which booking system is best?

There is no universal answer. A salon needs master calendars, online booking and commissions; a clinic needs patient records and compliant storage of medical data. We compare 2-3 options for your format and you make the decision.

Do we need a CRM if we have a booking system?

Not always. If clients mostly book themselves, a booking system is enough. A CRM is needed when many enquiries come through messengers, consultations are long or high-ticket services are sold over several touches.

Who pays for software licences?

You do, directly to the vendor at their official prices. We show licence costs upfront in the comparison.

What about clinic patient data?

Medical patient data is kept in the medical system or in a CRM with storage in the UAE, as the UAE health data law requires. See our clinic solution page for details.
