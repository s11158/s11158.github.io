Competitor analysis and growth strategy

We look at your market through the client's eyes: how competitors reply, what they charge, what clients praise and criticise, where and how they advertise. You do not get a folder of screenshots but a positioning and a 90-day action plan.

When you need it.

  • Opening a business or a new branch - to enter the market with a clear difference, not the lowest price
  • Sales or enquiries are falling - to see what changed with competitors and clients
  • You want to raise prices - to know what clients are ready to pay more for
  • Launching a new service - to see how others sell it and where the gap is
  • Moving into a new area or emirate - so you do not copy blindly what worked somewhere else

What we research.

We use only open data and what any client can see.

  • Mystery shopping - we message and call competitors as clients: reply speed, script, follow-up, price
  • Prices and packages - a price and offer matrix for your key services
  • Google Maps reviews - what clients praise and criticise, which pains repeat
  • Instagram and content - what they post, which formats get a response, how the profile looks
  • Advertising - the public Meta Ad Library and Google Ads Transparency Center
  • Website and search visibility - which searches find them and what the path to an enquiry looks like

What you get.

  • Competitor map - strengths and weaknesses of each company that really takes your clients
  • Price and offer matrix - where you are more expensive, where cheaper and why
  • Mystery shopper report - chat screenshots and a score against the same criteria
  • Positioning - how you are different and how to say it to a client in one sentence
  • 90-day plan - marketing, sales, service and team, with priorities and owners
  • Review session with the owner - a Zoom meeting to go through the findings and answer questions

Frequently asked questions.

Is mystery shopping legal?

We message and call competitors as ordinary clients and use only open information: replies, prices, public pages and reviews. We do not request confidential data, we do not record calls without consent, and we keep chat screenshots.

How long does a competitor analysis take?

As an orientation, about two weeks for 5-10 competitors. With more channels and competitors we state the timeline in the plan in advance.

Can we order only a strategy without the analysis?

A strategy without market analysis turns into guesswork. We can do a shorter version: fewer competitors and channels, with the same outcome - a positioning and an action plan.

How are you different from a marketing agency?

We do not sell advertising, so we gain nothing from telling you to raise the budget. We look at the whole client path: ads, reply, booking, repeat sale and team, and often find money not in ads but in reply speed and work with the client base.
