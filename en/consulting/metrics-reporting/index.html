Metrics, control and management reporting

An owner should see what matters in five minutes: what you earned, where utilisation is dropping, who in the team is falling behind and whether there is enough cash for next month. We pick the 10-15 metrics that really run the business, set up automatic calculation and introduce a rhythm: a daily summary, a weekly meeting, a monthly report.

When you need it.

  • Only the accountant has the numbers, once a month - while decisions have to be made every day
  • Reports are put together by hand - the front desk spends hours on spreadsheets and the numbers still do not match
  • It is unclear who in the team brings in the money - there is no utilisation or revenue by master or doctor
  • Revenue grows but profit does not - you need a management P&L, not just a bank statement
  • The owner wants to step back from daily operations - control through numbers instead of constant presence

What you get.

  • Metric tree - from profit down to utilisation, average ticket, retention and conversion
  • Automatic calculation - data comes from your CRM, booking system, POS and accounting with no manual entry
  • Owner dashboard - one page with the key numbers by day, week and team member
  • Daily summary - a short report on WhatsApp or Telegram every morning
  • Management report - monthly P&L, cash flow, plan versus actual
  • Management rhythm - meeting format, who owns which number and what to do when it drifts from plan

FAQ.

Do we need to buy new software?

Usually not. We start with what you already have: CRM, booking system, POS, bank. We suggest a new tool only if the data cannot be collected without it.

How many metrics does an owner need?

Usually 5-7 numbers on the daily screen and 10-15 in the weekly review. More rarely helps: people stop looking at the report.

Does this replace the accountant?

No. The accountant keeps the books and handles taxes. Management reporting answers a different question: what is happening in the business right now and which decisions to make. We reconcile the two so the numbers match.
