FAQ.

When will we see results?

The first changes usually show during the work with your client base: some clients return after well-written personal messages. A lasting effect comes from the combination of base, reply speed, team and metrics, so targets are set for the end of the project.

Do you guarantee revenue growth?

We agree metric targets and honestly show what was achieved. The result depends on the market and on how the team takes the changes, so promising a specific revenue figure would be dishonest.

Do we need to change the CRM or booking system?

Only if the current one gets in the way. More often it is enough to reconfigure what you have and train the team to use it.

How much does the package cost?

The price depends on the format, the size of the business and what you already have in place. After a free review we quote a fixed price per stage. You can start with the first stage and decide on the next ones as you go.
