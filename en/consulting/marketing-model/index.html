Marketing model: what a client really costs

We calculate where clients will come from, what each one costs and how many will come back. We connect advertising with what happens next: reply speed, booking and repeat visits. You get a budget by channel and month, and clear targets for your team and contractors.

When you need it.

  • You are preparing a launch - so you have bookings from day one and a costed launch budget
  • You pay for ads but cannot see the return - to separate channels that bring clients from those that only bring enquiries
  • Lots of enquiries, few bookings - to find where clients get lost between the ad and the visit
  • You are choosing an ads contractor - to give them numeric targets and check the result
  • Clients come once and never return - to build retention instead of buying new clients forever

What you get.

  • Funnel in numbers - impressions, enquiries, bookings, visits and repeat visits for each channel
  • Channels and budget - Google, Instagram, Google Maps and reviews, partnerships, broadcasts to your base, with a budget for each month
  • Client cost and value - what it costs to win a client and what they bring in a year
  • Launch plan - what to do 4-6 weeks before opening and in the first 90 days
  • Retention - repeat visits, memberships, reminders and broadcasts within WhatsApp rules
  • Targets for the team and contractors - which numbers to check every week

FAQ.

Do you run the ads yourselves?

We do not sell advertising or take a percentage of the budget, so we gain nothing by telling you to spend more. We build the model and plan, set targets and help you choose a contractor and check their work. If needed, we bring in trusted ads specialists.

How is this different from an agency media plan?

A media plan stops at enquiries. A marketing model goes further: how many enquiries become bookings, how many clients show up and return, and how much money that brings. That is why the money is often found not in the budget but in reply speed and work with your client base.

What about clinics with advertising restrictions?

Medical advertising in Dubai follows DHA rules. We reflect this in the model: more weight on reviews, expert content, work with the patient base and partnerships, and ad materials planned with time for approval.
