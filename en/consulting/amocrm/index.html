What amoCRM fixes.

Clients usually come to us with one of these problems. Each one is solved by a specific setup, not by buying a licence.

  • Chats drown in Instagram DMs and personal WhatsApp - all channels in one inbox, every enquiry becomes a deal with an owner
  • Managers forget to call back - automatic tasks, reminders and control of overdue follow-ups
  • Clients do not come back - repeat visit triggers, segments and broadcasts to the database
  • You do not know where leads come from or where they are lost - sources, pipeline stages and loss reason reports
  • Everything depends on one administrator - processes live in the system, not in one person's head

Frequently asked questions.

How much does an amoCRM implementation cost?

It depends on the number of users, pipelines, channels and integrations. After a free review we give a fixed project price. amoCRM licences are paid separately, per user per month, at the official price list.

How long does an amoCRM implementation take?

As an orientation, a basic setup with messengers connected takes 2-4 weeks. Complex projects with several pipelines, integrations and a chatbot run in stages; you get the plan after the audit.

Can WhatsApp be connected to amoCRM?

Yes. WhatsApp connects through the official WhatsApp Business API or through marketplace integrations. We help you choose the option, connect the number and set up message templates for broadcasts.

We already have amoCRM but nobody uses it. Can you help?

Yes, this is common. We audit the account, remove clutter, rebuild pipelines and automations, train the team and check in the first weeks that the system is actually used.

How can I check that you are an amoCRM partner?

Scan the QR code on the certificate or open the certificate page on the amoCRM website using the link under the image. It shows the status, the partner ID and the issue date.
