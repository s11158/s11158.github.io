When you need it.

  • Invoices are made in Word or Excel - numbers get mixed up and VAT is calculated by hand
  • Commissions are worked out in the evenings - and every month there is an argument about the numbers
  • Visa, Emirates ID and licence dates are tracked from memory - and fines arrive unexpectedly
  • Receipts live on the owner's phone - and the accountant chases them every month
  • The company is growing - time to put the books in order before a tax audit, not after

What you get.

  • Invoicing - templates with TRN and 5% VAT, numbering, sending by email or WhatsApp, payment tracking
  • Salaries and commissions - visits and sales from the booking system, clear rules, an export for payroll (WPS)
  • HR records - employee files, contracts, leave, sick days, end-of-service calculations
  • Document calendar - expiry dates for visas, Emirates ID, medical and professional licences, with reminders
  • Expenses and receipts - photo of the receipt into the software, categories, limits and approvals
  • Month-end close - a checklist for you and your accountant, bank and cash reconciliation

FAQ.

Do you do the bookkeeping?

No, we implement software and processes. Bookkeeping and tax filings are handled by your accountant or accounting firm. If you do not have one, we can help find and assess a candidate; the hiring decision is yours.

Will the system be ready for e-invoicing?

The UAE is phasing in mandatory electronic invoicing. We choose software whose developers state support for the new requirements and take this into account in the setup.

My accountant already uses their own software

Then we work around it: we set up invoices, exports and data exchange so it is convenient for your accountant and you never enter data twice.
