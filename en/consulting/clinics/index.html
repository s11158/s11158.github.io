Where a clinic loses patients.

In almost every clinic the money leaks in the same places, and most often not in advertising.

  • A reply in an hour, not in a minute - a patient writes to several clinics at once and books with whoever answers first
  • No-shows and late cancellations - without reminders, doctors' slots stay empty, and a doctor's time is the clinic's most expensive resource
  • A treatment plan with no follow-up - after the consultation the patient got a plan for a course or an implant, and then nobody reminded them
  • Chats on personal phones - when an administrator leaves, the history of conversations with patients leaves too
  • The same questions all day - prices, insurance, parking and free slots take hours the front desk needs for patients in the clinic
  • The owner does not see the numbers - how many enquiries, how many bookings, how many patients reached the doctor and from which ads

What we set up.

  • All channels in one inbox - WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram, website forms and telephony; every enquiry becomes a deal with a responsible administrator
  • Pipelines by department - separate flows for consultations, treatment courses, dentistry or surgery when they have different cycles and doctors
  • Reminders with confirmation - WhatsApp template messages the day before and on the day, with a reply to confirm or reschedule; a freed slot is visible to the front desk at once
  • Treatment plans under control - if a patient does not book after the consultation, the administrator gets a task and the patient gets a gentle reminder
  • Repeat visits - an invitation for a check-up or the next treatment at the right time, for patients who agreed to receive messages
  • Link to the medical system - medical records and appointments stay in the clinic's medical system, and the CRM handles everything before and after the visit; if the system has an API, we connect them with no double entry
  • A daily owner report - enquiries, bookings, no-shows, patients who reached the doctor and advertising sources

An AI chatbot for a clinic: what it may and may not do.

In healthcare a chatbot has to be more careful than anywhere else. We set the limits before launch and test them on your patients' real questions.

  • It answers - about prices, services, doctors, address, parking, opening hours and accepted insurance from your current list
  • It books - offers free slots and passes the booking to the administrator or the booking system
  • It never diagnoses - no medical advice, no discussion of symptoms, treatment or test results; such questions go straight to an administrator or a doctor
  • It knows about emergencies - if a patient describes an acute condition, the bot advises calling an ambulance on 998 and alerts a staff member
  • It promises no results - no promises like "no side effects" or "best in Dubai": Dubai medical advertising standards forbid such wording
  • It speaks the patient's language - English, Arabic, Russian and other languages

UAE rules we build into the system.

We do not replace the clinic's lawyer, but we build the system so that it does not create extra risk. The final decision on documents and consents stays with the clinic and its medical director.

  • Health data stays in the UAE - UAE Federal Law No. 2 of 2019 on ICT in health fields (Article 13) requires health data to be stored and processed in the country, with exceptions listed in Ministerial Resolution No. 51 of 2021; so the CRM holds contacts, interest and bookings, while diagnoses, test results and medical records stay in a medical system hosted in the UAE
  • Broadcasts only with consent - the same law bars using health data for non-health purposes without the patient's written consent, and the UAE personal data law gives the right to object to marketing; we record consent in the client card and always offer an easy opt-out
  • Messages that follow DHA standards - Dubai standards for medical advertising on social media also cover WhatsApp: no "best", "100%" or "instant results", and patient photos and testimonials only with written consent; we check bot answers and broadcast templates against these rules
  • Calls within telemarketing rules - since 2024, marketing calls in the UAE are allowed only from 9:00 to 18:00, from company numbers and with frequency limits, so the main channel for working the database is messages to people who gave consent
  • Role-based access - administrators see bookings and contacts, the marketer sees only statistics, and only the owner can export the whole database

Frequently asked questions.

Can patient data be stored in amoCRM?

Usually the CRM holds enquiries, contacts and bookings without medical details. Diagnoses, test results, medical records and before-and-after photos stay in the clinic's medical system: UAE law requires health data to be stored and processed in the country. We agree where the line is for your clinic with your medical director, and if you need to keep more in the CRM, we will propose a solution with data hosted in the UAE.

Will the chatbot start giving medical advice?

No. The bot answers only from your knowledge base: prices, services, doctors, insurance, address and booking rules. It does not diagnose, recommend treatment or discuss test results, and hands such questions straight to an administrator or a doctor. If a patient describes an acute condition, the bot advises calling 998 and alerts a staff member.

Will reminders reduce no-shows?

A reminder with confirmation is the simplest tool: the patient confirms or reschedules in advance, and the freed slot can be given to someone else. We will not promise a specific percentage: we measure the effect on your own numbers before and after launch.

Can the chatbot tell a patient whether you accept their insurance?

Yes, if it has an up-to-date list of the insurers and plans the clinic works with. If a plan is unclear or a pre-approval is needed, the bot hands the chat to an administrator.

We already have a medical system. Why a CRM?

The medical system handles appointments, medical records and billing. The CRM handles everything before and after the visit: enquiries from messengers, reply speed, reminders, treatment plans, repeat visits and advertising sources. If the medical system has an API, we connect them so the front desk does not enter data twice.

How much does it cost for a clinic?

It depends on the number of doctors, branches, channels and integrations. After a free review we give a fixed project price and timeline. amoCRM licences are paid at the official price list and WhatsApp messages at Meta's rates; we estimate these costs in advance and show them in the proposal.
