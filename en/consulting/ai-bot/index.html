An AI chatbot that answers like your best front desk

We train an AI chatbot on your prices, services and rules. It answers clients day and night, takes them to a booking, handles typical questions and hands complex cases to a person. It can also write first: appointment reminders, invitations for a repeat treatment and offers for clients who agreed to receive messages.

What the bot does.

A good bot does not replace your administrator. It takes the routine off them and keeps leads from going cold.

  • Replies in seconds - at night, at weekends and when the administrator is busy with a client
  • Knows your business - prices, services, specialists, address, booking and cancellation rules; it answers from the knowledge base and does not make things up
  • Gets to a result - asks clarifying questions, offers a time slot and books or creates an enquiry
  • Calls a person in time - hands the chat to the administrator when the question is complex or the client asks for a human
  • Saves everything in the CRM - name, phone, interest, source and the full conversation in the client record
  • Speaks the client's language - English, Arabic, Russian and other languages

The bot writes first: broadcasts and reminders.

The easiest money is in the client base you already have. The bot starts the conversation at the right moment:

  • Appointment reminder - a day before the visit, with an option to confirm or reschedule
  • Repeat treatment - an invitation the right number of weeks after the service
  • Win-back - a personal message to clients who have not visited for a long time
  • Segment offers - a new service or free slots for the clients it is relevant to
  • Review after the visit - a short question about the experience and a link to a Google review

How broadcasts stay within the rules.

On WhatsApp, a message from a business outside the 24-hour window after the client's last message can only be sent through the official WhatsApp Business API, using templates approved by Meta. We write only to people who gave consent and always give an easy way to opt out. This keeps the number's quality rating high and clients calm.

Instagram and Telegram have their own limits: for example, a Telegram bot cannot message a person first if they have never started it. We design the flows around each platform's rules.

Every day at a convenient time, for example before opening, you get a short report in Telegram or WhatsApp:

Frequently asked questions.

Will the bot give clients wrong answers?

The bot answers only from your knowledge base and rules. If the answer is not there, it does not invent one but hands the chat to a person. We test it on real questions before launch and review conversations and retrain it every week after launch.

Can we send WhatsApp broadcasts without getting blocked?

Yes, if you use the official WhatsApp Business API, write only to people who gave consent, use approved templates and do not send too often. That is how we set it up, and we watch the number's quality rating.

Will the bot replace our administrator?

No. The bot takes typical questions, night and weekend enquiries, reminders and broadcasts. The administrator handles clients who need a real person and does it without rushing.

How much does an AI chatbot cost?

The setup price is fixed after a free review. WhatsApp messages are paid separately at Meta's rates, as is AI model usage: we estimate these costs in advance and show them in the proposal.

Is it safe for client data?

The bot only gets access to the data it needs to answer and book. We do not give it extra rights, and conversations are stored in your CRM. For medical businesses we also agree which topics the bot does not discuss at all.
