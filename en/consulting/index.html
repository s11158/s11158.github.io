When it is too early.

If you get a few enquiries a week and have one employee, a full CRM may be too much for now. We will say so on the free review and suggest something simpler: a clean spreadsheet, a booking system, or only a bot for night-time messages.

Frequently asked questions.

Do you come on site or work remotely?

We are based in Dubai, and all our clients are UAE businesses. The review, audit and team training happen at your place: your salon, clinic or office. The technical setup is done on our side and we show you the result in meetings, and we stay available in your working hours.

How is the consulting practice different from hiring?

It is a separate service with a separate contract. You can order only a CRM implementation, only a chatbot or only a competitor analysis, without any hiring. If you need both, we connect them: the person you hire starts working in a system that is already set up.

How much does a CRM or an AI chatbot cost?

It depends on the number of users, channels and integrations. After a free review we give you a fixed project price and a timeline. CRM licences are paid to the vendor at its official price list; WhatsApp message fees and AI usage are estimated in advance and shown in the proposal.

How long does an implementation take?

As an orientation: a basic CRM with messengers connected goes live in 2-4 weeks, and a full implementation with a chatbot, broadcasts and reports usually takes 1-2 months. You get an exact staged plan after the audit.

Can you fix a CRM we already pay for?

Yes. Often the CRM is there but nobody uses it. We audit it, rebuild pipelines and automations, connect channels and train the team. If the system does not fit you, we will say so and help you move.

Will an AI chatbot tell clients nonsense?

The bot answers only from your knowledge base: prices, services, rules. We test it on real questions before launch, define what it hands over to a person, and review conversations every week. The bot never gives medical advice.
