When Zoho CRM is the right choice.

Zoho is strong where sales are only one part of the process and need to connect with the rest of the company.

  • B2B sales with a long cycle - several contacts per deal, proposals, approvals
  • You need finance in the loop - invoices and accounting in Zoho Books next to the CRM, no double entry
  • Several departments or branches - complex access rights, different processes for different teams
  • Deep analytics - reports and dashboards, Zoho Analytics when needed
  • You already use Zoho - Mail, Desk, Books, People and other apps in one account

Zoho CRM or amoCRM?.

In short: amoCRM is simpler and faster to launch when sales run through messengers and reply speed matters. Zoho CRM is stronger as part of a larger ecosystem, when the CRM must connect with finance, projects and support. We implement both and will tell you honestly on the free review which one fits you.

Frequently asked questions.

Which is better for a small business: Zoho CRM or amoCRM?

If sales run mostly through WhatsApp and Instagram and you need a fast launch, amoCRM usually fits better. If you need the CRM connected to accounting, projects and several departments, Zoho CRM is stronger. We decide after reviewing your processes.

Can WhatsApp be connected to Zoho CRM?

Yes, through integrations and extensions from Zoho Marketplace or through the WhatsApp Business API. We pick the option, connect the number and make sure chats are saved in the client record.

Do you migrate data from Excel and other CRMs?

Yes. First we clean the data: duplicates, empty fields, different phone formats. Then we migrate it and spot-check it together with you.

How much does a Zoho CRM implementation cost?

The project price is fixed after a free review and depends on modules, integrations and data volume. Zoho licences are paid to the vendor at its official price list.
