Russian-speaking staff in Dubai

Russian-speaking clients are one of the largest and highest-spending groups in Dubai's clinics, salons, real estate and private households. We specialise in bilingual Russian and English staff.

Talk to us

Why businesses hire Russian speakers

  • Clients from Russia, Kazakhstan, Ukraine, Belarus and Central Asia prefer to consult in their language
  • Higher conversion from consultation to treatment or deal
  • Russian-speaking families look for nannies, tutors and assistants who speak the language with their children

Roles we fill most often

Aesthetic doctors and cosmetologists, nurses, laser specialists, nail technicians, hair stylists, lash artists, salon and clinic administrators, personal and executive assistants, nannies and governesses, sales executives and real estate agents.

How we work

TLNT is run in Russian and English. We source in both communities, check the real spoken level in the interview and handle visa and licensing questions for candidates coming from CIS countries.

FAQ

Can you find Russian-speaking doctors with a DHA licence?

Yes. We start with doctors already licensed in Dubai and also bring candidates from CIS countries through DataFlow and DHA licensing.

Do you check the Russian and English level?

Yes, both are checked in the interview, not taken from the CV.

How much does it cost?

Our fee is one month's salary of the placed employee: 50% when they start and 50% after probation. No full prepayment, and a free replacement within 2 months.
