Visa for domestic workers

A housekeeper works on a domestic worker visa sponsored by the family, under the standard MOHRE domestic worker contract.

1
Selection: interviews, reference calls and a trial day.
2
Contract: the standard MOHRE domestic worker contract.
3
Visa: sponsored by the family directly or through a Tadbeer centre.
4
Medical and Emirates ID: completed before the start.

The sponsor must meet the requirements for sponsoring domestic workers. We explain the steps for your situation.

Cost of hiring a housekeeper in Dubai

Besides the salary, the employer covers the work permit and residence visa, medical test, Emirates ID and mandatory health insurance. By UAE law these costs cannot be deducted from the employee's pay.

FAQ

How much does a housekeeper cost in Dubai?

A live-in housemaid typically earns 2,500 - 4,000 AED per month plus accommodation; an experienced villa housekeeper 4,000 - 6,000 AED; a head housekeeper 6,000 - 9,000 AED.

Who sponsors a housekeeper's visa?

The family sponsors a domestic worker visa directly or through a Tadbeer centre, using the standard MOHRE contract.

How do you check a housekeeper?

We call previous families, check the timeline of her work and arrange a trial day before you decide.
