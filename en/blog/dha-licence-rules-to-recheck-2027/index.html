DHA licence rules to re-check before you plan 2027 five points that move a doctor's start date

We are not announcing a new DHA rule here, because we only publish what we can source. What we can say is that DHA states in its own materials that it may change the format, content and pass score of its exams without notice. If a clinic is planning a launch or a new doctor in 2027, the safe move is to re-check five points against the official source before an offer goes out. This is the list our team uses.

Why a watch-list instead of a headline

Licensing rules in Dubai are set by the authority, and the candidate's own file decides the timeline. Our DHA licensing guide says it plainly: exact requirements depend on the specialty, the country of the degree and the experience, and DHA rules change periodically. So the useful question for an owner is not what changed this week but what to confirm before a date is promised to a doctor.

The official home for current rules is the Dubai Health Authority site, https://www.dha.gov.ae/. Treat it as the final word over any summary, including ours.

Five points to confirm before you plan a 2027 start

Each point below comes from the guides on this site; the licence manual and exam guideline on the DHA site are the source to check them against.

  • The licensed title: DHA assigns the title from the qualification, its tier and the years of experience, and the doctor may practise only within it. See DHA licence types. The figures there are from the April 2025 edition of the unified requirements, so check the current edition
  • The exam: three attempts are allowed, and after a third failure DHA blocks a new application for two years. DHA may change the format, content and pass score without notice. Details in DHA assessment and Prometric exam
  • Primary source verification: DataFlow confirms documents with each issuer, and the slowest issuer sets the pace. A result of Unable to Verify allows re-verification of the same document twice. See DataFlow verification
  • The transfer for a doctor already licensed: the licence is linked to a facility, the previous facility must release it, and the notice period is often the longest part. See DHA licence transfer
  • The authority: DOH in Abu Dhabi and MOHAP follow their own processes, so a licence from another emirate still needs a DHA licence in Dubai

What this means for a 2027 plan

A candidate who already holds an active DHA licence can start in around two weeks in the typical case, mostly facility linking. A candidate without a licence needs several weeks, sometimes months, for verification, the exam and processing. If your opening date is fixed, prioritise licensed candidates; if you have time, a strong unlicensed doctor can be guided through the process.

Build the plan backwards from the opening date, add the notice period for a licensed doctor, and leave slack for one re-verification. Do not promise a start date to a doctor abroad until the file has been reviewed.

A short routine to keep

Once a quarter, re-read the DHA pages for the titles you hire. Before each offer, confirm the licensed title, the exam route and the verification status. Write the date you checked into the hiring file. It takes minutes and prevents the most expensive surprise: a start date that moves by weeks.

FAQ

Has DHA announced new licence rules for 2027?

We have not verified any announcement, so this article does not claim one. DHA states that it may change exam format, content and pass score without notice, which is why we recommend re-checking the official source before each offer.

Where should an owner check current DHA rules?

On the official Dubai Health Authority site, https://www.dha.gov.ae/. Our guides summarise the process, but the authority's current manual and guidelines decide.

How long does it take a doctor to start in a Dubai clinic?

With an active DHA licence, around two weeks in the typical case, mostly facility linking and notice. Without a licence, several weeks and sometimes months for verification, the exam and processing.
