"Hire the coordinator before the second doctor" why patient coordinators pay for themselves

When a clinic in Dubai feels busy, the first idea is often a second doctor. But many clinics are busy at the desk, not in the treatment room: enquiries wait for answers, consultations end without a plan and undecided patients are never called back. A patient coordinator fixes that part first.

Talk to us

Where the clinic actually loses patients

A second doctor adds hours to the schedule. That helps only if the hours you already have are full of patients who decided to be treated. In many clinics the gap is earlier in the journey.

  • WhatsApp and Instagram enquiries answered hours later, after the patient has booked elsewhere
  • Consultations that end with "I will think about it" and no next step
  • Treatment plans explained by a busy doctor in five minutes, without prices on paper
  • No one who owns the follow-up call two days later

What a patient coordinator does

A coordinator guides the patient from the first message to the procedure and back for follow-up. It is a sales role in a medical setting, and it is non-clinical, so no DHA licence is needed. The full role profile is on our patient coordinator hiring page.

  • Supports consultations and presents the treatment plan and its price
  • Follows up undecided patients and rebooks those who missed a visit
  • Keeps every conversation in the CRM and on WhatsApp, so nothing is lost
  • For international patients: travel, hotel and transfer coordination

Why one person at the desk stops working

In a clinic with one or two doctors, one receptionist can answer calls and also explain treatment plans. With more traffic the roles split, because answering the phone and selling a treatment plan need different skills. If your receptionist is also your closer, both jobs suffer. Our guide to front office roles and pay shows how clinics divide the desk.

What a patient coordinator costs in Dubai in 2026

Market estimates per month from our role page and the Dubai Salary Index 2026:

  • Patient coordinator: 6,000 - 10,000 AED plus bonus
  • Senior or medical tourism coordinator: 10,000 - 15,000 AED plus bonus
  • Patient relations manager: 15,000 - 22,000 AED

How the role pays for itself

The logic is simple: a coordinator does not create new hours, but turns more of the consultations you already have into paid treatment plans. That is why the bonus should be paid on plans the patient actually paid for, not on consultations booked. Before you add a second doctor, compare two numbers for the last three months: how many consultations you had and how many became paid plans. If the gap is large, the coordinator comes first.

  • Bonus on paid treatment plans, with written rules
  • No bonus for discounts given to close a sale or pressure on patients
  • Two to four weeks of training on your software and scripts before full speed

How to test a candidate

Ask for their real conversion: how many consultations turned into treatment plans in their last clinic. Then run a role play with a price objection and a hesitant patient on WhatsApp, check clinic software and CRM, and confirm the visa status. You make the decision and the person is employed directly by your clinic; we help you find and assess candidates. See also the clinic receptionist role if you need both seats.

FAQ

Does a patient coordinator need a DHA licence in Dubai?

No. It is a non-clinical role, so no medical licence is needed; the employer sponsors the work permit.

How much does a patient coordinator earn in Dubai in 2026?

About 6,000 - 10,000 AED a month plus bonus, and 10,000 - 15,000 AED plus bonus for a senior or medical tourism coordinator.

When should a clinic hire a coordinator instead of a second doctor?

When the schedule is not full of decided patients: many consultations end without a paid plan and nobody owns the follow-up.
