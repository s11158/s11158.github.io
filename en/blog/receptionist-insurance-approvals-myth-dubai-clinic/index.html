Myth: any receptionist can run insurance approvals and when you need a dedicated person

Many Dubai clinic owners put insurance approvals on the front desk because the receptionist already holds the phone and the patient file. For a small clinic with few insured patients that can work. Once insurance becomes a large share of visits, the approval work needs its own owner.

Talk to us

Why the myth sounds true

A receptionist already checks the Emirates ID and the insurance card, so adding approvals looks like a small extra task. In a clinic where most patients pay themselves, it often is.

Where the front desk stops being enough

eClaimLink is the Dubai Health Authority portal clinics use to send insurance claims. The portal is simple; money is won or lost by the people who work with it. A receptionist who is also on the phone and at the door rarely has time to chase every approval.

  • Prior authorisation requested after the service, not before
  • Service delivered differs from what was approved
  • Rejections not read, not corrected and not resubmitted in time

What each role costs

Market estimates from our role pages (clinic receptionist, insurance officer), AED per month:

  • Receptionist / front desk: 4,500 - 7,000
  • Senior receptionist with insurance experience: 7,000 - 10,000
  • Insurance officer: 6,000 - 10,000
  • Medical coder: 8,000 - 14,000
  • Revenue cycle manager: 15,000 - 25,000

Rejected claims are silent revenue loss

Our eClaimLink guide has a calculator: enter your claims per month, average claim and rejected share, and it shows what rejections cost you. Compare that number with the monthly salary of a specialist before you decide.

How to choose for your clinic

Few insured visits: a receptionist with insurance experience may be enough. About half of patients insured: a dedicated insurance coordinator. Mostly insured: add a medical coder, because coding becomes a full-time job. We help find and assess candidates; you decide and employ directly, and the employer arranges the visa.

  • Test the real task: a rejected claim to analyse, not only a CV
  • Ask for rejection rates before and after their previous work
  • Check the insurers and systems they used
  • No medical licence is needed for the role; some coders hold a DHA coder licence

FAQ

Can a clinic receptionist do insurance approvals in Dubai?

Yes, for a small clinic with few insured patients, if the person has insurance experience. As insured visits grow, a dedicated insurance coordinator or officer is safer.

How much does a medical insurance officer earn in Dubai?

About 6,000 - 10,000 AED per month; medical coders 8,000 - 14,000 AED; revenue cycle managers 15,000 - 25,000 AED. Market estimates.
