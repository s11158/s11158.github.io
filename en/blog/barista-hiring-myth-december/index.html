Myth: a good barista is easy to hire in December and what to do instead

Dubai's busy season runs through the winter, and cafes, hotels and bakeries look for the same people at the same time. Owners who start the search in December usually choose from fewer candidates and pay more for speed.

Talk to us

Why the myth sounds true

There are many baristas in Dubai, and job boards fill up with CVs within days. But volume is not the same as choice: in the winter season experienced baristas are usually already working, and the ones on the market often have only a few months of specialty coffee behind them.

What actually happens in peak season

From late autumn new outlets open, hotel lobby lounges get busier and events need extra bar staff. Everyone hires at once.

  • The strongest baristas get two or three offers in the same week
  • Notice periods and visa steps push the start date by several weeks
  • Owners in a hurry choose by latte art photos instead of a real test

What a barista earns in Dubai in 2026

Monthly salary ranges from the Dubai Salary Index 2026:

  • Barista: 2,500 - 3,800 AED
  • Senior or specialty barista: 3,800 - 5,500 AED
  • Head barista or bar supervisor: 5,000 - 7,000 AED

The 4-part test to run before the offer

Run it on your own machine. It takes about 40 minutes and says more than any CV.

  • Calibration: dial in a new coffee and explain each change in grind, dose and time
  • Milk: a flat white, a cappuccino and an oat latte, three cups that look the same
  • Peak workflow: eight mixed tickets against the clock with a clean station
  • References: a call to the previous cafe manager about speed and punctuality

What to do instead

Start the search 6 to 8 weeks before you need the person, agree the package early, and keep one strong reserve candidate. The full role profile and test are on our barista hiring page; for the whole floor team see waiter and restaurant manager.

FAQ

How much does a barista earn in Dubai in 2026?

About 2,500 - 3,800 AED a month for a barista, 3,800 - 5,500 AED for a senior or specialty barista and 5,000 - 7,000 AED for a head barista, according to the Dubai Salary Index 2026.

When should a cafe start hiring for the winter season?

Six to eight weeks before the start date you need, so the notice period and visa steps fit in.

What is the best test for a barista?

A practical test on your own machine: calibration, milk on three cups in a row, a timed queue of mixed orders and a reference call.
