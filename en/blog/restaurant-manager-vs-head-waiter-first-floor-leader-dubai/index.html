Restaurant manager vs head waiter who should lead your floor first

A new restaurant in Dubai often needs one person to lead the floor before the team is complete. Two profiles come up again and again: a restaurant manager who owns the venue, and a head waiter who runs the service. They cost different money and solve different problems.

Talk to us

What a restaurant manager owns

The restaurant manager owns one venue end to end: the floor team and the rota, the guest experience and online reviews, the link between kitchen and floor, and the numbers behind every night (covers, average spend, labour and beverage cost).

  • Pre-shift briefings, section plans, VIP and large-table handling
  • Rotas, days off, training and the house rules for service charge and tips
  • Daily sales, labour cost against sales and a monthly P&L review with the owner
  • Reviews on Google and booking apps, regulars and private dining

What a head waiter owns

The head waiter (or captain) leads the service on the floor: the sequence at the table, the timing with the kitchen and the standards of the waiters in the section. The role is about the room during service, not about the budget.

  • Course timing with the kitchen and the bar
  • Menu, allergens and wine or drinks knowledge for the whole team
  • Coaching waiters and runners at the table
  • Passing guest complaints to the manager in time

What each costs in Dubai in 2026

Market estimates for 2026, monthly, from our role pages (housing, transport, service charge, tips or a bonus change the full package):

  • Head waiter or captain: 5,500 - 8,000 AED
  • Assistant or floor manager: 7,000 - 10,000 AED
  • Restaurant manager, casual dining or cafe: 10,000 - 15,000 AED
  • Restaurant manager, fine dining, beach club or hotel outlet: 15,000 - 25,000 AED

How to choose

Choose a restaurant manager when nobody else in the venue reads the numbers: you are the owner and want to step back from the daily floor. Choose a head waiter when you or a general manager already hold the budget and the guests need a strong service lead. In a small cafe one strong person may do both, but then test both parts.

A test for both roles

Venue names on a CV say little about what the person actually ran. Whichever role you hire, check four things before the offer.

  • Service case: a table of twelve arrives forty minutes late and the kitchen is behind on two mains
  • Numbers task (manager): a one-week sales and rota sheet with labour cost too high on two days
  • Test table (head waiter): your team plays a table of four with a difficult guest
  • References from the previous owner or manager about reviews, costs and turnover

The common mistake

Promoting the best waiter to manager, or hiring from a famous brand, without checking that the person ever owned a rota, a budget or a P&L line. Full profiles and tests: restaurant manager and waiter. For the whole team see restaurant host or hostess.

FAQ

Who earns more in Dubai, a restaurant manager or a head waiter?

A restaurant manager: about 10,000 - 25,000 AED a month in 2026 depending on the venue, against 5,500 - 8,000 AED for a head waiter or captain.

Can a head waiter become a restaurant manager?

Sometimes, if the person has also owned a rota, a budget and a weekly sales review. Test the numbers part before promoting.
