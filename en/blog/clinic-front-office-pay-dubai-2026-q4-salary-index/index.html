Clinic front office pay in Dubai 2026 what the Q4 salary index says

The Q4 2026 edition of the Dubai Salary Index covers 155 roles. For a clinic owner the front office rows are the most useful: they show what a receptionist, a patient coordinator, an insurance officer and a manager cost, level by level.

Talk to us

Why the front office matters

Patients meet the front desk before they meet the doctor. Booking, insurance approvals and follow-up calls all run through this team, so a weak front office shows up in no-shows and rejected claims long before it shows up on a salary sheet.

Receptionist and front desk

Monthly gross ranges from the Dubai Salary Index 2026, Q4 edition:

  • Receptionist or front desk: 4,500 - 7,000 AED
  • Senior receptionist with insurance experience: 7,000 - 10,000 AED
  • Front office manager: 10,000 - 15,000 AED

Patient coordinator and insurance officer

The coordinator owns the patient journey from first call to repeat visit. The insurance officer owns approvals and claims. Ranges from the index:

  • Patient coordinator: 6,000 - 10,000 AED plus bonus
  • Senior or medical tourism coordinator: 10,000 - 15,000 AED plus bonus
  • Insurance officer: 6,000 - 10,000 AED
  • Medical coder: 8,000 - 14,000 AED
  • Revenue cycle manager: 15,000 - 25,000 AED

Clinic manager

The same index puts a clinic manager at 12,000 - 15,000 AED at entry level, 18,000 - 22,000 AED when experienced and 28,000+ AED at senior level. Read the levels as a ladder: you can grow a strong front desk person into the next row instead of hiring from outside.

How to use the numbers

The index is a market estimate by TLNT.ae based on our own searches and offers since 2022 (400+ clients, 1000+ specialists hired) and on candidate expectations. Ranges do not include commission, housing, transport or visa costs unless stated. Use them to budget a hire or to check an offer, then agree the package yourself. The method and the open dataset are on our press and data page and at tlnt.ae/data; the role profiles are clinic receptionist, patient coordinator and medical insurance officer.

FAQ

How much does a clinic receptionist earn in Dubai in 2026?

About 4,500 - 7,000 AED a month at front desk level and 7,000 - 10,000 AED for a senior receptionist with insurance experience, according to the Dubai Salary Index 2026 (Q4 edition).

How much does a patient coordinator earn in Dubai?

6,000 - 10,000 AED a month plus bonus; a senior or medical tourism coordinator earns 10,000 - 15,000 AED plus bonus.

How often is the salary index updated?

Every quarter. The current edition is Q4 2026 and the next update is planned for Q1 2027.
