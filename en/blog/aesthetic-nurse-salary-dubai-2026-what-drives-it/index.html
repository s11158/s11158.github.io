Aesthetic nurse salary in Dubai in 2026 and what drives it

In the Dubai Salary Index 2026 an aesthetic nurse is listed at 9,000 - 14,000 AED a month, and a senior nurse injector at 14,000 - 20,000. The gap between the two is the real budgeting question, so here is what sits behind it.

Talk to us

The numbers

The Dubai Salary Index 2026 covers 155 roles and gives monthly gross ranges in AED. For this role it shows three lines:

  • Aesthetic nurse: 9,000 - 14,000 AED
  • Senior nurse injector: 14,000 - 20,000 AED
  • Nurse with own client base: fixed salary plus commission, no single range
  • See the role page: aesthetic nurse

Driver 1: scope of work

A nurse who prepares the patient, assists the doctor and handles aftercare sits at the lower end. A nurse who carries out injectable procedures within the scope the regulator allows, under a licensed doctor of the facility, moves to the senior line. The scope is set by the regulator and the licence, not by the clinic: confirm it before you write the offer.

Driver 2: the licence route

The nursing licence and its verification decide the start date, and a start date that slips is a cost too. Read our guide on the licence for nurses and plan the timeline before you plan the salary.

Driver 3: fixed pay or commission

For nurses with their own client base the index lists a fixed salary plus commission instead of a range. Commission works only when the nurse can see the numbers: see commission plans for clinic and salon staff.

Driver 4: the doctor above the nurse

Injector pay overlaps with other roles. The index lists a nurse injector (DHA RN) at 12,000 - 20,000 AED, while an aesthetic physician or GP injector is listed at 22,000 - 35,000 AED. See Botox injector to compare the levels before you decide which role you need.
