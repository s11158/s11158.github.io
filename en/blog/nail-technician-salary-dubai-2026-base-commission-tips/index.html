What a nail technician earns in Dubai in 2026 fixed pay, commission, tips

In the Dubai Salary Index 2026 a nail technician is listed at 3,000 - 4,000 AED a month at entry level, 4,500 - 5,500 for an experienced master and 6,500+ for a senior. The number on the offer is only part of the picture, so here is how base, commission and tips fit together.

Talk to us

The numbers

The Dubai Salary Index 2026 covers 155 roles and gives monthly ranges in AED. For a nail technician it shows three levels:

  • Entry: 3,000 - 4,000 AED
  • Experienced: 4,500 - 5,500 AED
  • Senior: 6,500+ AED
  • Role page: nail technician

Base and commission

Most masters in Dubai salons are not paid a flat salary only. Our guide on salon salaries by role describes the usual build: a base that covers living costs and a commission that rewards the work the master brings in. Where a role is paid with commission, the range is the base and the commission comes on top.

The split is the owner's decision and should be written in the contract before the first day.

Which split suits whom

A lower base with a higher commission suits an experienced master with a client following, and carries more risk for the master in a quiet month. A higher base with a lower commission suits a new master who is building a client list. A fixed minimum base for the first months helps a newcomer settle.

Tips and what they are not

Tips depend on the clients and the season. Treat them as a bonus on top of the package, not as part of the salary you promise in the offer. If the salon pools tips, write the rule down in the same contract.

What moves the number

Two nail technicians with the same title can sit apart in pay. The usual reasons are experience in Dubai or the Gulf, a personal client base, narrow skills with demand such as e-file work, extensions and nail art, and languages for your clients. Housing, transport or an allowance also change how two offers compare.
