"Agree the day off before the start date" how to keep household staff in a Dubai home

Most household staff do not leave because of the salary. They leave because the day off, the working hours and the leave were never written down, and the first disagreement happens in week two. A clear rota agreed before the start date removes most of those conflicts.

Talk to us

Why the day off is the first conflict

A family and a new employee often meet with a shared wish and no shared detail. The family expects flexibility when guests come; the employee expects a fixed free day. If nobody wrote it down, each side remembers a different conversation, and the disagreement looks personal instead of practical.

What to agree before day one

Put it on one page that both sides sign together with the contract:

  • The weekly day off and what happens if it has to move (which day replaces it)
  • Daily working hours, start and end time, and a rule for guest evenings and travel
  • Annual leave: how it is planned and how much notice each side gives
  • Live-in or live-out, and what the package includes (accommodation, food, annual ticket home for live-in roles)
  • Who gives instructions: the owner, the spouse or a house manager, so staff do not get conflicting tasks

Which contract applies

Housekeepers, nannies and drivers in a family home work on a domestic worker visa sponsored by the family, under the standard MOHRE domestic worker contract, directly or through a Tadbeer centre. The employer pays the work permit, residence visa, medical test and Emirates ID. For the exact legal entitlements, check the current rules with MOHRE; our employer guide to annual leave, sick leave and public holidays gives the general picture.

When a house manager helps

In a home with three or more staff, rotas and leave are a daily job. A house manager keeps the schedules, covers gaps when someone is away and reports to the owner, so the family does not negotiate the day off every week. For smaller homes, see the role pages for a housekeeper, a nanny and a private driver.

FAQ

What should be agreed with household staff before they start?

The weekly day off and how it moves, daily hours, annual leave and notice, live-in or live-out terms, and who gives instructions. Write it on one page and sign it together with the contract.

Which contract does a housekeeper or nanny in Dubai work under?

Under the standard MOHRE domestic worker contract on a visa sponsored by the family, directly or through a Tadbeer centre. The employer covers the visa and medical costs.
