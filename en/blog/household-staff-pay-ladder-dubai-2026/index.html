Household staff pay in Dubai in 2026 from housemaid to house manager

A villa team is not one salary line. In the Dubai Salary Index 2026 household roles run from 2,500 AED for a live-in housemaid to 25,000 AED and more for a senior butler or chef. Here is the ladder, so you can budget the team before you start the search.

Where the numbers come from

The Dubai Salary Index 2026 covers 155 roles and quotes monthly ranges in AED. The full table is open data: see the press and data page or download the files at https://tlnt.ae/data/.

The daily-care roles

These are the roles most families hire first:

  • Housemaid, live-in: 2,500 - 4,000 AED plus accommodation; experienced villa housekeeper 4,000 - 6,000 AED; head housekeeper 6,000 - 9,000 AED (housekeeper)
  • Nanny, live-in: 3,000 - 5,000 AED plus accommodation; professional English-speaking nanny 5,000 - 8,000 AED (nanny)
  • Family driver: 3,000 - 5,000 AED; experienced private driver 5,000 - 7,000 AED; VIP or executive chauffeur 7,000 - 10,000 AED (private driver)

The specialist roles

Cooking, education and service are paid on higher scales:

  • Private chef: 8,000 - 15,000 AED; senior private chef 15,000 - 25,000 AED; chef for a UHNW family or yacht 25,000+ AED (private chef)
  • Governess, live-out, one or two children: 10,000 - 15,000 AED; with a teaching background 15,000 - 22,000 AED; senior governess for a UHNW family 22,000+ AED (governess)
  • Butler in a family villa: 9,000 - 15,000 AED; senior butler 15,000 - 25,000 AED; head or travelling butler 25,000+ AED (butler)

The step that runs the team

A house manager of a villa with 3-5 staff is listed at 10,000 - 15,000 AED, and a senior house manager for several properties or a large staff at 15,000 - 20,000 AED. A head housekeeper with supervisory duties sits at 6,000 - 9,000 AED (house manager).

Once the team passes two or three people, one person who owns schedules and standards usually costs less than the friction without one.

How to use this before you open the search

FAQ

How much does a nanny earn in Dubai in 2026?

In the Dubai Salary Index 2026 a live-in nanny is listed at 3,000 - 5,000 AED a month plus accommodation, and a professional English-speaking nanny at 5,000 - 8,000 AED.

What does a private chef cost in Dubai?

The index lists a private chef at 8,000 - 15,000 AED a month and a senior private chef at 15,000 - 25,000 AED.
