Myth: a cosmetologist can do injections in a Dubai salon the licence decides, not the title

In the UAE the word cosmetologist covers two different jobs: a physician and an esthetician. Owners who hire by title alone can end up with a specialist who is not allowed to do what the menu promises.

Talk to us

Why the myth sounds true

Many cosmetologists arrive from CIS countries with a medical degree and years of injectable practice. On a CV the title looks the same as a local esthetician's, and a salon owner reasonably assumes both can do the full menu.

What the DHA standard says

The DHA Standards for Non-Surgical Cosmetic Procedures (version 2.1, effective February 2025) list who may perform each procedure. Check the current version before you build a menu.

  • Botox and fillers: licensed physicians (and certain specialist dentists) in a DHA-licensed facility
  • PRP injections and injectable mesotherapy: physicians
  • Deep chemical peels: dermatologist or plastic surgeon
  • Superficial peels, hydrafacial, microdermabrasion: physicians, nurses and trained beauty therapists
  • Laser hair removal and IPL: licensed staff in a clinic, not a salon without supervision

The two roles and what they earn

Market estimates from our cosmetologist page, in AED per month:

  • Esthetician or beauty cosmetologist: 5,000 - 9,000 plus commission
  • Aesthetic physician (DHA): 22,000 - 35,000 plus commission
  • Senior aesthetic physician: 35,000 - 50,000 and above

What goes wrong in practice

Injectables in a salon count as unlicensed medical practice, with fines and closure risk. A visiting injector does not turn a salon into a clinic, and the facility carries liability for the visitor's errors. Details are in the guide aesthetic clinic vs beauty salon.

How to hire the right one

Decide first which side of the line your business is on. A physician needs DataFlow verification, a DHA assessment and a licence activated under your clinic, so allow longer than for an esthetician: first candidates in days, hiring in 2-4 weeks, longer for a new DHA licence. FAQ

Can an esthetician do Botox in Dubai?

No. Injectables are medical procedures performed by licensed medical staff in a licensed facility.

Is a cosmetologist a doctor in Dubai?

It depends. A medical cosmetologist who does injectables must be a DHA-licensed physician; an esthetician works with non-medical skin treatments.

Can a salon do laser hair removal?

Under the current DHA standard, laser hair removal and IPL are excluded from what therapists may do in a salon without supervision.
