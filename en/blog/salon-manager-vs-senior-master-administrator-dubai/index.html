Salon manager vs senior master as administrator: who runs the salon better?

Many Dubai owners promote their best master to run the desk because it feels cheaper and safer. Sometimes it works. Often you lose a top earner and gain a part-time administrator. Here is how to compare the two options before you decide.

Talk to us

What each option really is

A salon manager owns the numbers: utilisation, rebooking, reviews, stock and the team. A senior master who covers the desk still earns from clients, so the desk gets the minutes between appointments. These are two different jobs, not two prices for one job.

What each costs in Dubai in 2026

Market estimates from our role pages, monthly in AED:

  • Salon manager: 8,000 - 10,000 entry, 12,000 - 15,000 experienced, 18,000+ senior (see salon manager)
  • Salon administrator: 3,500 - 4,000 entry, 4,500 - 5,500 experienced, 6,000+ senior (see salon administrator)
  • A senior master on the desk: you pay the administrator premium and also lose the hours that master would bill to clients

When a senior master on the desk is enough

It can work for one location, up to about 6-8 masters, with the owner present most days. In that case a strong administrator plus a clear checklist is usually cheaper than a manager. Our guide Salon manager: hire one or manage yourself lists the signs.

  • One location and a stable team
  • You review the numbers weekly yourself
  • The master wants the role and keeps a reduced client book

When a manager pays off

A manager makes sense when you open a second location, are away from the salon, or when rebooking and reviews fall and nobody owns the fix. Then the cost of a missing owner of the numbers is higher than the salary.

  • Utilisation, rebooking or reviews are falling
  • Conflicts between masters reach you every week
  • You cannot leave for two weeks and keep the numbers

The hidden cost of promoting a master

A top master often brings the salon its most loyal clients. If they move to the desk, the revenue they billed drops and the other masters now report to a colleague. Link the move to a ladder, as described in training and career ladder for salon masters, so the master has a next step that is not the desk.

A simple test to decide

Write down what you want done weekly: utilisation per master, rebooking, reviews, stock, schedule, payroll data. If more than half of that list is management and not reception, you need a manager. If it is mostly bookings, greetings and upsell, you need an administrator and a good script. Start with the administrator scripts.

FAQ

How much does a salon manager earn in Dubai?

Market estimate: roughly 8,000 - 15,000 AED a month plus a bonus, depending on salon size, languages and experience. Senior managers can be above 18,000 AED.

Can a senior master work as an administrator?

Yes, for a small salon with the owner present, but the master loses billable hours and you carry the risk of losing a top earner. Decide with numbers, not with a feeling.
