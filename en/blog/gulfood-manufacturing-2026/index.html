Are you going to Gulfood Manufacturing? 3-5 November 2026, Dubai

A food and beverage manufacturing exhibition covering ingredients, processing, packaging, supply chain and automation, with a FoodTech Summit. We will be watching it closely: here is what it means for clinics, salons, hotels and families who hire in Dubai.

Talk to us

What happens and when

A food and beverage manufacturing exhibition covering ingredients, processing, packaging, supply chain and automation, with a FoodTech Summit.

Dates: 3-5 November 2026. Venue: Dubai World Trade Centre, Dubai. Organizer: Dubai World Trade Centre. Registration and the programme are on the official site: www.gulfoodmanufacturing.com.

Why it matters for your hiring

Central kitchens, bakeries and caterers that supply hotels plan capacity here, a signal to hire production chefs and operations staff.

Roles employers usually look at around this event:

  • Sous chef
  • Restaurant manager
  • Procurement officer
  • Operations manager

Three questions to settle before you go

Write down your answers before the event, so a good conversation there turns into a clear plan after it.

  • Which outlets or menus are you planning for 2027, and who will run them?
  • Is the team ready for the peak season from December to March?
  • Which roles are hardest for you to fill: chefs, baristas, front office?

Going there, or planning the hire?

Tell us in WhatsApp if you are going and which roles you are thinking about. We work in the UAE since 2022 and help employers find and assess candidates; the employer makes the hiring decision.
