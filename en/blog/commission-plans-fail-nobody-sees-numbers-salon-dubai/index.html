"Commission plans fail when nobody sees the numbers" how to pay salon staff in Dubai

Most masters in Dubai salons earn a base salary plus commission. The percentage is rarely the reason a plan fails. It fails when the master cannot see what was counted, the rules live only in a conversation, and the first quiet month turns into a quarrel.

Talk to us

Why the percentage is not the problem

Owners often argue about 15% against 25% and miss the real issue. A master who cannot check how the number was reached assumes the worst, and a master who assumes the worst starts to look at other salons. Our guide on commission plans lists the same cause in every case: the details matter more than the percentage.

Four things a master should see every month

A plan works when the master can rebuild the payslip alone. For that, show these items:

  • Which services and which retail sales were counted
  • The rate that applied, and the threshold if the plan is tiered
  • What was deducted for consumables, and why
  • The base, the commission and the total, in one sheet

Write it into the offer

Commission promised verbally is one of the mistakes that cause turnover, together with deductions that were never explained and thresholds that are impossible in the first months. Another is different rules for different staff without a reason. The split between base and commission is the owner's decision and should be written in the contract before the first day, as described in our salon salaries guide.

  • Experienced master with own clients: lower base, higher commission
  • New master building a client list: higher base, lower commission
  • Newcomer: a fixed minimum income for the first 2-3 months while the book fills
  • Retail and rebooking bonuses: agree the rule in writing

What the market pays in base salary

Market estimates in AED per month for a full-time specialist in a private salon in Dubai. Where a role is paid with commission, the range is the base and the commission comes on top. These are orientation ranges, not a rate card.

  • Hair stylist: 4,000 - 7,500 AED
  • Senior colorist: 8,000 - 12,000 AED
  • Nail technician: 3,000 - 5,500 AED
  • Esthetician: 4,000 - 7,500 AED
  • Lash technician: 4,000 - 6,000 AED
  • Salon manager: 8,000 - 15,000 AED

Do not forget the front desk

For administrators and coordinators, pay a bonus on booked and attended clients, not on calls. For clinics, a bonus on converted treatment plans works well. A salon manager who watches the numbers weekly is the person who keeps the master-facing sheet honest.

How to test the plan before you hire

Before you make an offer, run one real month on paper. Take a typical week of bookings for a hair stylist or a nail technician and calculate the payslip with your rules. If the master would be surprised by the result, the plan is not ready. FAQ

How much commission do salon masters get in Dubai?

It varies by role and salon. Many masters work on a basic salary plus 10-30% of services, and top masters on higher commission or revenue share.

Should the commission be in the contract?

Yes. Unwritten commission promises are one of the main reasons staff leave after probation.
