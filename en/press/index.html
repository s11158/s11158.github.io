What the Dubai Salary Index 2026 is

Monthly gross salary ranges in AED for 155 roles in Dubai, three levels per role (entry, experienced, senior), 465 rows in total. Nine groups: medical and aesthetic, beauty, front office and management, household and private staff, business and office, hotels and restaurants, finance and legal, sales and marketing, tech and operations.

The index is a market estimate by TLNT.ae based on our own searches and offers since 2022 and on candidate salary expectations. Roles added in October 2026 were also cross-checked with public salary sources that are listed on the index page. It is updated quarterly; the current edition is Q4 2026.

Open data: download and reuse

The full dataset is free to download and reuse with attribution. Files are regenerated from the same tables that readers see on the index page.

Attribution line: TLNT.ae, Dubai Salary Index 2026 (Q4 2026 edition), https://tlnt.ae/en/salary-index-2026/. A link to the index page is required when the data is used online.

  • CSV in English: tlnt.ae/data/dubai-salary-index-2026-en.csv (group, role, level, range, min, max, link to the role page)
  • CSV in Russian: tlnt.ae/data/dubai-salary-index-2026-ru.csv
  • JSON with metadata: tlnt.ae/data/dubai-salary-index-2026.json

Ready-to-quote ranges (AED per month, market estimates)

  • Sous chef in a restaurant or hotel outlet: 7,500 - 11,000; restaurant manager in fine dining: 15,000 - 25,000
  • Highest range in the index: consultant plastic surgeon, up to 150,000; group medical director, up to 130,000

FAQ

Can I quote the Dubai Salary Index 2026 in an article?

Yes. Quote individual ranges with the attribution TLNT.ae, Dubai Salary Index 2026 and a link to https://tlnt.ae/en/salary-index-2026/. The full dataset is available as CSV and JSON on this page.

Where do the figures come from?

They are market estimates by TLNT.ae based on our own searches and offers in Dubai since 2022 and on candidate salary expectations; roles added in October 2026 were also checked against public salary sources listed on the index page. Ranges are monthly gross salaries in AED and exclude commission, housing and visa costs unless stated.
