Hire a butler in Dubai

Butlers for large villas, palaces and UHNW families in Dubai. A butler is the person who serves the family and its guests personally: the table, the drinks, the wardrobe, the arrivals and departures, the small rituals that make a home feel like a well-run private residence. The work is hands-on and visible, and the standard is set by the family, not by a hotel manual. We assess candidates on real service: a dinner for guests, a wardrobe and a day with the principal.

Who we help you hire

A butler who knows how the family likes things done, anticipates before being asked and keeps service calm when twenty guests arrive at once.

  • Table and drinks service: formal and family meals, laying the table to the family's standard, wine and coffee service, service for guests at dinners and receptions
  • The principal's day: morning routine, wardrobe and clothing care, packing and unpacking for trips, shoes, accessories and small repairs
  • Guests: receiving visitors, guest rooms prepared, preferences remembered, discreet coordination with the driver and the chef
  • Valuables and silver: care of silver, crystal, porcelain and art, an inventory of what is used for events, storage after use
  • Standards for the team: shows the housekeeping team how rooms, tables and trays should look, checks before the family sees it
  • Travelling with the family where agreed: setting up the residence or hotel suite abroad the way it is at home

What to check before hiring

A butler is judged by the quality of service and by discretion. A good CV from a five-star hotel is a start, not proof: private service for one family is different from serving rotating guests. The checks below are practical.

1
Household scenario walk-through: we describe a Friday evening at the villa: a family dinner for twelve, two guests arrive early, the principal asks for a suit to be ready for the morning flight. The candidate talks through the evening hour by hour: what they prepare, what they delegate, what they check last.
2
Service demonstration: laying a formal place setting, serving drinks, clearing without noise; folding and packing a suitcase for a three-day trip. We look at precision, calm and how the candidate asks about the family's preferences instead of assuming.
3
Discretion scenario: a guest asks about the family's travel plans, a supplier offers a gift, a photo of the dining room appears in a chat. We listen for what the candidate refuses and how they report it.
4
References from private households: calls to previous principals or house managers, not only hotels; how long the candidate stayed, why they left, how they worked with the rest of the staff; an agreed first-month plan with the family's standards written down.

Typical hiring mistake: hiring a butler when the home needs a house manager, or the other way round. A butler serves the family personally and sets service standards; a house manager runs the home as an operation: staff rotas, suppliers, maintenance and budgets. A housekeeper cleans and runs laundry and the kitchen. In a large villa with frequent guests, many families need both a house manager and a butler. Another common mistake: no written description of the family's standards, so a good butler from another household repeats the old house's habits.

FAQ

How much does a butler earn in Dubai?

Market estimate for 2026: about 9,000 - 15,000 AED per month for a butler in a family villa, 15,000 - 25,000 AED for a senior butler in an UHNW household and 25,000 AED and more for a head butler or a butler who travels with the family. Live-in accommodation and travel change the full package.

Butler or house manager: which one does my villa need?

If you want personal service at the table, for guests and for the principal's wardrobe and travel, you need a butler. If you want someone to run the staff, suppliers, maintenance and the budget, you need a house manager. A villa with several staff and frequent guests often has both: the house manager runs the home, the butler runs the service.
