Who we help you find

A leader who turns complaints and reviews into process changes and shows the result in numbers.

  • Metrics: NPS, CSAT, CES, response and resolution time, linking service to repeat sales
  • Customer journey: touchpoints from enquiry to repeat purchase, finding where customers drop off
  • Tools: Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk, Freshdesk, Medallia or Qualtrics, Power BI reports
  • Complaints and reviews: escalations, replies on Google and social media, handling an unhappy VIP
  • Standards and training: scripts, mystery shopping, front office and call centre training
  • Multilingual service: English, Arabic, Russian and other languages of Dubai customers

What to check before you hire

Many people talk well about customer experience; results show only in data and changed processes. The check is built on your case, and you make the call.

1
Data case: a quarter of reviews and NPS data. In two hours the candidate finds the three main drivers of dissatisfaction and proposes a 90-day plan.
2
Customer journey map: the candidate maps your customer journey and shows where the company loses money.
3
Escalation: a role-play with an unhappy VIP client and a discussion of how to change the process so it does not happen again.
4
References: calls with former managers: which metrics actually improved and why.

Typical mistake: giving a senior call centre agent the CX manager title and expecting company-wide change. Without access to data and the right to change processes, a CX manager becomes a complaints desk.

FAQ

How much does a customer experience manager earn in Dubai?

In 2026 a customer experience specialist in Dubai earns about AED 10,000 - 16,000 per month, a CX manager AED 16,000 - 28,000 and a head of CX AED 28,000 - 45,000. NPS and retention bonuses are often on top.

How is a CX manager different from an account manager?

An account manager looks after specific clients and their requests. A CX manager owns the experience of all customers: metrics, processes, standards and changes across departments. We help you work out which role you need before the search starts.
