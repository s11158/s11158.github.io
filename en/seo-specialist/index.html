Who we help you find

A specialist who brings leads, not just rankings, and can explain why traffic went up or down.

  • Technical SEO: indexing, Core Web Vitals, sitemaps, canonicals, hreflang for multiple languages, JS rendering on Tilda, WordPress or Next.js
  • Keyword research for the UAE: English, Arabic and Russian queries, buyer and tenant intent, Dubai seasonality
  • Local SEO: Google Business Profile, maps, reviews, landing pages for communities and emirates
  • Content and links: briefs for writers, E-E-A-T, PR placements in regional media instead of buying dubious links
  • Analytics: Google Search Console, GA4, Ahrefs or Semrush, Looker Studio, tying organic traffic to leads in the CRM
  • Working with developers: clear tickets, release checks so a site update does not wipe out traffic

What to check before you hire

Past reports are easy to polish. So the checks are built on your site and your competitors.

1
Mini-audit of your site: in 2-3 hours the candidate finds the 5-7 biggest issues and ranks them by impact on leads, not a 300-row crawler export
2
Competitor teardown: why a competitor outranks you on 2-3 key queries: content, links, structure, local pack
3
90-day plan: what to do in month one, which metrics will show progress and what is needed from developers and writers
4
Case studies and references: Search Console access or dated screenshots from a past project, a call with a former manager

A typical mistake: expecting growth within a month and judging the SEO by rankings instead of leads. Another one: hiring someone who only knows English search when half your clients search in Arabic or Russian. Decide upfront which languages and emirates you need visibility in.

FAQ

How much does an SEO specialist earn in Dubai?

Usually 7,000 to 12,000 AED a month at junior level, 12,000 - 20,000 AED for an experienced specialist and 20,000 - 35,000 AED for a head of SEO. We advise on the market package for your scope, and you agree the terms with the candidate.

In-house SEO or an agency?

If the site is large, content ships weekly and SEO drives sales, an in-house specialist usually pays off: they know the product and move faster with developers. An agency suits a one-off audit or a small site. We help define the profile and shortlist from our live database.
