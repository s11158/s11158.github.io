Hire an HR business partner in Dubai

HR business partners for growing SMEs, groups of companies, clinic and salon groups, retail and service businesses in Dubai. The HRBP sits next to the business leaders, not in the HR back office: they plan headcount with the managers, run performance and pay reviews, coach managers through difficult conversations, watch turnover by team and fix the reasons, and make sure HR policies serve the business. In a group of several companies, an HRBP per business line often works better than one central HR manager. We assess candidates with a stakeholder role-play and a people data case.

Who we help you hire

An HR business partner who speaks the language of the business, gives managers a straight answer and turns people problems into a plan with numbers and dates.

  • Workforce planning: headcount and cost plan with each business head, new roles, structure changes, hiring priorities
  • Performance and pay: goals, reviews, calibration, pay and bonus reviews within budget, promotion cases
  • Manager coaching: difficult conversations, underperformance plans, conflicts in teams, exits handled with respect
  • Retention and engagement: turnover by team and reason, exit interviews, onboarding that works, career paths for key people
  • Policies and employee relations: applies HR policies fairly, investigates complaints with the HR manager and legal adviser
  • People data: simple dashboards on headcount, turnover, absence and cost per team for the leadership meeting

What to check before hiring

Many candidates carry the HRBP title but have mostly done HR administration. The checks show whether the person can influence managers and use data.

1
Stakeholder role-play: the candidate meets a sales director who wants to fire a long-serving team member tomorrow and hire two juniors instead. We play the director; we watch whether the candidate asks the right questions, protects the company and still helps the manager reach a decision.
2
People data case: a table of headcount, turnover and absence by team for a year. The candidate finds the two teams that need attention, suggests likely causes and a plan for the next quarter.
3
Pay review exercise: a team's salaries, performance ratings and a budget for increases. The candidate proposes the split and explains it in a short note to the business head.
4
Structured reference calls: calls to business leaders the candidate partnered with, not only the HR director: did managers trust them, did they solve problems or add process.

How it differs from neighbouring roles. An HR manager runs the HR function: policies, hiring process, employee files, payroll inputs and the HR team. An HRBP works with specific business leaders on their people decisions and is measured by the results of those teams. A payroll specialist runs the monthly numbers; a PRO officer handles visas and government paperwork. Typical hiring mistake: hiring an HRBP into a company without basic HR administration, so the partner spends the day on contracts and leave; or hiring an administrator with an HRBP title and expecting them to challenge senior managers.

FAQ

How much does an HR business partner earn in Dubai?

Market estimate for 2026: about 15,000 - 22,000 AED per month for an HR business partner and 22,000 - 30,000 AED for a senior HRBP covering a group or business line. An HR generalist moving into the role is usually 10,000 - 15,000 AED. Allowances and a bonus change the full package.

HR business partner or HR manager: which one does my company need?

If you have no HR function yet, start with an HR manager who sets up contracts, policies, files and the hiring process. An HR business partner makes sense when HR basics work and the leaders of several teams, branches or companies need someone to help with structure, performance, pay and retention. Larger groups often have both: an HR manager running the function and HRBPs working with each business line.
