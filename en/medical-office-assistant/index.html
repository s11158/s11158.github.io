Hire a medical office assistant in Dubai

Assistants for clinic owners, medical directors and busy doctors in Dubai. This is a non-clinical administrative role: the assistant protects the doctor's time, keeps the paperwork and suppliers moving and takes the first look at patient complaints. We assess organisation and tact with patients and staff, and we check the clinic admin vocabulary the person really knows.

Who we help you hire

An assistant who knows how a clinic runs, keeps the owner's calendar and paperwork under control and brings only real decisions to the doctor.

  • Calendar and priority management for the owner or doctor: meetings, supplier visits, conferences, leave
  • Support with DHA and insurance paperwork: preparing documents, tracking renewals, following up with the people responsible (not clinical decisions and not licensing advice)
  • First response to patient escalations: complaints, delays, billing questions, with a clear route to the clinic manager or the doctor
  • Coordination of suppliers: consumables, equipment service, laboratory and courier contacts, with orders approved by the owner
  • Reporting: weekly summary of appointments, open issues, supplier costs and follow-ups; Excel or Google Sheets and the clinic software

What to check before hiring

A medical office assistant does not need a clinical licence because the role is administrative. What matters is the boundary: the assistant never gives medical advice or makes clinical decisions.

1
Email triage task: a mixed inbox of a supplier quote, an insurer query, a patient complaint and a conference invitation; the candidate sorts it and drafts two replies.
2
Calendar conflict task: the doctor's clinic list, an owner meeting and a supplier visit clash: what moves, what the patient hears, what the candidate escalates.
3
Confidentiality scenario: a patient's relative calls asking for information, or a pharma representative asks about the clinic's numbers. We check that the candidate protects patient and business information.
4
References and visa: a call to a previous clinic owner or manager; own visa, transferable or new sponsorship (the employer sponsors the work permit and residence visa).

How it differs from neighbouring roles. A clinic manager runs the clinic and its team. A patient coordinator looks after patient journeys. A medical insurance officer works on claims and approvals. A PRO handles government transactions. The medical office assistant supports one person, the owner or the doctor, with their calendar, paperwork and first-line escalations. Hire a clinic manager first if nobody runs the clinic day to day; hire this assistant when the owner or doctor is the bottleneck.

FAQ

How much does a clinic owner's assistant earn in Dubai?

Market estimate: about 8,000 - 12,000 AED per month for a medical office assistant and 12,000 - 18,000 AED for a senior assistant to a clinic owner or medical director. It varies by clinic size, experience and language skills.

Does a medical office assistant need a DHA licence?

The role is administrative and non-clinical, so it is not a licensed healthcare profession. The assistant can support DHA and insurance paperwork by preparing documents and tracking dates, but clinical and licensing decisions stay with the licensed staff and the clinic management. Check your clinic's own compliance requirements.
