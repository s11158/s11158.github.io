Who we help you find

A stylist who listens to the client and the budget, builds looks the person feels comfortable in and still sells the brand's pieces.

  • Personal styling: body type and lifestyle, wardrobes for the hot climate and Dubai business settings
  • Modest fashion and cultural context: looks for Ramadan, weddings and formal events in the UAE
  • Commerce: personal shopping in the boutique, higher basket size, a client book and repeat visits
  • Shoots: styling lookbooks, e-commerce and campaigns, working with photographers, models and production
  • Trends and brands: luxury and high street, buyer previews, regional designers
  • Tools: mood boards, Pinterest and Instagram, the boutique CRM, client languages (English, Russian, Arabic)

What to check before you hire

A stylist's beautiful Instagram does not show how they work with a real client and budget. The check is built on a concrete task, and you make the decision.

1
Client look: brief: a client is going to a business dinner and a wedding in Dubai, and the budget is limited to your boutique's range. In 2-3 hours the candidate builds two looks and explains the choices.
2
Portfolio review: the candidate shows their work and explains the client or brand objective, the constraints and the outcome.
3
Practice with a model: in one hour the candidate styles a model on the shop floor and talks to her as a client. We look at taste, speed and tact.
4
References: calls with employers or regular clients: quality of work, deadlines, client relationships.

Typical mistake: choosing a stylist on their personal style and follower count without checking whether they can work with someone else's taste and budget. Another one: not deciding whether you need a stylist for boutique sales or for shoots, as these are different skills and a different pace.

FAQ

How much does a fashion stylist earn in Dubai?

In 2026 a junior stylist or styling assistant in Dubai earns about AED 4,500 - 7,500 per month, a fashion stylist AED 7,500 - 14,000 and a senior stylist or head of personal shopping AED 14,000 - 25,000. Boutiques add commission on sales.

In-house or freelance stylist?

For shoots and one-off campaigns a freelance stylist is usually better value. With a steady flow of personal shopping clients or weekly shoots for an online store, an in-house stylist costs less and keeps the brand look consistent. We help you choose the format and find the right specialist.
