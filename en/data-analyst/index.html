Who we help you hire

A data analyst who checks the numbers before showing them, builds dashboards people actually open and answers the owner's question in two sentences, not twenty charts.

  • Data preparation: exports from CRM, POS, ERP, marketing platforms and spreadsheets joined, duplicates and errors cleaned, definitions agreed
  • SQL and Excel: queries, pivot tables and formulas that others can follow; Python is a plus for repeated work
  • Dashboards: Power BI, Tableau, Looker Studio or Excel dashboards for sales, margin, branches, marketing and stock, refreshed on a schedule
  • Analysis: why sales dropped in one branch, which products lose money, which campaigns bring paying customers, with a clear recommendation
  • Reporting rhythm: weekly and monthly reports for the owner and managers, one source of truth instead of five versions of the same number
  • Data quality: rules for how teams enter data in the CRM or POS, so next month's report is cleaner than this month's

What to check before hiring

Many candidates list every tool. Check whether the person gets the right answer from imperfect data and explains it to a non-technical owner.

1
SQL or spreadsheet data task: a small anonymised dataset with sales, returns and branches, with a few deliberate errors. The candidate cleans it, answers three business questions and notes what they fixed. SQL for a tech company, Excel or Google Sheets for an SME.
2
Dashboard review: a screenshot of a cluttered dashboard. The candidate says what they would remove, what is missing and how they would show the same story on one page.
3
Explain it to the owner: the candidate presents one finding from the task in five minutes to a non-technical person: what happened, why it matters, what to do next.
4
References and work samples: calls to managers who used the person's reports: were numbers trusted, were deadlines met; samples of dashboards or analyses with confidential data removed.

Typical hiring mistake: hiring a data analyst before the data exists. If sales are in WhatsApp chats and stock in a notebook, start with a CRM administrator or a process fix, then hire the analyst. Another mistake: hiring a data scientist to build reports that a strong data analyst with SQL and Power BI would do faster. A business analyst designs processes and requirements; a data analyst measures what happens.

FAQ

How much does a data analyst earn in Dubai?

Market estimate for 2026: about 6,000 - 10,000 AED per month for a junior data analyst, 10,000 - 16,000 AED for a data analyst and 16,000 - 25,000 AED for a senior data analyst or BI lead. Allowances, a bonus and strong SQL or Python change the full package.

What should a data analyst test include?

Use a small dataset that looks like yours, with a few errors in it. Ask three business questions, for example which branch or product lost margin and why. Look at whether the candidate finds the errors, writes clean SQL or formulas and explains the answer simply. Then ask them to present one finding to a non-technical manager in five minutes.
