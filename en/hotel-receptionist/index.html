Hire a hotel receptionist in Dubai

Hotel receptionists (front desk agents) for hotels, serviced apartments and holiday home operators in Dubai. The receptionist handles arrivals and departures, the property system, payments and deposits, room changes, upselling and the first complaint of the day, often in the middle of the night. We assess candidates with a mock check-in that goes wrong: the room is not ready, the card is declined and the guest is tired after a long flight.

Who we help you hire

A receptionist who checks in a tired family in minutes, gets every payment and handover right and still offers the upgrade with a smile.

  • Arrivals and departures: check-in with passport or ID, registration, key cards, deposits, check-out and a clear explanation of the bill
  • Property system and payments: Opera, Protel or a similar PMS, room status with housekeeping, card authorisations, cash and the shift handover report
  • Upselling: room upgrades, late check-out, breakfast and spa packages offered naturally at the desk
  • Guest requests: wake-up calls, taxis, luggage, messages, information about the hotel and the city
  • Complaints: noise, cleanliness, wrong room type; the first response at the desk and escalation to the duty manager
  • Shifts and languages: morning, evening and night shifts; English plus Russian, Arabic, Chinese or another language of the hotel's guests is a plus

What to check before hiring

A friendly smile is easy to see at an interview; accuracy on payments and calm on a night shift are not. The checks below show both.

1
Mock check-in with a problem: a member of the team plays a guest arriving late with a booking for a sea-view room that is not ready, and the card is declined for the deposit. The candidate checks the guest in, offers an honest solution and explains the next steps without blaming housekeeping.
2
System and accuracy task: a short PMS exercise or a paper version: an arrivals list with two mistakes, a group booking and a late check-out request. The candidate finds the mistakes, updates room status and writes the handover note for the next shift.
3
Upselling and phone role-play: a call asking about prices and availability, then a guest at the desk who could use an upgrade. We listen for questions about the guest's needs instead of a memorised script.
4
References: a call to the previous front office manager or duty manager about punctuality on night shifts, accuracy of cash and handover, and guest feedback.

How it differs from neighbouring roles: a front office manager runs the whole front office team and its numbers; a guest relations officer looks after VIPs, regulars and guest satisfaction beyond the desk; a concierge arranges restaurants, transport and experiences outside the building. The receptionist is the transaction point: arrivals, departures, payments and the first answer to every request. Typical hiring mistake: hiring a pleasant person without checking accuracy on payments and handover, which shows up as cash differences and lost requests after a month of night shifts.

FAQ

How much does a hotel receptionist earn in Dubai?

Market estimate for 2026: about 3,000 - 4,500 AED per month for a hotel receptionist or front desk agent, 4,500 - 6,500 AED for a senior receptionist in a five-star hotel or luxury serviced apartments, and 6,500 - 9,000 AED for a front desk supervisor. Housing, transport, duty meals or service charge can change the full package.

Hotel receptionist, guest relations officer or concierge: who does what?

The receptionist handles arrivals, departures, payments and room changes at the desk. The guest relations officer looks after VIPs and regulars and follows up on their satisfaction. The concierge arranges restaurants, cars, tickets and trips outside the hotel. Small hotels and serviced apartments often combine the roles at the desk; larger hotels hire each one separately.
