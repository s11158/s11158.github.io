FAQ

How much do doctors earn in Dubai?

From about 15,000 AED per month for an entry GP to 90,000 - 150,000 AED and more for a consultant plastic surgeon; see the table by specialty.

How much do beauty masters earn in Dubai?

Most beauty masters earn a basic of about 3,500 - 8,000 AED plus commission; senior and lead artists more.

How often is the index updated?

Every quarter.
