How to read this index

Market estimates for Dubai in 2026, based on our searches and candidate expectations. Commission, housing and transport change the full package; ask us for a range for your role.

Roles added on 25.10.2026 were cross-checked with public salary sources: ae.indeed.com, arabic.naukrigulf.com, dubai.dubizzle.com, gulftalent.com, naukrigulf.com, payscale.com, talentmate.com.

Methodology

Ranges are monthly gross salaries in AED for full-time roles in Dubai in 2026, by level (entry, experienced, senior). They are market estimates by TLNT.ae based on our own searches and offers since 2022 (400+ clients, 1000+ specialists hired) and on candidate salary expectations; roles added on 25.10.2026 were also cross-checked with the public salary sources listed above. Ranges do not include commission, housing, transport or visa costs unless stated.

Edition: Q4 2026, 130 roles. Next update: Q1 2027.

How to cite

TLNT.ae, Dubai Salary Index 2026 (Q4 2026 edition), https://tlnt.ae/en/salary-index-2026/. Journalists and researchers may quote individual ranges with this reference and a link.

How much do beauty masters earn in Dubai?

Most beauty masters earn a basic of about 3,500 - 8,000 AED plus commission; senior and lead artists more.
