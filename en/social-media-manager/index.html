Who we help you hire

A social media manager who turns your services and your team into a steady flow of content, answers every DM and can show which posts brought bookings.

  • Content plan: monthly plan by format (Reels, carousels, stories), linked to services, seasons, launches and offers the business can actually deliver
  • Production: shooting days with doctors, masters and clients who agreed in writing, simple editing on the phone, captions in English and in the audience's languages
  • Safe wording for clinics and beauty: no promises of results, no misleading before-and-after, claims checked with the medical director or the owner before publishing
  • Community: comments and DMs answered within agreed hours, booking questions passed to the administrators or telesales agent with notes
  • Reporting: reach, saves, profile visits, link clicks and bookings from social media where the CRM can track them; what to repeat and what to stop
  • Paid promotion support: boosting posts and preparing creatives with the marketing manager or an external ads specialist

What to check before hiring

A beautiful personal account does not prove the person can run a brand. The checks show planning, wording discipline and whether content leads to bookings.

1
Content plan task: a short brief on your salon or clinic: three priority services, the audience, one offer. The candidate prepares a two-week content plan with formats, hooks and captions for three posts. We look for ideas that sell the service, not only pretty pictures.
2
Wording check: five draft captions for a clinic or beauty brand, two of them with promises of results or misleading comparisons. The candidate finds the problems and rewrites them so they stay attractive and honest.
3
Portfolio and numbers review: accounts the candidate managed: what they did personally, what the results were and how they measured them. Ask for screenshots of insights, not only links.
4
DM role-play and references: the interviewer sends three DMs: a price question, a complaint, a question about side effects. The candidate replies as the brand and knows what to pass to a specialist. Then a reference call to a former manager on reliability and deadlines.

Typical hiring mistake: hiring a designer or a content creator and expecting a social media manager. A creator shoots well; a manager plans, publishes, answers and measures. How it differs from neighbours: a marketing manager owns the whole marketing budget, channels and strategy; a social media manager owns the brand's social accounts day to day; a graphic designer makes the visuals that both of them use.

FAQ

How much does a social media manager earn in Dubai?

Market estimate for 2026: about 6,000 - 10,000 AED per month for a social media executive or specialist, 10,000 - 18,000 AED for a social media manager and 18,000 - 30,000 AED for a senior social media manager or head of social. Allowances and bonuses change the full package.

Should a clinic or salon hire a social media manager in-house or use a freelancer?

A freelancer or studio works when you need a fixed number of posts and the team is rarely available for shooting. In-house makes sense when you want daily stories, fast DM replies and content shot with your doctors and masters every week. In both cases agree in writing who checks the wording before publishing, especially for medical and aesthetic services.
