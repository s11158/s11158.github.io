TLNT — thoughtful recruitment in the UAE

A small, turnkey recruitment agency for businesses in Dubai. You tell us who you're looking for — we handle the rest.

Who we are

We help owners of salons, clinics, galleries, restaurants and families fill roles without an in-house HR team. We work calmly, on a human level and turnkey — from sourcing to the employee's first day at work.

What we do

  • Source candidates and run first interviews — you only see suitable ones
  • Help formalise the employment contract correctly
  • Set salary, bonuses and KPIs
  • Draft the onboarding plan and the playbook
  • Support adaptation in the first days

Why clients come back

  • Fast — first candidates within days, hiring in ~2 weeks on average
  • Guaranteed — free replacement within 2 months
  • No 100% prepayment — 50% upfront, 50% once the employee starts
  • A live base of vetted candidates
  • We help you decide — on profile, salary and even licensing (DHA for medical roles)
