Permanent Makeup Artist Recruitment in Dubai

PMU artists for salons and aesthetic studios in Dubai. We check healed results, not fresh photos, because healed work is what brings clients back.

Who we place

An artist with natural healed results, careful hygiene and a client list that follows them.

  • Powder brows, microblading, lip blush, eyeliner
  • Healed results portfolio (photos 1-2 months after)
  • Correction and removal of old work is a plus
  • Strict hygiene and consultation with contraindications
  • English; Russian or Arabic is a plus

What to check before hiring

PMU mistakes stay on a client's face for years. We check the things that protect your salon's reputation.

1
Healed work: photos of healed results, not only fresh ones.
2
Trial: a model procedure in your salon.
3
Hygiene: needles, pigments and sterilisation protocol.
4
Health card: the occupational health card required for salon staff in Dubai.

The employer sponsors the work permit and residence visa through MOHRE.
