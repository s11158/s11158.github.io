Hire a country manager in Dubai

Country managers for foreign companies entering the UAE and for groups opening a Dubai office: software and tech, industrial and building products, consumer brands, healthcare and professional services. Often this is the first local hire, so the country manager is sales lead, office founder and the headquarters' eyes in the region at once: they win the first clients and distributors, set up partners and suppliers, hire the first team and report to headquarters in their language. Who we help you hire

A country manager who opens doors in the UAE in the first months, signs the first clients or distributors and builds a small team the headquarters can trust.

  • Market entry plan: target segments, first accounts, channel choice (direct sales, distributors, partners), pricing for the region, the first 90 days
  • Revenue: personal selling to the first key clients, partner and distributor agreements, a pipeline headquarters can follow
  • Setting up: office, bank and service providers coordinated with the company's advisers, local suppliers, events and associations
  • First team: hiring the first sales, operations or support people and setting their targets
  • Headquarters: reporting, forecasts and budgets; explaining the local market honestly, including what will not work
  • Languages and network: English, Arabic or Russian depending on headquarters and clients; a real network in the sector

What to check before hiring

For a first hire, a big title in a large company can mislead: the candidate may never have built anything from zero. The checks focus on what the person can do alone.

1
90-day market entry plan: the candidate prepares and presents a plan for your product in the UAE: segments, the first twenty target accounts or partners, channel, pricing logic, first hires and what they expect to show headquarters after 90 days. We look at realism and specifics.
2
Network check: the candidate names the types of decision-makers and partners they can reach in the first month and how; claims are tested in reference calls, not taken on trust.
3
Headquarters role-play: an interviewer playing the CEO at headquarters challenges the plan and the budget. We watch how the candidate defends the local reality and where they agree to compromise.
4
Structured reference calls: with former managers at headquarters and, where possible, with a former partner or client: what the candidate built, the results and how they reported bad news.

How it differs from neighbouring roles. A general manager runs an established business with a team and processes; a country manager of a new market often starts alone and builds both. A business development manager opens deals but does not carry the P&L, the office and the team. Typical hiring mistake: hiring a senior manager from a large corporation who has always had a brand, a team and a budget behind them, for a start-up entry with none of those. Another: no agreed targets and decision rights between the country manager and headquarters.

FAQ

How much does a country manager earn in the UAE?

Market estimate for Dubai in 2026: about 25,000 - 35,000 AED per month plus bonus for a country manager who is the first hire or runs a small office, 35,000 - 50,000 AED plus bonus for an established UAE business, and 50,000 AED and more for a regional or GCC managing director. Bonus and allowances change the full package.

Who should be the first hire when a foreign company enters Dubai?

Usually a country manager who can sell personally, choose partners or distributors and set up the basics, rather than a pure administrator or a pure salesperson. Ask candidates for a 90-day market entry plan for your product and check what they built from zero before. Admin and office support can be added once the first clients are in place.
