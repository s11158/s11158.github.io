Who we help you hire

A telesales agent who calls a new lead within minutes, books the visit without pressure and leaves a CRM note that the next person can act on.

  • New leads: calls and WhatsApp messages within minutes of the enquiry, several attempts at different times, numbers that do not answer marked correctly
  • Qualification and booking: what the person wants, budget or treatment interest, preferred time; booking a consultation, a first procedure or a viewing
  • Follow-ups: no-shows, people who asked for a price and went quiet, old leads reactivated with a reason to come back
  • Clinic and salon calls: describing services in plain words, no medical promises, questions about results passed to the doctor or specialist
  • Real estate calls: budget, area, move-in date, cash or mortgage; a hot lead handed to the agent with notes the same hour
  • Tools and languages: a CRM or call software, WhatsApp Business, scripts used as a guide; English plus Russian, Arabic or Hindi depending on the audience

What to check before hiring

A pleasant voice is not enough. The checks are about speed, listening, honest wording and the discipline to log every call.

1
Recorded call role-play: the interviewer plays a lead from an Instagram ad who asked for a price and is busy. The candidate has three minutes to qualify and book. We listen for questions, tone, no pushing and a clear next step.
2
Objection round: three short calls: "too expensive", "I will think about it", "is it safe?" for a clinic or "is the price negotiable?" for real estate. The candidate answers without inventing facts and knows when to pass the question to a specialist.
3
CRM notes task: after each call the candidate writes the CRM note and sets the follow-up date. A colleague reading it must know what was said and what to do next.
4
Reference and numbers check: a reference call to a previous team leader: calls per day, booking rate, attendance of booked visits, complaints. Ask the candidate what numbers they were measured on and how they improved them.

Typical hiring mistake: hiring for confidence on the phone and not checking honesty. An agent who promises results or discounts that do not exist books visits that end in complaints. How it differs from neighbours: a customer service agent answers incoming questions and solves problems; a telesales agent makes outgoing calls to leads and books; a sales executive meets the client and closes the sale.

FAQ

How much does a telesales agent earn in Dubai?

Market estimate for 2026: about 3,000 - 4,500 AED base per month for a telesales agent, 4,500 - 6,000 AED for an experienced one and 6,000 - 8,000 AED for a team lead. Many employers add commission per booking that attended or per sale, which changes the full package.

How do I stop losing leads from ads in my clinic or salon?

Measure how long it takes to call a new lead back and how many leads never get a call. A dedicated telesales agent with a CRM, a simple script and a rule of several call attempts usually fixes the gap faster than more ad spend. Check the bookings that actually attended, not only the number of calls.
