What to check before you hire

Beautiful dish photos in a portfolio do not show how a chef runs a busy kitchen and counts money. So the check is in the kitchen, and you lead the tasting.

1
Tasting: the candidate cooks three or four dishes for your concept from an agreed basket. You score taste, plating, technique and timing.
2
Recipe costing: the candidate costs one dish and proposes a price. We look at how they find savings without losing quality.
3
Kitchen and team conversation: how to set up stations, rotas and quality control, what to do before a Dubai Municipality audit, how to cut waste.
4
References: calls with owners and F&B directors: actual food cost, audit results, kitchen turnover, guest reviews.

Typical mistake: hiring a chef for a big name and a guide-listed restaurant without checking their experience with cost and the scale of your format. That chef may create a wonderful menu that makes no profit. Another one: not agreeing upfront who owns the recipes and menu after the chef leaves.
