Who we help you hire

A person who explains a treatment plan clearly and honestly, handles price and payment questions without pressure, follows up consistently on WhatsApp and reports numbers to the owner.

  • Experience in dental or medical sales support, patient coordination or front office with clear conversion results
  • Understanding of dental treatments at plan level (implants, veneers, orthodontics) without making clinical decisions
  • Experience with financing, instalment plans and insurance pre-approval where the clinic uses them
  • Follow-up discipline: reminders, no-show control, WhatsApp scripts and CRM notes
  • Fluent English; Arabic, Russian or Hindi is a plus depending on your patients

No clinical licence but clear boundaries

A treatment coordinator does not diagnose or treat, so a clinical licence is normally not the route. The role still has boundaries: the coordinator presents the doctor's plan and price, and never gives medical advice or changes the plan. Whether the facility requires a specific registration for this job title depends on the case, so we confirm it for your clinic.

FAQ

How much does a dental treatment coordinator earn in Dubai?

Market estimate: about 6,000 - 10,000 AED per month, and up to about 14,000 AED for a senior coordinator who runs the full consultation to payment process. Many clinics add commission on accepted and collected treatment plans, so the total depends on the model.

What does a treatment coordinator do, and is it a clinical role?

No, it is a commercial role. The coordinator meets the patient after the doctor's consultation, explains the plan, price and payment options, answers practical questions, books the next visit and follows up if the patient does not decide. Medical questions go back to the doctor.
