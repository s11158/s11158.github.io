Who we help you find

A leader who lifts lesson quality and the school's rating while keeping budget and enrolment on track.

  • Leadership experience: principal or deputy in a school with your curriculum, ideally in the UAE or the Gulf
  • Inspections: preparing for KHDA, Ofsted, BSO or CIS, NEASC, IB accreditation
  • Teaching quality: lesson observations, teacher development, using progress data
  • Team: recruiting and retaining teachers, culture, difficult staffing decisions
  • School business: budget, enrolment, fee policy, working with owners
  • UAE requirements: KHDA approval of the principal, NPQH or equivalent, safeguarding

What to check before you hire

A polished vision presentation does not show how someone will lead a school through inspection. The checks use real situations, and the owners make the decision.

1
Inspection case: the candidate receives a KHDA report rated Acceptable and within a week prepares a one-year plan: priorities, timelines, measurable outcomes.
2
Lesson observation: a joint observation of a lesson or video: the candidate gives the teacher feedback and the panel scores its accuracy and tone.
3
Budget conversation: enrolment fell 10%. What the principal does with hiring, marketing and costs, and how they talk to the owner.
4
References: confidential calls with previous board chairs and colleagues, checking inspection results during the candidate's tenure.

Typical mistake: hiring a strong educator with no experience managing budget and people, and a year later lessons are good but staff turnover is up and enrolment is down. Another one: forgetting that KHDA approves the principal and announcing the appointment too early.

FAQ

How much does a school principal earn in Dubai?

In 2026 a vice principal in Dubai earns about AED 25,000 - 40,000 per month, a school principal AED 40,000 - 65,000 and the principal of a premium school or school group AED 65,000 - 100,000. Housing and children's tuition usually come on top.

Can the principal search be confidential?

Yes. Often the current principal is still in post and the school does not want to unsettle parents and teachers. We search without a public vacancy and present candidates only to the board of owners.
