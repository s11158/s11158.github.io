Hire a chief of staff in Dubai

Chief of staff for founders, owners and managing directors in Dubai whose business has outgrown one calendar. The chief of staff is the owner's right hand on substance, not on logistics: they hold the list of priorities, run the projects that cross several teams, prepare and follow up the leadership meetings and make sure decisions actually land. We assess candidates on a real owner's week: a messy set of priorities, a stuck cross-team project and a meeting that needs a decision.

Who we help you hire

A chief of staff who turns the owner's intentions into a tracked list of decisions, owners and dates, and who can tell the owner what is not moving and why.

  • Priorities: keeps the owner's top ten in one place, prepares the weekly review, pushes back when the list grows without anything being dropped
  • Cross-team projects: a new branch, a system roll-out, a reorganisation; the chief of staff owns the plan and the follow-through, the department heads own the work
  • Meeting cadence: agendas, pre-reads, minutes with decisions and owners, follow-up before the next meeting, not after
  • Preparation of decisions: a one-page summary with options and a recommendation, numbers checked with finance, the question stated plainly
  • Communication on the owner's behalf inside the company: what was decided, by when, by whom; sensitive topics handled quietly
  • Typical background: consulting, operations, project management or a senior EA who already does this work; English for the team, Russian or Arabic for the owner where relevant

What to check before hiring

Chief of staff is not a licensed role, and the title is used loosely in Dubai: some candidates are strong EAs, some are junior consultants without a team behind them. The checks below are about judgment, follow-through and trust.

1
Priorities task: we give the candidate a founder's real-looking week: twelve competing items, two of them urgent, one that should be killed. The candidate sorts them, writes a short plan for the week and explains what they would stop. We look at the reasoning, not the format.
2
Stuck-project case: a cross-team project is three weeks late and two department heads blame each other. The candidate describes their first three conversations, what they bring to the owner and what they decide alone. This shows whether they manage through people or escalate everything.
3
Meeting and decision exercise: a page of raw notes from a leadership meeting: the candidate turns it into decisions, owners and dates and drafts the follow-up message. Clear, short, no decision lost.
4
References, background and access: calls to the previous owner or CEO, not only HR; a background check within what the law allows; a written confidentiality clause; a rule on which accounts, numbers and documents the chief of staff sees from day one and which later; a probation plan with three concrete deliverables for the first ninety days. Own visa, transferable or new sponsorship (the employer sponsors the work permit and residence visa).

How it differs from neighbouring roles. An executive assistant owns the owner's time: calendar, travel, inbox, documents. A chief of staff owns the owner's agenda: priorities, projects, decisions and their follow-through across teams, and usually does not do travel or diary. A COO owns operations with their own budget, team and targets; the chief of staff has no department and works through the authority of the owner. Hire a chief of staff when you have several teams and no time to drive the cross-team work yourself; hire an EA when the problem is your calendar and inbox; hire a COO when you need someone accountable for running the business day to day. Common hiring mistakes: hiring a chief of staff and using them as an EA, or expecting them to run departments without authority.

FAQ

How much does a chief of staff earn in Dubai?

Orientation, market estimate: about 25,000 - 40,000 AED per month total package, depending on the size of the business, the candidate's background and whether they manage anyone. A senior executive assistant moving into the role is usually 18,000 - 25,000 AED. The employer also pays visa, health insurance and gratuity provision.

Chief of staff, executive assistant or COO: which one do I need?

If your problem is time (calendar, travel, inbox), you need an executive assistant. If your problem is that decisions and cross-team projects stall because nobody drives them, you need a chief of staff: they hold priorities and follow-through but have no department of their own. If you need someone accountable for operations with a budget and targets, that is a COO. Many owners hire an EA first and a chief of staff when the business has several teams.
