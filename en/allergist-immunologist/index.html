Licensing for allergist-immunologists (DHA)

An allergist-immunologist in Dubai practises under a DHA title, usually Specialist or Consultant, within the specialty of the qualification. Paediatric allergy is a separate question from adult allergy, so we check what the doctor was trained and licensed for.

1
DataFlow (PSV): primary source verification of the medical degree, the allergy and immunology qualification, previous licences and work experience.
2
Eligibility check: the qualification, its tier and the years of licensed clinical experience are compared with the current requirements for Specialist and Consultant.
3
Assessment: an exam or interview may be required depending on the qualification and the country of training.
4
Licence and facility: the licence is activated under the employing facility; the facility confirms that testing and immunotherapy are covered by its licence and privileges.

A paediatrician with an allergy interest and an allergist-immunologist are different licensed profiles. State in the vacancy whether you need children, adults or both, and do not advertise services the doctor's title and the facility do not cover.

FAQ

How much does an allergist-immunologist earn in Dubai?

Market estimate: roughly 28,000 - 42,000 AED per month for a specialist and 42,000 - 60,000 AED for a consultant. In smaller clinics the doctor is often part-time or paid per session, so the monthly total depends on the days and the patient flow.

Can a GP or a paediatrician do the allergy testing instead?

A GP or a paediatrician can handle simple cases and first-line advice within their own licensed scope. Food and drug allergy work-up, challenge testing and immunotherapy are normally allergist territory, and each depends on the facility's privileges and equipment. If the volume is low, a visiting allergist on fixed days is a common compromise.
