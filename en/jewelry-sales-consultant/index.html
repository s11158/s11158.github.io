Who we help you find

A consultant who explains stones and certificates in plain words, keeps clients for years and handles stock worth millions with care.

  • Product knowledge: 18K, 21K and 22K gold, diamond cut and the 4Cs, GIA and IGI certificates
  • Selling: finding the occasion, presenting the piece, handling price objections and bargaining
  • Clientele: CRM, client book, invitations to private viewings, repeat sales
  • Languages: English is a must; Arabic, Russian, Hindi or Chinese widen the client flow
  • Procedures: stock control, opening and closing showcases, security, tourist VAT refunds via Planet
  • Service: resizing, repairs, engraving, old gold exchange at the daily rate

What to check before you hire

Boutique experience does not always mean the ability to sell expensive pieces. The checks show real behaviour with a client, and your manager scores the result.

1
Sales role-play: the candidate sells a ring or watch to a client buying a gift who bargains hard. We look at the questions, the presentation and how the person holds the price without pressure.
2
Product knowledge check: a short review: the difference between 18K and 22K, how to read a GIA certificate, what to say to a client who doubts a stone is genuine.
3
Clientele conversation: how the candidate keeps regular clients, how many repeat sales they made last year, how they use CRM and follow-up messages.
4
References: calls with boutique managers: target achievement, discipline with stock and cash, relations with the team.

Typical mistake: hiring a consultant for presentation and fashion experience alone, with no knowledge of stones and metals. That seller gets lost on the first certificate question and cannot defend the price. Another one: not checking whether the person has their own clientele, which drives the big sales in season.

FAQ

How much does a jewelry sales consultant earn in Dubai?

In 2026 a junior consultant earns about AED 5,000 - 8,000 per month base, an experienced consultant AED 8,000 - 14,000 and a senior consultant or boutique manager AED 14,000 - 25,000. Commission can raise income noticeably; in high-end brand boutiques it is often comparable to the base.

Do I need jewelry experience, or is luxury fashion enough?

For gold and diamonds product knowledge is critical: clients ask about purity, certificates and the gold rate. A strong fashion seller can be trained, but it takes two to three months. We help you see which candidates can sell from day one and who needs training.
