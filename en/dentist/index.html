Licensing for dentists (DHA)

Dentists need a DHA licence to practise in Dubai. Candidates from abroad pass primary source verification and, for most categories, a DHA assessment.

1
DataFlow (PSV): verification of degree, licence and experience.
2
DHA assessment: an exam for general dentists and some specialties.
3
DHA licence: issued once the file and assessment are cleared.
4
Facility linking: the licence is activated under your clinic.

Abu Dhabi uses DOH, other emirates MOHAP. A dentist already licensed in Dubai can transfer in weeks instead of months.

Besides the salary, the employer covers the work permit and residence visa, medical test, Emirates ID and mandatory health insurance. By UAE law these costs cannot be deducted from the employee's pay.
