Beauty educators and trainers for salon networks, beauty brands and academies in Dubai. We check how they teach, not only how they work.

Who we place

An educator who raises the level of your team, keeps standards the same in every branch and trains new staff fast.

  • Strong hands-on skill in hair, nails, lashes, skin or makeup
  • Training programmes, manuals and assessments
  • Brand or academy education experience
  • Teaching in English; Russian or Arabic is a plus
  • Content for social media and live demos

What to check before hiring

A great master is not always a great teacher. We check both.

1
Teaching demo: a short session with your team or a model.
2
Programme: a sample course plan with assessments.
3
Results: how trainees performed after their courses.
4
Visa status: own visa, transferable or new sponsorship.

If you run paid courses for external students, the training activity may need its own licence; check with your licensing authority. The employer sponsors the work permit and residence visa through MOHRE.
