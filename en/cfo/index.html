Hire a CFO in Dubai

CFOs for founder-led companies, trading and holding groups, family businesses and clinic or salon groups in Dubai. A CFO owns the money side of the business at board level: cash and funding, the budget and the forecast, reporting the owner and the bank can trust, controls, tax and audit in practice, and the numbers behind every big decision. Not every company needs a full-time CFO: many SMEs are better served by a part-time or fractional CFO a few days a month on top of a good accountant, and we say so honestly. Who we help you hire

A CFO who tells the owner how many months of cash the business has, what is driving the margin and which decision the numbers support.

  • Cash and funding: a rolling cash-flow forecast, bank relationships, credit lines and trade finance for trading companies, working capital (stock, receivables, supplier terms)
  • Budget and forecast: an annual budget built with department heads, a monthly variance review, a reforecast when the year changes
  • Reporting: monthly management accounts for the owner, consolidated numbers for a group of mainland and free zone companies, a board pack that fits on a few pages
  • Controls, audit and tax in practice: approval limits, segregation of duties, a clean annual audit; knows UAE VAT and corporate tax in practice and works with the external advisers
  • Decisions and deals: pricing, a new branch, an acquisition or an investor round; models with clear assumptions and a recommendation
  • Team: leads the finance manager, accountants or the outsourced bookkeeping firm; sets the month-end calendar and the standards

What to check before hiring

The CFO title is used loosely in Dubai: some candidates are strong chief accountants, some come from big groups and have never worked without a large team. The checks below show whether the person can own the numbers of your business.

1
Cash-flow model: we give the candidate a simple set of figures from an SME: sales by month, stock, receivables, supplier terms, a loan. The candidate builds a thirteen-week cash forecast, names the week cash runs short and proposes what to do. We look at the logic and the questions they ask, not at the formatting.
2
Budget variance case: a budget against actuals for a quarter with revenue below plan and costs above plan. The candidate explains in plain words what happened, what is a timing issue and what is real, and writes the three questions they would ask the department heads.
3
Board pack exercise: raw monthly numbers from a group of two or three companies: the candidate turns them into a one-page summary for the owner with the key message first.
4
Structured reference calls: calls to a previous owner, CEO or board member, not only HR: did the numbers arrive on time, were they trusted, how did the candidate handle bad news. Then a probation plan with concrete deliverables: cash forecast, month-end calendar, first board pack.

How it differs from neighbouring roles. An accountant keeps the books, reconciliations and filings in order. A finance manager runs month-end, reporting and cash for one company and manages the accountant. A CFO owns funding, strategy, the board relationship and the finance function of the whole business or group. Typical hiring mistake: a growing SME hires a full-time CFO when it still needs clean books and a finance manager; or a group with several entities and a bank facility relies on a chief accountant with a CFO title. If the business needs board-level thinking but not a full week of it, a fractional CFO two to four days a month plus a solid accountant is often the honest answer.

FAQ

How much does a CFO earn in Dubai?

Market estimate for 2026: about 35,000 - 55,000 AED per month for a CFO of an SME or family business and 55,000 - 85,000 AED for a group CFO of a holding or multi-company group. A fractional or part-time CFO for a few days a month usually works on a retainer of about 10,000 - 25,000 AED. Allowances, a bonus or equity change the full package.

Do I need a full-time CFO or a fractional CFO?

A fractional CFO is usually enough when there is one company or a small group, a good accountant keeps the books and the owner needs a cash forecast, a budget, a monthly review and help with banks or an investor. A full-time CFO makes sense when there are several entities, bank facilities or investors, a monthly board pack and a finance team to lead. Many Dubai SMEs start with a finance manager or a fractional CFO and move to a full-time CFO as the group grows.
