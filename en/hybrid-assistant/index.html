Hire a hybrid business and personal assistant in Dubai

A hybrid assistant, often called a multi-assistant, is one person who runs an owner's business calendar and inbox, personal errands, travel, family logistics and the coordination of household staff. It is the most requested assistant role among Dubai founders, doctors and professionals who live and work in the same city and want one trusted point of contact instead of three. We help you draw the boundaries first, because this role fails when the scope is undefined, and then assess candidates on a mixed day: a client meeting, a school pickup and a plumber at the villa.

Who we help you hire

One assistant who keeps the business diary, the private life and the home running from a single task list and knows which of the three gets priority today.

  • Business: calendar, inbox triage, meeting preparation, documents, supplier and client follow-ups, simple reporting
  • Personal: errands, bookings, medical appointments, gifts, car and property paperwork, renewals of visas, Emirates ID and insurance
  • Travel: flights, hotels, visas, transfers for the owner and the family, with a plan B for changes
  • Household: schedules of the nanny, driver, housekeeper and chef; suppliers and maintenance at the villa; household budget records
  • Family logistics: school calendar, activities, birthdays, guests; messages to the family in their language (Russian, English, Arabic as needed)
  • Working style: available in agreed hours and on an agreed emergency rule, a written weekly summary, a password manager and separate logins

What to check before hiring

A hybrid assistant is not a licensed role. The person will see your business, your family and your home at once, so trust, boundaries and references matter more than any certificate.

1
Mixed-day task: one day on paper: an investor call moves, a child is sick at school, a courier with documents arrives at the office, the housekeeper asks for a day off. The candidate orders the day, writes who they call first and what they decide without you.
2
Email and chat triage: a mixed inbox and a WhatsApp feed: supplier quote, school note, bank message, a friend's request, a tenant's complaint. The candidate sorts, drafts two replies and lists what needs your decision.
3
Confidentiality and money scenario: a relative asks about your travel plans, a vendor asks for a card number by phone, a household employee asks for an advance. We listen for what the candidate refuses and how politely.
4
References, background, access and probation: calls to the previous employer or family, not only HR; a background check within what the law allows; a written confidentiality clause; access rules: which accounts and cards, with what limits, and separate logins; a probation plan for the first three months with a review at the end. Own visa, transferable or new sponsorship (the employer sponsors the work permit and residence visa).

How it differs from neighbouring roles. A personal assistant covers one person's diary and errands. An executive assistant supports the business side only. A family office assistant works for the whole family and its household, often without the owner's business. A house manager runs the home and its staff but not the owner's calendar. The hybrid assistant does all of it for one owner and one household. One person is enough when the business has its own office manager or team and the household is one home with up to three or four staff. You need two people (an EA plus a house or lifestyle manager) when there are several companies, several properties or frequent travel. Boundaries to agree in writing: working hours and the emergency rule, what is business and what is private, what the assistant may pay for and up to what amount, who manages the household staff when the assistant is away. Common hiring mistakes: hiring a junior PA for the price of a PA and expecting an EA, a travel agent and a house manager in one; no written scope; no backup for leave.

FAQ

How much does a hybrid business and personal assistant cost in Dubai?

Orientation, market estimate: about 10,000 - 18,000 AED per month total package for a hybrid assistant who covers business, personal and household tasks, and 18,000 - 25,000 AED for a senior one with a large household, travel and staff coordination. The employer also pays visa, health insurance and gratuity provision. For comparison, a personal assistant is 5,000 - 10,000 AED, an executive assistant 12,000 - 20,000 AED.

Is one assistant enough for business, personal and household tasks?

Usually yes, if the business has its own office manager or team, there is one home with up to three or four household staff and travel is occasional. Two people are better when there are several companies, several properties, children in different schools or frequent travel: an executive assistant for the business and a house or lifestyle manager for the home. Whatever you choose, write the scope, hours, emergency rule and spending limits down before the start.
