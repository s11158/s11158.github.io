Content managers for brands, real estate developers, clinic and beauty groups, SaaS companies and e-commerce stores in Dubai. A content manager owns what the company says and how: the content plan, briefs to writers, designers and videographers, editing, the website and blog, product and service pages, newsletters and the tone of voice in English, Arabic and often Russian. Who we help you hire

A content manager who plans content around what clients search and ask, writes clear briefs and turns a weak draft into a page that sells.

  • Content plan: topics from client questions, search demand and the sales calendar; one plan for website, blog, email and social
  • Briefs: clear briefs for writers, designers, photographers and video teams with the goal, audience, key message and examples
  • Editing: structure, facts, tone of voice, consistency across English, Arabic and Russian versions
  • Website and SEO content: service and product pages, project pages for developers, FAQs, meta titles; work with the SEO or digital team
  • Approvals and compliance with brand rules: claims checked with the business, especially in clinics and real estate
  • Measurement: traffic, enquiries from content, time on page; what to update, merge or remove

What to check before hiring

A portfolio shows the final result after editors and designers. The tests below show what the candidate does alone.

1
Content brief task: for a real-looking topic (a new clinic service, an off-plan project, a software feature) the candidate writes a brief for a freelance writer: goal, reader, key questions to answer, structure, keywords, tone and what not to say.
2
Edit of a weak text: we give a long, vague page with mistakes and unclear claims. The candidate edits it: shorter, clearer, facts flagged for checking. We look at judgement as much as at style.
3
Content plan for one month: the candidate drafts a month of content for one product line across website, blog, email and social, with the reason behind each piece.
4
Structured reference calls: with a former marketing head: deadlines, accuracy, how they worked with designers and agencies and with feedback from the business.

How it differs from neighbouring roles. A social media manager runs social channels, posting and community day to day; a content manager owns the content across all channels, especially the website, and the quality of every text. A marketing manager owns the whole marketing plan and budget. A PR manager works with media. Typical hiring mistake: hiring a strong writer in one language and expecting them to manage writers, designers and translation in three. FAQ

How much does a content manager earn in Dubai?

Market estimate for 2026: about 6,000 - 9,000 AED per month for a content specialist or copywriter, 9,000 - 16,000 AED for a content manager and 16,000 - 25,000 AED for a head of content in a tech company, developer or large brand. Allowances change the full package.

Content manager or social media manager: which one do I need?

If you mainly need regular posts, stories and replies on Instagram and TikTok, hire a social media manager. If you need a website that answers client questions, product and service pages, a blog, newsletters and one tone of voice across channels and languages, hire a content manager. Larger teams have both, with the content manager setting the plan.
