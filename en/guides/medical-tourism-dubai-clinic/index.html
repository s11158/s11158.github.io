Medical tourism for a clinic in Dubai

Patients do fly to Dubai for treatment, including from Russia, Kazakhstan and other CIS countries. But an international patient is a different sale: they decide from a distance, compare clinics in several countries and need someone who handles the whole trip, not just the booking.

Talk to us

What international patients need from a clinic

  • A coordinator who speaks their language and answers in WhatsApp or Telegram before, during and after the trip
  • A preliminary opinion from the doctor based on photos, scans or reports, with a clear note that the final plan comes after an in-person exam
  • A written estimate with what is included and what may change
  • Help with the practical side: dates, transfer, a partner hotel near the clinic, a clear schedule of visits
  • Records and instructions to take home and a plan for follow-up after they return

Packages without overpromising

  • Package the logistics (consultation, procedure, follow-up visits, transfer, hotel) rather than the result
  • State the risks and recovery time honestly: DHA advertising rules on claims apply to international marketing too
  • Plan the length of stay around medical safety, not around a cheaper flight
  • Agree in advance what happens if the patient needs extra care after the procedure

Visas and official channels

Many nationalities can enter the UAE visa-free or with a visa on arrival; others need a visa in advance. Entry permits for medical treatment exist, and the sponsor and documents depend on the case.

  • Check the patient's entry route on ICP or GDRFA Dubai channels before confirming dates
  • Dubai Health Experience (DXH) is DHA's medical tourism platform for Dubai facilities and packages: check with DHA whether and how your clinic can participate
  • Remote follow-up with a patient abroad has its own rules: check the DHA telehealth standards before offering online consultations

The team behind it

  • Doctors who are comfortable explaining the plan to a patient remotely
  • Front desk that knows international patients and does not lose them in the general queue

FAQ

How can a Dubai clinic attract patients from Russia and the CIS?

Russian-speaking coordinators available in WhatsApp and Telegram, a preliminary doctor opinion from records, clear written estimates, help with logistics and a follow-up plan after the patient returns home.

Do medical tourists need a special visa for Dubai?

It depends on nationality. Many can enter visa-free or on arrival; others need a visa in advance, and entry permits for treatment exist. Check the current route on ICP or GDRFA Dubai channels.

What is DXH?

Dubai Health Experience, the medical tourism platform created by DHA to present Dubai facilities and treatment packages. Check with DHA how your clinic can take part.
