CRM implementation cost in Dubai what the price consists of

Quotes for the same CRM project in Dubai can differ several times over, and owners often compare a licence price with a full project price. This guide breaks the cost into its parts, explains what drives each one and lists what must be written in a quote before you sign. We do not give generic price ranges here: an honest figure depends on your processes and is set after a review.

Four parts of the cost

Every CRM project, whoever does it, is made of the same blocks. Some are paid once, some every month, and they are often paid to different companies.

  • Licences: the right to use the CRM, paid to the vendor, usually per user per month, at the vendor's official price list
  • Implementation work: process review, pipeline design, integrations, data migration and training, paid to the implementer, usually as a project
  • Paid integrations and widgets: messenger connectors, telephony, AI bots and other add-ons with their own subscriptions
  • Support after launch: answers to questions, fixes and changes, either for a set period within the project or as a separate monthly plan

What drives the implementation price

Two quotes are comparable only if they cover the same scope. These factors move the price of the work most:

  • Number of pipelines and departments: one sales pipeline is simpler to design than sales, a call centre and repeat visits together
  • Number and type of integrations: each messenger, website form, telephony line, booking system or accounting link is separate work
  • Volume and condition of data: a clean export from one system moves quickly; spreadsheets, phone contacts and old CRMs need cleaning first
  • Depth of automation: simple reminders and tasks, or multi-step bot scenarios and custom scripts
  • Users and roles: different rights for administrators, doctors or agents, managers and the owner
  • Training format: one group session, or training per role with written rules and recorded videos
  • Custom development: anything the CRM does not do out of the box, built through the API

The hidden cost of doing it yourself

Setting up a CRM yourself saves the implementation fee, and for a team of two or three with simple needs it can be the right call. The cost does not disappear, though; it moves to places that are harder to see.

A fair test: if nobody on the team can give the project several hours a week for a month, a do-it-yourself setup usually stalls halfway.

  • Owner's and manager's time: weeks of evenings in settings instead of in the business
  • Leads lost during the switch, while chats are half in the CRM and half on phones
  • Licences paid while the team still works the old way
  • A structure that cannot produce the reports you need later, so the account is rebuilt and the data is migrated twice
  • Unofficial messenger connectors chosen because they were quick, with a risk that the business number is blocked

How to compare quotes: what must be in writing

Ask every implementer for a written quote with the same structure. If an item is missing, ask for it before you compare prices.

  • Stages with deliverables: what you receive after each stage, for example a lead path document, a configured pipeline, connected channels, imported data
  • Integrations listed by name, not "integrations as needed"
  • Data migration scope: which sources, how many records, who cleans the data
  • Training: how many sessions, for which roles, whether written rules and recordings are included
  • Who owns the account: it is registered to your company, you hold the main administrator access, and you pay the licences to the vendor
  • Support period after launch, with a response time and a clear line on what counts as a change outside the scope
  • Fixed price and payment schedule by stage, plus what is not included: licences, add-on subscriptions, Meta fees, VAT

FAQ

How much does CRM implementation cost in Dubai?

It depends on the scope: the number of pipelines, channels and integrations, the volume of data and the training needed. The cost is made of monthly licences paid to the vendor, a one-off implementation project, add-on subscriptions such as messenger connectors or telephony, and support. Ask for a fixed project price with stages and deliverables.

Are CRM licences included in the implementation price?

Usually not. Licences are paid to the vendor, typically per user per month, at the vendor's official price list. A good quote lists them separately, and the account is registered to your company, so it stays with you if you change implementer.

Is it cheaper to set up amoCRM or Zoho CRM yourself?

It saves the implementation fee, and for a very small team with simple needs it can work. The cost moves to the owner's time, leads lost during the switch and often a rebuild later. If nobody can give the project several hours a week for a month, it is worth at least getting a quote for outside help.
