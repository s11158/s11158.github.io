Agency or job boards in Dubai?

Job boards are cheap and give volume. An agency costs more and gives a shortlist. The right choice depends on the role and on how much your time is worth.

Talk to us

When job boards work

  • Entry roles with many candidates: receptionists, customer service, drivers
  • You have someone to screen hundreds of CVs
  • The start date is flexible

When an agency pays off

  • Licensed or rare roles: doctors, injectors, laser specialists
  • The role must be filled fast
  • You need candidates who are not actively looking
  • You want checked references and a replacement guarantee

The hidden cost of doing it yourself

Screening, calls and interviews take tens of hours per role. A wrong hire adds visa costs and weeks of lost revenue.

FAQ

Is it cheaper to hire through job boards in Dubai?

The posting is cheaper, but screening time and the risk of a wrong hire are on you. For rare and licensed roles an agency is usually faster and safer.

Which job boards are used in the UAE?

LinkedIn, Bayt, Indeed, Dubizzle and Naukrigulf are the most common.
