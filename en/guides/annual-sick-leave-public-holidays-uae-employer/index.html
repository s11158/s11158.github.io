Annual leave, sick leave and public holidays in the UAE: employer guide

A salon or clinic owner in the UAE gets the same questions from every new employee: how many days of leave, what happens when someone is ill and what is paid on a public holiday. This guide sets out the main rules for private-sector mainland companies in plain language and what to write in the contract. Check the current text on the MOHRE website before you fix terms, because rules and executive regulations are updated.

Annual leave: how it works in practice

The employee earns leave from the first day, but the amount depends on length of service. The employer plans the dates around the business, and the cleanest approach is to agree them in writing.

  • Under one year of service but over six months: 2 days of leave for each month worked
  • From the first full year: 30 calendar days for every year of service
  • The employer sets the timing with the needs of the business in mind; give notice and record the dates
  • Unused leave is counted when the employment ends, so keep a simple leave balance for every person, see our end-of-service gratuity guide
  • For a salon or clinic, plan cover for the master's appointments before the leave starts, not on the day

Sick leave: certificate, pay and limits

Sick leave needs a medical report from a licensed health facility. The right to paid sick leave starts after the probation period.

  • Up to 90 days a year in total, not necessarily in a row
  • First 15 days on full pay, the next 30 days on half pay and the remaining days without pay
  • The employee informs the employer and sends the medical report in the way the contract and the company policy describe
  • Repeated short absences without a report should be handled as a conversation first, with the contract and the rules in hand
  • Probation and notice are a separate topic, see our guide on probation and notice period in the UAE

Public holidays and work on a day off

The government announces the official holidays, often shortly before they happen, so keep a calendar and tell the team early. Staff who are paid monthly keep their pay on a public holiday.

  • If someone works on a public holiday, the rules give a substitute day off or additional pay; confirm the current rate on the MOHRE website and record the choice
  • For clinics and salons that stay open on holidays, agree a rota in advance and mention holiday work in the contract
  • Reduced working hours in Ramadan are set by the rules for the private sector; plan the schedule of appointments accordingly
  • Keep a copy of the holiday calendar and the rota, they are the first documents asked for in a dispute

What to put in the contract and the policy

Most conflicts come from rules that exist only in the owner's head. A one-page leave policy removes them.

  • Annual leave days, how a request is made and how many days of notice are needed
  • Who approves leave and how overlapping requests are handled in a small team
  • Sick leave notification and the medical report requirement
  • Holiday work: rota, substitute day or extra pay
  • How the leave balance is tracked and paid out at the end of employment
  • A line that the contract follows the labour law and the law prevails where it gives more

FAQ

How many days of annual leave does an employee get in the UAE?

Under the federal labour law for private-sector companies the employee gets 30 calendar days of paid leave for each year of service, and 2 days for each month of service once six months are completed and the first year is not. Check the current MOHRE text and your contract, and note that free zones with their own labour rules can differ.

How long is paid sick leave in the UAE and who pays?

Sick leave is up to 90 days a year after probation and needs a medical report. The employer pays full salary for the first 15 days, half salary for the next 30 days, and the remaining days are unpaid. Confirm the current rules on the MOHRE website before applying them to a specific case.

What happens if an employee works on a public holiday?

The employee is entitled to a substitute day off or additional pay under the rules. Agree the method in the contract or the rota in advance, record the hours worked and check the current rate with MOHRE.
