Why staff quit in Dubai, and how to keep them

Many salons and clinics in Dubai rehire the same roles every few months. The reasons are usually the same, and most of them can be fixed.

Talk to us

The main reasons people leave

  • Commission promised at hiring is lower in practice
  • Not enough clients to earn the expected income
  • Split shifts and six-day weeks without a clear schedule
  • A better offer from a competitor on a similar package
  • No growth: no training, no senior title, no raise

What keeps people

  • A clear commission plan written into the offer
  • Guaranteed minimum income during the first months while the book fills
  • A fixed schedule and fair days off
  • Training on new devices and techniques
  • A visible path to senior or lead roles

Start at hiring

Turnover often starts with the hiring conversation. We discuss salary, commission, schedule and client flow honestly with candidates before the offer, so expectations match reality.

FAQ

What is normal staff turnover for a salon in Dubai?

It varies a lot. If you rehire the same role more than once a year, the reasons are usually pay structure, client flow or schedule.

How do I stop staff leaving after probation?

Write the commission plan into the offer, guarantee a minimum income while the client base builds, and give a clear schedule and training.

What if a new hire leaves early?

With TLNT a free replacement is guaranteed within 2 months. Our fee is one month's salary of the placed employee: 50% when they start and 50% after probation. No full prepayment, and a free replacement within 2 months.
