Doctor salaries in Dubai: by specialty

Benchmarks so you can plan the hiring budget upfront and not lose candidates to an off-market range.

Before searching for a doctor, an owner needs the range: too low drags out the search and causes churn; too high is overpaying every month. Here are Dubai market benchmarks.

Salary benchmarks (AED/month)

SpecialtyRange, AED/month
General Practitioner (GP)15,000 – 30,000

What drives the number

  • An active DHA licence — pricier candidates, but they start faster
  • Experience and specialisation
  • Languages and an existing patient base
  • Format: full-time, part-time, revenue share

Frequently asked questions

How much do doctors earn in Dubai?

Salaries vary widely by specialty, experience, licence and clinic type. Aesthetic medicine and niche specialists are usually paid more. Exact ranges are best confirmed per role — the market moves.

What determines a doctor's salary level?

Key factors are specialty, holding a DHA licence, experience, languages and pay model (fixed, percentage of revenue, or a mix). In aesthetics a results-based bonus scheme is common.

What besides base salary is included in compensation?

The employer typically provides visa, health insurance and certain legal guarantees. There may also be bonuses, commission, housing or transport — depending on the clinic and agreement.
