DHA licensing requirements for aesthetic doctors

Who may inject botulinum toxin and fillers in a Dubai clinic, what a GP needs on top of a DHA licence, and what to check before you hire. Based on the DHA Standards for Non-Surgical Cosmetic Procedures.

Hire a DHA-licensed aesthetic doctor

TLNT is a hiring consultancy, not a regulator. Rules change: always check the current DHA standard on dha.gov.ae before you apply or hire.

By the TLNT hiring team · Published 25 Oct 2026 · Updated 25 Oct 2026

In short: every doctor doing aesthetic procedures in Dubai needs an active DHA licence, a valid BLS certificate, malpractice insurance and privileges granted by the clinic. A GP additionally needs at least 2 years of clinical experience after graduation before aesthetic training, a recognised in-person training certificate (at least 50% hands-on) and a 1-year mentorship under a dermatologist or plastic surgeon. Professionals coming from abroad need at least 2 years of experience in non-surgical cosmetic procedures. The clinic must have at least one DHA-licensed dermatologist or plastic surgeon supervising these procedures.

Requirements by type of doctor

From the DHA Standards for Non-Surgical Cosmetic Procedures (version 2, code DHA/HRS/HPSD/ST-05, effective 22 Aug 2022).

What applies to everyone

  • An active DHA full-time or part-time professional licence, and work only within its scope and the privileges granted
  • A valid Basic Life Support (BLS) certificate and up-to-date medical malpractice insurance
  • No titles other than the one on the DHA licence: the standard names "Aesthetic Specialist", "Cosmetologist", "Anti-Aging Consultant" and similar as not allowed
  • Professionals from abroad: at least 2 years of experience performing non-surgical cosmetic procedures before working in this field in Dubai
  • Training: an in-person course from an institution recognised by an accrediting body, with at least 50% hands-on practice under a dermatologist or plastic surgeon, and a practical exam

What the clinic must have

  • At least one full-time or part-time DHA-licensed consultant or specialist dermatologist or plastic surgeon supervising non-surgical cosmetic procedures
  • The supervisor has at least 3 years of post-qualification experience in the procedure supervised and at least 150 of the same procedures performed
  • A clinical privileging committee led by the medical director that grants and removes privileges and keeps evidence of competence

Opening the facility itself is a separate process: see how to open a clinic in Dubai.

What to check before hiring an aesthetic doctor

A good CV is not enough. Before an offer, ask for documents that match the table above.

  • The DHA licence itself: title, full or part time, and status in the DHA Sheryan system; or a clear route to it (see DHA licence for doctors and licence transfer)
  • The aesthetic training certificate: in-person, recognised, with the hands-on part
  • For a GP: proof of the mentorship and a logbook of procedures observed and performed
  • Privilege letters from previous clinics, BLS, malpractice insurance
  • A practical check on a model with your supervising dermatologist or plastic surgeon

FAQ

Can a GP do Botox and fillers in Dubai?

Yes, under the DHA standard, if the GP has at least 2 years of clinical experience after graduation before training, a recognised in-person training certificate, a 1-year mentorship under a DHA-licensed dermatologist or plastic surgeon, and privileges granted by the clinic. The GP works in accordance with the supervising dermatologist or plastic surgeon.

Can a doctor call themselves an aesthetic specialist or cosmetologist?

No. The DHA standard says healthcare professionals must not use titles different from their DHA licence, such as Aesthetic Specialist, Cosmetologist or Anti-Aging Consultant.

How much aesthetic experience does a doctor from abroad need?

At least 2 years of experience performing non-surgical cosmetic procedures before working in this field in Dubai, according to the DHA Standards for Non-Surgical Cosmetic Procedures.

Who must supervise aesthetic procedures in a clinic?

At least one full-time or part-time DHA-licensed consultant or specialist dermatologist or plastic surgeon, with at least 3 years of post-qualification experience and at least 150 of the same procedures performed.

Can dentists inject Botox in Dubai?

Only DHA-licensed orthodontists, prosthodontists and maxillofacial surgeons with a recognised training certificate and privileges. General dentists may not perform Botox and filler procedures.
