Common mistakes when hiring

What owners most often overlook — and how to avoid these traps.

Most hiring problems are predictable. Here are the ones we see most often.

Five common mistakes

  • Not budgeting for visa processing time — so the employee "doesn't start on time"
  • Setting salary by guesswork: too low — a slow search and lost people; too high — overpaying
  • Not checking the licence/qualification upfront (especially for doctors — DHA)
  • Hiring a "star with connections" instead of a systematic employee
  • Hiring with no probation or onboarding plan — and losing the person in the first month
Most of these risks are removed by one simple thing: reviewing the vacancy before the search starts. That's exactly our free mini-audit.

Frequently asked questions

What's the most common hiring mistake in Dubai?

Underestimating the paperwork. Owners focus on finding a person and forget visas, MOHRE, insurance and timelines — which derails start dates and budgets. Onboarding should be planned in parallel with recruitment.

Why can't you hire someone without the right licence?

In medicine and aesthetics, working without a valid licence (e.g. DHA) is illegal and risks fines and suspension. The licence must be checked before the offer, not after the employee starts.

How do you avoid quick staff turnover?

Beyond salary, transparent terms, onboarding and motivation from the first weeks matter. TLNT helps not only find an employee but also settle them in — which lowers the risk of an early exit.
