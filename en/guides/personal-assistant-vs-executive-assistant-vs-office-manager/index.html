Personal assistant vs executive assistant vs office manager which one do you actually need in Dubai

Employers in Dubai often write assistant in the job title and then discover that three different jobs were hiding behind one word. A personal assistant runs your life and household, an executive assistant runs your working calendar and communication, and an office manager runs the place. This guide compares the three on scope, market salary estimates and the type of employer they suit, so you hire for the job you really have.

Personal assistant: when your life is the workload

A personal assistant removes friction from private life: bookings, renewals, school and doctor appointments, contractors, gifts, travel, and the household staff schedule. The skill that matters most is discretion and reliability on small things that add up.

  • Best for a family, a busy professional or a founder whose private life takes real time
  • Judge by organisation, tact and the habit of checking details, not by business writing
  • Often combined with light household coordination; clarify early whether the role includes managing a nanny, housekeeper or driver
  • See our personal assistant page for the role scope and the way we assess candidates

Executive assistant: when your calendar is the workload

An executive assistant protects the time and decisions of one senior person. The work is prioritising, communicating on their behalf and keeping their commitments moving, often in more than one language.

  • Best for an owner, managing director, clinic owner or investor with a full calendar and many stakeholders
  • Judge by judgement under pressure, writing quality, confidentiality and the ability to say no politely on your behalf
  • A strong EA often does project follow-up and board or investor preparation, which pushes the salary toward the upper range
  • See our executive assistant page for the role scope

Office manager: when the place is the workload

An office manager keeps the workplace running: suppliers, maintenance, staff administration, coordination with the PRO on visas and licences, budget tracking and the first point of contact for visitors. The focus is the team and the premises, not one person.

  • Best for a company of about 10 to 40 people, a clinic or a salon group that needs one person to hold operations together
  • Judge by process thinking, vendor negotiation and staff communication
  • Often covers basic HR and PRO coordination in a small business, see our office staff salary guide
  • See our office manager page for the role scope

Which one to choose: founder, clinic owner, family

Use the shortest honest description of where your time actually goes.

  • Founder with a growing company: start with an executive assistant if your calendar and inbox are the bottleneck; add an office manager when the team passes roughly ten people
  • Clinic owner who also treats patients: an executive assistant for your business side only works if a clinic manager or receptionist already runs the floor; otherwise the office manager or clinic manager role comes first
  • Family or private household: a personal assistant, and if the household has several staff, ask whether you also need a house manager
  • One person for everything: possible at the small end, but it usually means paying for the widest scope and accepting that one task type will be weaker. Write down the top three tasks and hire for those
  • When in doubt, run a practical skills assessment for the top tasks of the job; it shows quickly which role you are buying

FAQ

What is the difference between a personal assistant and an executive assistant?

A personal assistant works on private life and household matters for a person or family; an executive assistant works on the business calendar, inbox and communication of a senior executive or owner. The skills overlap, but discretion in private matters matters most for a PA and business judgement and writing matter most for an EA.

How much does an executive assistant earn compared with an office manager in Dubai?

Market estimate: an executive assistant about 12,000 - 20,000 AED per month (20,000 - 30,000 for a C-level or family office role) and an office manager about 8,000 - 14,000 AED. The EA is paid for trust and proximity to the decision maker, the office manager for running operations.
