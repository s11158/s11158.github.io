How to keep doctors in your clinic in Dubai

Replacing a doctor costs a clinic months: a new licence file, a gap in the schedule, patients who followed the doctor and a team that has to adjust again. Most departures have a handful of repeating reasons, and an owner can influence almost all of them. This guide lists the reasons, the pay models, and what to fix first.

Talk to us

Why doctors leave

Every clinic is different, but the same reasons come up in conversations with clinic owners and doctors. Treat the list as a checklist for your own exit talks, not as a statistic.

  • Unclear pay: the doctor does not understand how the share or the bonus is calculated, or the rules changed after the start
  • Empty schedule: the clinic promised a patient flow that marketing did not deliver, so the doctor earns less than expected
  • Overloaded schedule: back-to-back patients with no time for notes, which burns out good doctors
  • Weak support: no assistant, a reception that books the wrong slots, equipment that is often missing or broken
  • No growth: the same role and the same package after a year, with no path to senior roles, courses or conferences
  • Management style: decisions about the doctor's patients made without the doctor, or conflicts that nobody resolves

Pay models that keep people

Pay should be simple to explain in two minutes and fair in a quiet month. Whatever you choose, write the rules into the contract before the start.

  • Base plus a share of procedures: gives stability and rewards a full schedule; define whether the share is calculated on billed or on collected revenue
  • A fixed base for the first months of a new doctor, while the patient flow is built, with a clear end date
  • A monthly statement: the doctor sees every procedure, the amount and the share, so there is nothing to argue about
  • Bonuses tied to what the doctor controls, such as quality and follow-up visits, not only to the clinic's total revenue
  • A written rule for cancelled, refunded and insurance-rejected cases, since these are the usual source of disputes

Schedule and support

A doctor's satisfaction depends on the working day more than on one salary figure. Small fixes here are cheap compared with a replacement.

  • Set the length of an appointment by the type of visit and leave time for notes
  • Give each doctor a trained assistant or nurse, and a reception that knows the doctor's rules for booking
  • Check the equipment, consumables and room availability before the doctor starts, not after the first complaint
  • Agree days off and leave dates early, and keep a cover plan so that one absence does not stop the clinic
  • Use a CRM or a shared calendar so that patients, reminders and no-shows are not kept in personal messages

Licence, visa and relocation help

For a doctor who moves to Dubai, the first months are full of paperwork. A clinic that helps with it earns trust early.

  • The employer covers the work permit, residence visa, medical test, Emirates ID and mandatory health insurance for the employee
  • Help with the health authority licence file: a person in the clinic who knows the steps and the documents, or a qualified adviser
  • A clear start date that depends on the licence, so the doctor does not resign from the previous job too early
  • Support with the first weeks: housing orientation, school and bank questions for the family, a named contact in the clinic
  • Licence renewals, CME hours and the cost of professional cover discussed openly, with the clinic's share written down

Growth and a conversation every quarter

A doctor who sees a path stays longer. It does not have to be a promotion: it can be a course, a new service, a teaching role or a say in the clinic's choices.

  • Hold a short one-to-one every quarter: what works, what blocks the doctor, what the clinic can change
  • Define what a senior doctor, a head of department or a medical director means in your clinic, even if the roles are not open yet
  • Fund or share the cost of courses and conferences that bring a new service to the clinic
  • Let the doctor take part in decisions about equipment, protocols and the patient experience
  • Track why people leave and stay, and review it twice a year with the owner or the medical director

FAQ

Why do doctors leave clinics in Dubai?

The most common reasons are unclear pay, an empty or overloaded schedule, weak support staff, no growth path and management conflicts. Each of these can be reviewed with the doctors you have.

What pay model helps to keep a doctor?

A base plus a share of procedures, with a monthly statement the doctor can check and written rules for cancelled and rejected cases, is the most common model that keeps both sides clear. Agree it in the contract before the start.

How quickly can a clinic find a replacement doctor?

First candidates usually appear in one to two weeks, and hiring takes about three to six weeks. The health authority licence of a candidate who is not yet licensed in Dubai adds to the timeline.
