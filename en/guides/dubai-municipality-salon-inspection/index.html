Dubai Municipality inspections and hygiene in salons

Inspections in Dubai salons can come without warning, so the only reliable preparation is a salon that is ready every day. This is a practical checklist; the official requirements are set and updated by Dubai Municipality, so always check the current version.

Talk to us

What inspectors usually look at

  • Valid occupational health cards for every employee who works with clients
  • How tools are cleaned, disinfected and sterilised, and where clean and used tools are kept
  • Single-use items used once and disposed of properly
  • Products: labels, expiry dates, and that they are compliant for use in the UAE
  • General cleanliness: surfaces, towels, pedicure equipment, washrooms, waste
  • That the services provided match the licence activities

Sterilisation and tool hygiene

  • A clear cycle for every tool: clean, disinfect, sterilise where required, store closed
  • Separate, labelled containers for used and clean tools
  • Pedicure chairs and foot spas cleaned and disinfected between clients
  • Towels and linen washed properly and stored separately from used ones
  • Staff trained on the procedure, not only the manager

Records to keep ready

  • Copies or a list of staff health cards with expiry dates, so none lapse
  • Cleaning and sterilisation logs, filled in by shift
  • Product purchase invoices from known suppliers
  • Trade licence, and any approvals and permits for the premises
  • Waste disposal arrangements

Products and services to be careful with

Use products from suppliers who can show they are compliant for the UAE market; unlabelled or imported-in-a-suitcase products are a risk. Procedures that use medical devices or injections are regulated by the Dubai Health Authority (DHA) and are not done under a salon licence: check before adding any service that sounds medical.

How to be ready every day

  • A daily opening and closing checklist signed by the shift lead
  • A monthly internal check by the manager using the same list
  • A tracker of health card expiry dates for the whole team
  • A clear owner for hygiene: usually the salon manager
  • New staff trained on hygiene in their first days

FAQ

What does Dubai Municipality check in a beauty salon?

Usually staff occupational health cards, tool cleaning and sterilisation, single-use items, product labels and expiry dates, general cleanliness and that the services match the licence. Check the current official requirements with Dubai Municipality.

Do salon workers in Dubai need a health card?

Yes, staff who work with clients need an occupational health card issued through Dubai Municipality after the required medical screening. Track expiry dates so none lapse.

How do I prepare my salon for an inspection?

Be ready every day: a daily hygiene checklist, sterilisation logs, valid health cards, compliant products with invoices, and a manager responsible for hygiene.
