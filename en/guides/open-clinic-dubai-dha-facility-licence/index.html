How to open a clinic in Dubai the DHA facility licence step by step

A clinic in Dubai cannot see a single patient until DHA activates its facility licence, and that only happens when the premises, design, systems and licensed staff are all in place. Here is the sequence as the DHA facility licensing manual describes it, and where owners usually lose time.

Before you apply: define the clinic

DHA licenses a facility by category and by the specialties it offers. The category (for example general or specialised clinic, polyclinic, day surgery centre) drives the design rules, the staffing and the inspection checklist, so fix it first.

  • Facility category and the exact list of specialties and services
  • Mainland (trade licence from Dubai Economy and Tourism, DET) or a free zone. Dubai Healthcare City has its own regulator, not DHA
  • Premises that can meet the DHA Health Facility Guidelines and the Dubai Universal Design Code
  • Who will be the medical director: DHA will not activate a facility without one

Steps 1-2: initial approval in Sheryan and the trade licence

All DHA facility applications go through Sheryan, the DHA e-licensing system (the Invest in Dubai platform can also be used). The process can start with DHA or with the trade licence authority. The first result is an initial approval: a facility licence with inactive status.

  • Documents: trade licence details or the proposed trade name and owners, a facility proposal (type, services, specialties, clinical programme), location with Makani number, DM plot certificate and Ejari, and a floor plan
  • The floor plan must be prepared by a DHA prequalified health facility design consultant, with rooms and beds counted as per DHA guidelines
  • Under the current manual the inactive licence is valid for one year and can be renewed subject to approval. If requirements are not completed, the process is cancelled and you reapply
  • While the licence is inactive the clinic may not treat patients or advertise health services

Step 3: premises, design and fit-out

Fit-out should follow the approved layout. Changes later go through an amendment in Sheryan, so it is far cheaper to fix the design on paper than on site.

  • Civil Defence certificate for the premises
  • Medical waste management contract
  • X-ray or other radiology: registration and authorisation with FANR before use
  • Electronic medical record system connected to NABIDH, the Dubai health information exchange
  • Approved policies and procedures, the medical equipment list and a third party insurance contract, ready for inspection

Steps 4-5: licensed staff and inspection

A facility with an inactive licence can already apply for professional licences for its doctors, nurses and therapists. Those licences stay inactive and activate automatically together with the facility licence. DHA checks that there are enough licensed professionals to deliver the approved services.

  • Start doctor licensing early: DataFlow verification and the DHA assessment can take weeks or months
  • Where the opening date is fixed, prefer candidates who already hold a DHA registration or licence
  • Malpractice insurance must be in place for every licensed professional
  • At inspection DHA checks the approved layout, staffing, equipment, certificates and policies. Findings mean an amendment and a paid re-inspection

Step 6: activation and the first months

After a successful final inspection the facility licence is activated for one, two or three years, and the linked professional licences activate with it. From then on the clinic is subject to DHA inspections, needs prior DHA approval for advertising materials and must keep licensed staff on site: a facility left without licensed professionals can be temporarily closed. Fees are set by DHA and DET and change, so take them from the official service pages, not from blogs.

FAQ

How long does it take to open a clinic in Dubai?

It depends on the category, the premises and how fast the design is approved and the staff licensed. The DHA initial approval is valid for one year, which is the window to finish fit-out, staffing and inspection. The longest item is often licensing the doctors, so run it in parallel with fit-out.

Can I open a clinic in Dubai if I am not a doctor?

Ownership rules depend on the legal form and the licensing authority and can change, so confirm them with DHA and DET for your case. What does not change: the clinic must appoint a licensed medical director and have licensed professionals for every service it offers.

Can we hire doctors before the clinic licence is active?

Yes. A facility with an inactive licence can apply for professional licences, which activate together with the facility licence. Nobody may treat patients before activation.
