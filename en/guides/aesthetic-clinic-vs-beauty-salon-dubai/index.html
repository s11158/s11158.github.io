Aesthetic clinic vs beauty salon what needs a medical licence in Dubai

The line between a salon and an aesthetic clinic in Dubai is drawn by DHA, not by the service menu. Injectables and medical lasers belong in a DHA-licensed facility with licensed staff; a salon licence does not cover them.

Talk to us

The rule in one paragraph

The DHA Standards for Non-Surgical Cosmetic Procedures (version 2.1, effective February 2025) list each procedure and who may perform it. Beauty and massage therapists may perform a limited number of listed procedures in a salon or other non-clinical setting without medical supervision, except laser hair removal, IPL and microneedling. Everything else on the medical side needs a DHA-licensed facility and a licensed professional with the right privileges. Check the current version before you build the menu.

What an aesthetic clinic needs

  • A DHA facility licence that covers the services
  • At least one full-time or part-time DHA-licensed consultant or specialist dermatologist or plastic surgeon to supervise non-surgical cosmetic procedures
  • GP injectors: at least two years of clinical experience before cosmetic training, a recognised in-person training certificate and one year of mentorship under a dermatologist or plastic surgeon
  • Nurses and therapists work to a written treatment plan from the treating physician and hold privileges from the clinic's privileging committee
  • Procedures under general sedation or anaesthesia only in hospitals or day surgery centres

Risks for owners

  • Injectables or medical lasers in a salon are unlicensed medical practice, with fines and closure risk
  • A visiting injector does not make a salon a clinic; a facility hosting a visiting physician carries liability for their errors
  • Clinic advertising needs prior DHA approval; salon rules come from Dubai Municipality and DET
  • Titles matter: a beauty therapist inside a clinic needs a DHA licence under that title

FAQ

Can a beauty salon in Dubai do Botox or fillers?

No. Under the DHA standard, Botox and fillers are performed by licensed physicians (and certain specialist dentists) in a DHA-licensed facility. Nurses and beauty therapists cannot inject, and a salon licence does not authorise it.

Can a salon do laser hair removal in Dubai?

Under the current DHA standard, laser hair removal and IPL are excluded from what therapists may do in a salon without supervision. They require a DHA-licensed facility and staff with a DHA hair laser licence and training.

What does an aesthetic clinic in Dubai need beyond a licence?

At least one DHA-licensed consultant or specialist dermatologist or plastic surgeon to supervise, trained and privileged staff for each procedure, written treatment plans for nurses and therapists, and malpractice cover for every licensed professional.
