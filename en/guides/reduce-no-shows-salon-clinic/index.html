How to reduce no-shows in a salon or clinic

Every empty slot is paid for twice: the master's time and the marketing that brought the booking. Most no-shows can be prevented with a simple routine.

Talk to us

The routine that works

  • Confirmation message right after booking with time, address and map link
  • Reminder 24 hours before with a one-tap confirm or reschedule
  • Second reminder 2-3 hours before for first-time clients
  • A call from the administrator if the client has not confirmed

For long or expensive procedures

  • A small deposit at booking
  • A clear cancellation policy sent in writing
  • A waiting list to fill cancelled slots quickly

Measure it

Show-up rate = clients who came divided by clients booked. Track it by source and by administrator: some channels and some scripts bring far more no-shows.

How we help

We set up automated reminders in the CRM and WhatsApp and train administrators on confirmation calls.

FAQ

How do I reduce no-shows?

Confirm right after booking, remind 24 hours and a few hours before, call unconfirmed clients, and take deposits for long procedures.

Should a salon take deposits?

For long or expensive procedures and for first-time clients a small deposit sharply reduces no-shows.

Can reminders be automated?

Yes, through CRM and WhatsApp integration.
