DataFlow when hiring a doctor: what it is and how long

The main "invisible" stage that moves your timeline. Explained from the owner's side.

When you hire a doctor in Dubai, the DataFlow check sits between the offer and the first appointment. It directly affects when the person actually starts — so it pays to understand it upfront.

What DataFlow is

It's primary source verification (PSV) of the doctor's documents: degree, prior licences and experience are confirmed directly with the issuing bodies. Without it, no DHA licence.

How long it takes — and how it affects you

  • Usually a few weeks — depends on the country of the degree and how fast institutions reply
  • If the candidate has done DataFlow before, the stage can be shortened
  • An un-planned DataFlow is the most common reason a "doctor didn't start on time"
Owner takeaway: if the launch is urgent, prioritise candidates with an active licence (DataFlow already done). If you have time, budget weeks for the check and take a strong doctor.
