AI chatbot for WhatsApp that answers like your best administrator

A salon or clinic often loses a client in the chat, not in the treatment room: the reply comes three hours later, the price is unclear, nobody writes again. An AI chatbot can answer in seconds, day and night, in the client's language. This guide explains how to build one that behaves like a good administrator, where it must stop and pass the client to a person, and how to check its work every week.

What a good bot does and what it must never do

A good bot is not a chat that talks about everything. It is an administrator with a narrow job: answer questions about your business, help the client choose, book, remind and call a person in time. It works only from your data: your prices, services, team and rules.

The narrow job is also what the platforms expect. Under Meta's terms for the WhatsApp Business Platform, general-purpose AI assistants offered as the main product are not allowed since 15 January 2026, while a business that uses AI to serve its own clients - questions, booking, support - remains a normal use. WhatsApp's business policy also requires that automated chats give the client a quick and clear way to reach a human.

  • Answers about services, prices, duration, address, parking and hours in the client's language, in seconds and at any time
  • Asks one or two questions and suggests two or three options instead of sending the whole price list
  • Offers a free slot and books it, or creates a booking request for the administrator
  • Never invents a price, a discount or a free slot: if the answer is not in the knowledge base, it hands over
  • Never gives medical advice: no diagnosis, no judgement on contraindications, no answers about medication or dosage. These questions go to a doctor or a nurse
  • Never argues, never promises results and never pressures a client who said no

The knowledge base: what the bot needs to know

The bot is as good as the knowledge base behind it. In practice it is a structured table or document that one person on your team owns. Build it from the price list, the booking system and, most of all, from real chats of your best administrator: they show how clients actually ask and which answers lead to a booking.

Put a date next to every price. When prices or the team change, the knowledge base changes the same day, otherwise the bot will confidently quote last season's offer.

  • Services and prices: name, duration, price or range, what changes the price, what is included
  • People: masters or doctors, their specialisation, languages and working days; who is new and who is on leave
  • Rules: deposit, cancellation and late arrival, consultation before a procedure, age limits, payment methods
  • Logistics: address with a landmark, parking, branches and hours, public holidays
  • Objections with answers: too expensive, I will think about it, why is it cheaper elsewhere, can I come today
  • Tone: 15-20 real replies of your best administrator, how to address clients, message length, which language to start in
  • Red lines: topics the bot never discusses and phrases it never uses

Bot alone or bot plus human

Handover is not a failure of the bot. It is the moment where a person adds value, so write the triggers down before launch: the client asks for a person or repeats a question; any medical question; a complaint, a refund or an unhappy tone; a request outside the rules; a large order; anything the knowledge base does not cover.

The handover must be visible. The bot tells the client that a manager will reply and when, the manager gets a notification with a short summary, and nobody asks the client the same questions again. Decide who covers evenings and weekends: a bot that hands over at 23:00 to nobody is worse than no bot. On Instagram, replies are possible within 24 hours of the client's last message; Meta's human agent tag lets a person reply later, up to 7 days, but not with promotions.

Connection to CRM and booking

Without a CRM the bot answers, but the business learns nothing. With a CRM every conversation becomes a contact and a deal you can count and follow up. Both amoCRM (Kommo) and Zoho CRM can work with WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram through native or partner integrations; the choice depends on your process more than on the bot.

Collect only the data you need for the booking. A bot should not ask for medical history, Emirates ID or card details in chat. In the UAE personal data is protected by Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021, and health information has its own stricter rules, so agree with your lawyer what the bot may collect and keep, and check where your provider stores conversations. Meta's terms also restrict using WhatsApp data to train AI models, so ask your provider how it handles your chats.

  • A new chat creates or finds the contact, with the channel and the ad that brought the client
  • The bot fills in fields: service, preferred date, branch, language
  • A booking moves the deal to the next stage; a handover creates a task for a named person
  • The slot goes into your booking system, or the administrator confirms the request
  • Consent to reminders and offers is stored in a separate field, with the date and the channel
  • The bot writes first only by each channel's rules: on WhatsApp, outside the 24-hour window, only approved templates and only to clients who agreed; a Telegram bot cannot write to someone who has not started it

Test before launch, then check every week

Before the bot talks to real clients, run it through a list of 40-60 test dialogues written by your administrators. Include the ten most common price questions, the same question in English, Russian and Arabic, typos and one-word messages, a voice note, a medical question, a complaint, a demand for a discount, a request for a master who is on leave, a booking at night, a service you do not offer, and an attempt to make the bot break its rules. The bot passes when it gives no wrong price and no medical advice, and hands over in every case where it should.

After launch, read 20-30 random dialogues every week and track a few numbers. There is no universal norm for them: compare week to week and read the dialogues behind every change.

  • Share of dialogues closed by the bot without a person
  • Number of handovers and their reasons: the most common reason is the next item for the knowledge base
  • Bookings from chats, and the share of chats that ended in a booking
  • Complaints and wrong answers found in the weekly review
  • Response time of a person after a handover
  • Opt-outs and blocks after messages the bot sent first

FAQ

Can I use an AI chatbot on WhatsApp Business in 2026?

Yes, for your own clients. Since 15 January 2026 Meta's terms for the WhatsApp Business Platform do not allow general-purpose AI assistants as the main product, but a business that uses AI to answer its clients, book and support them is a normal use. The bot must give clients a clear way to reach a person, and outside the 24-hour window it can only send approved templates to clients who agreed.

Can an AI chatbot replace a salon or clinic administrator?

It can take over the repeated questions: prices, hours, address, booking and reminders, at any time of day. It should not replace a person for medical questions, complaints, discounts and non-standard requests. In practice the bot answers first, and the administrator handles fewer but harder conversations.

How much does an AI chatbot for WhatsApp cost in Dubai?

It depends on the setup work, the platform or CRM subscription, the AI usage and Meta's charges: Meta charges per delivered WhatsApp template message, while replies inside the 24-hour service window are free.
