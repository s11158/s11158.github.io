Aesthetic clinic salaries in Dubai

An owner opening or growing an aesthetic clinic needs one honest picture of what each role costs before the first offer is made. This guide puts the usual team side by side with market estimates in AED per month, explains the pay models and shows what else belongs in the payroll plan. The figures are orientation ranges, not offers.

Talk to us

The team and market estimates

Market estimate, AED per month, before housing and flights. The final figure depends on experience, licence category, languages, the clinic's positioning and the package, and every employer sets the terms.

  • Aesthetic doctor: about 15,000 - 25,000, higher for a senior doctor with an own patient base, often with a revenue share
  • Botox and filler injector (nurse or doctor level): about 12,000 - 35,000 depending on licence level and experience
  • Aesthetic nurse: about 9,000 - 20,000
  • Laser technician: about 6,000 - 18,000, from a trainee to a technician who runs several devices
  • Patient coordinator: about 6,000 - 22,000, from a front-line coordinator to a team lead
  • Clinic receptionist: about 4,500 - 15,000, higher for a bilingual senior receptionist who also handles sales
  • Clinic operations manager: about 15,000 - 50,000, depending on the number of rooms and sites

Pay models in aesthetics

Clinical roles in aesthetics are often paid with a variable part, because revenue depends on the person's own work. Whichever model you choose, write it down before the start.

  • Fixed salary: stable and simple, suits a new clinic that is still building its patient flow
  • Base plus a share of procedures: the most common mix for doctors, injectors and laser specialists
  • Share only: seen with visiting doctors and consultants; agree how cancelled and refunded sessions are counted
  • Coordinators and reception: a base plus a bonus for booked and attended appointments works if the target is clear and fair
  • Put the base, the share, the payment date and the rules for a quiet month in the contract, so the first slow month does not become a dispute

What else goes into the plan

The salary line is only part of the cost of a person. Plan the rest with the real numbers for your company category and emirate.

  • The employer covers the work permit, residence visa, medical test, Emirates ID and mandatory health insurance; by UAE law these costs cannot be deducted from the employee's pay
  • Clinical staff need a valid health authority licence for the facility, and the file takes time, so a candidate who is not yet licensed adds weeks to the start date
  • Training on your devices, products and treatment protocols, which a new specialist needs even with a strong CV
  • Consumables, device maintenance and malpractice cover, which belong to the clinic budget, not to the salary
  • Payroll through the WPS-compliant channel agreed with your bank; exact costs depend on the plan and the company

How to set an offer that holds

The aim is not the lowest number but an offer the specialist accepts and stays for.

  • Start from the role and the patient flow you can honestly promise, not from a competitor's rumour
  • Compare with the ranges above and with two or three live offers in your segment
  • Keep the base fair and put growth into the variable part, so a good month is visible to the person
  • Describe the path: what changes after six months and after a year, so the offer is not only a number
  • Check a candidate's expectations early, so a long search does not end on a gap nobody mentioned

FAQ

How much does an aesthetic doctor earn in Dubai?

Market estimate: about 15,000 - 25,000 AED per month for a base salary, and more for a senior doctor with an own patient base, often with a revenue share. The figure depends on licence, experience and the clinic.

How much does an injector or an aesthetic nurse earn in Dubai?

Market estimate: about 12,000 - 35,000 AED per month for an injector depending on licence level and experience, and about 9,000 - 20,000 AED for an aesthetic nurse.

How long does it take to hire a specialist for an aesthetic clinic?

First candidates usually appear in one to two weeks and hiring takes about three to six weeks. If a clinical candidate still needs a health authority licence, the licensing steps add to the timeline.
