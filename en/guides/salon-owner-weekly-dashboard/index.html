The salon owner's Monday dashboard

Owners who look at the same few numbers every week catch problems in days, not months. One hour on Monday with a one-page dashboard is enough for most salons.

Talk to us

The one-page dashboard

  • Revenue last week against the same week last month, split into services and retail
  • Utilisation per master: booked hours divided by available hours
  • Rebooking rate: share of clients who left with the next visit booked
  • New clients by source and returning clients
  • No-shows and late cancellations
  • Google rating and new reviews, with any negative ones highlighted
  • Payroll and commission as a share of revenue
  • Stock alerts: professional products running low, retail slow movers

How to read it

Look at trends over 4-8 weeks rather than one week. As an orientation, many salons treat utilisation around 70-85% as healthy; much lower means a marketing or schedule problem, constantly near 100% means you may need another master or higher prices.

  • Utilisation down: check marketing, free slot stories, administrator response time
  • Rebooking down: check that administrators offer the next visit every time
  • New clients down: check the source that dropped
  • Negative reviews: which master and service, fix this week
  • Payroll share rising: check schedules against bookings

The 60-minute Monday routine

  • 15 minutes: read the dashboard alone
  • 20 minutes: meeting with the manager or senior administrator on the three biggest gaps
  • 15 minutes: decide actions with an owner and a deadline for each
  • 10 minutes: check last week's actions are done
  • Monthly: add profit and loss, cash position and prepaid packages balance

Where the data comes from

The dashboard is only as good as the booking system behind it. Every visit, source, cancellation and product sale must be recorded in the CRM by the administrators. If the numbers need a day of manual work, fix the system first.

FAQ

What should a salon owner check every week?

Revenue, utilisation per master, rebooking rate, new and returning clients by source, no-shows, Google reviews, payroll share and stock alerts.

What is a good utilisation rate for a salon?

As an orientation, many salons treat around 70-85% of available hours as healthy. Much lower points to marketing or schedule issues; constantly near 100% may mean you need another master or higher prices.

How do I build a salon KPI dashboard?

Record every visit, source, cancellation and product sale in a CRM, then set up one weekly report with the same eight numbers and review it every Monday with your manager.
