How to hire staff in Dubai: an owner's guide

Who's responsible for what, the steps a hire goes through, how long it takes — and where it's easier not to do it yourself.

Hiring in the UAE isn't just "finding a person". After that comes the paperwork: visas, MOHRE, contract, insurance. As an owner it pays to see the whole picture upfront — so you don't break your timeline or budget.

The key point: the employer is the sponsor

In the UAE the business sponsors the employee: the company initiates and pays for the work permit and residence visa — not the employee. Under labour law (Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021), deducting these costs from the salary is not allowed.

What the process looks like

For a candidate from abroad the path is usually:

  • Job offer to the employee
  • MOHRE work-permit / quota approval
  • Entry (employment) permit
  • Entry to the UAE and a medical fitness test
  • Biometrics and Emirates ID
  • Residence visa
  • Labour contract registered with MOHRE (salary via the WPS)

If the candidate is already in the UAE on a visit visa, their status can often be changed without leaving the country.

Mainland or free zone

Who processes the visa differs. On the mainland it's MOHRE plus immigration (GDRFA/ICP), and the person can work across the country. In a free zone the zone itself handles it, but the employee generally works within that zone. The number of visas is usually tied to office space.

Frequently asked questions

Who pays for an employee's work visa in Dubai?

The employer is the sponsor: the company initiates and pays for the work permit and residency visa. Under UAE labour law (Federal Decree-Law No. 33/2021), these costs cannot be deducted from the employee's salary.

How long does it take to onboard an employee?

The full cycle — from offer to Emirates ID and work visa — usually takes a few weeks and depends on document readiness and government approvals. Exact timelines are best confirmed per case, as they change.

Can you hire a candidate already in the UAE?

Yes. If the candidate is already in the country on a visit visa, it's often possible to change status to a work visa without leaving — which saves time. Details depend on the current visa type and emirate.
