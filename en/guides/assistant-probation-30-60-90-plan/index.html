Assistant probation plan: 30, 60 and 90 days a template a Dubai employer can copy

Most assistant hires fail or succeed in the first three months, and the employer usually decides by feeling: the person seems fine, or something feels off. Probation works better when it is a plan: written goals for each month, a short weekly check-in, a few things you actually measure and a date on which you decide. This guide gives that plan for a personal, executive or hybrid assistant, in a form you can copy into an email on day one. Legal points are limited to what the current UAE rules state; always check the contract and the current law for your case.

Before day one: what to prepare

A probation plan starts before the person arrives. Half of the failures in month one are caused by things the employer did not prepare.

  • Access: email, calendar, shared drive, the password manager with the accounts the role needs, a company phone or number. Grant access to the person; do not let them create accounts in their own name
  • A one-page brief: the scope, the working hours and the urgent window, the three things that matter most to you, your preferences (how you travel, how you like meetings, what you never agree to)
  • Introductions: your team lead, accountant, PRO, household staff, two or three key external contacts, with a line on what each one is for
  • The written goals for 30, 60 and 90 days from the sections below, sent as an email on day one so both sides can look back at the same text
  • A fixed slot for the weekly check-in: fifteen minutes, same day each week, in the calendar already
  • The contract itself: probation period and notice written down. The brief on the role should not say one thing while the contract says another

Days 1 to 30: learn the system, no double bookings

The first month is about learning your world and not breaking anything. Judge reliability and attitude, not speed.

  • Goals: knows the calendar rules, your key contacts and your communication preferences; manages the calendar with no double bookings; handles routine bookings and reminders without prompting
  • What to measure: calendar conflicts (target: none after week two), response time on your messages during working hours, errors in names, dates and amounts
  • What to watch: does the person ask questions early or guess; do they write things down; do they come back to you with a finished item or with a problem
  • Template, goals email: In the first 30 days you will take over the calendar and routine bookings, learn my key contacts and preferences, and we will meet every Monday for 15 minutes. By day 30 I expect no calendar conflicts and that all routine reminders come from you
  • Give the first real task with a deadline in week two: a trip with a document renewal date, a supplier payment to chase, a family appointment to arrange. Watch the process, not only the result

Days 31 to 60: own the inbox and the follow-ups

The second month is about ownership. The assistant should now run parts of the role without being asked, and you should be noticing less admin on your own desk.

  • Goals: owns email triage with a daily summary of what needs you; runs travel planning end to end; keeps a follow-up list and closes items on time; for a hybrid role, coordinates household staff and vendors
  • What to measure: priority emails answered or escalated within the agreed time, follow-ups closed on time, travel arranged without errors, one weekly summary sent without a reminder
  • What to watch: judgement under pressure, the tone of messages written on your behalf, discretion with information, how the person handles a mistake
  • Template, goals email: By day 60 you will run the inbox and travel on your own, send me a daily short summary and a Friday list of open items, and I will see fewer than three admin tasks a week that reach me by mistake
  • Mid-probation conversation at day 45: a longer talk of 30 minutes. If there are serious doubts here, say so clearly and write down what must change by day 60

Days 61 to 90: anticipate, then decide

The third month shows whether the person is an assistant or a task-taker. Then you decide, on the date you set, not later.

  • Goals: prepares meeting packs and follow-up lists before being asked; proposes improvements to one process; handles a week of your absence with the agreed decision rights; for a hybrid role, runs the household calendar without your involvement
  • What to measure: items you no longer touch at all, your own hours spent on admin compared with month one, feedback from the two or three people who work with the assistant most
  • Decision: confirm, extend with specific goals, or part. Write it down and tell the person on the agreed date; silence is read as a no
  • Probation facts, cautious: under current UAE labour rules the maximum probation period is six months; an employer who ends the contract during probation must give written notice of at least 14 days; after probation the notice period is set in the contract, usually between 30 and 90 days.   • If you confirm: say what the next six months look like, including the next review and the pay step if the scope grows; our guide on why assistants leave explains why this matters
  • Template, goals email: By day 90 you will prepare my week before I ask, run one process you improved, and we will meet on [date] to confirm the role

Weekly check-ins and how to give feedback

Feedback is the part most employers skip, and the part assistants value most. Fifteen minutes a week saves a difficult conversation at day 90.

  • Format: the same three questions each week. What went well, what went wrong and why, what do you need from me
  • Specific, not general: not you need to be more organised, but the Thursday supplier meeting was not in the calendar; let us agree how new meetings get in
  • Timely and private: the same day or the next, one to one. Praise can be public; corrections are never public
  • Both directions: ask what you do that slows the assistant down. Late answers, reversed decisions and unclear priorities are usually at the top of that list
  • Write the notes in one shared document; at day 90 you will have twelve short entries instead of a feeling
  • Pay attention to patterns, not single events: one missed detail in month one is normal; the same type of error in weeks three, six and nine is a decision

FAQ

How long should an assistant's probation be in the UAE?

Under current UAE labour rules probation cannot exceed six months; the exact period is set in the contract. For an assistant, three months with written goals at 30, 60 and 90 days is usually enough to decide; you can set a longer period in the contract within the legal maximum if the role is senior or the access is high. Check the contract and the current law for your case.

What should I measure during an assistant's probation?

A few observable things: calendar conflicts, response time on priority messages, follow-ups closed on time, travel arranged without errors, the number of admin tasks that still reach you by mistake, and feedback from the people who work with the assistant most. Review them in a fifteen-minute weekly check-in with written notes.
