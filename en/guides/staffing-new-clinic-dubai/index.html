Staffing a new clinic or salon in Dubai

Opening day is fixed, the fit-out is running, and you still need 5 to 15 people. The order in which you hire matters more than the speed.

Talk to us

Typical opening team

  • Aesthetic clinic: medical director, 1-2 doctors or injectors, nurse, laser specialist, 2 receptionists, clinic manager
  • Salon: salon manager, administrator, 2-4 nail technicians, 2 hair stylists, esthetician, lash artist
  • Both: someone for marketing and content from day one

Order of hiring

Hire the licensed and hard-to-find roles first, then the roles that train fast.

  • 3-4 months before opening: medical director and doctors (DHA licensing takes time)
  • 2-3 months: clinic or salon manager, who will help hire the rest
  • 1-2 months: nurses, laser and beauty specialists
  • 2-4 weeks: receptionists and administrators

Budget lines to plan

  • Salaries for the first months before full revenue
  • Visa, Emirates ID, medical test and insurance for each employee
  • DHA licensing costs for medical staff
  • Recruitment fees

How we help

We plan the team with you, hire in the right order and keep one point of contact for the whole opening team, with the same 50/50 terms and guarantee for every hire.

FAQ

When should I start hiring for a new clinic?

Doctors and other licensed staff 3-4 months before opening, because DHA licensing and licence transfer take time. Non-licensed staff 2-6 weeks before.

Can you hire the whole team?

Yes, from one role to the full opening team. Our fee is one month's salary of the placed employee: 50% when they start and 50% after probation. No full prepayment, and a free replacement within 2 months.

Who should I hire first?

The medical director or salon manager, then the hardest licensed roles.
