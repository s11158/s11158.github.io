DataFlow verification documents, delays and how to prepare

Primary source verification is the step where most licence timelines slip. DataFlow contacts every university, hospital and regulator in your file directly, and the slowest one sets the pace.

Talk to us

What is verified

DHA delegates primary source verification (PSV) to a third-party verification agency, DataFlow Group. Documents are confirmed directly with the body that issued them. A positive PSV result is required before a DHA registration is issued.

  • Educational qualifications: medical or nursing degree, specialty certificates
  • Internship and training certificates
  • Experience certificates from each employer
  • Professional licences and registrations
  • Any other document the authority asks for

Documents to prepare

  • Passport copy with the exact spelling you will use everywhere
  • Diplomas and, where required, academic transcripts
  • Experience letters on letterhead for each employer: exact dates, position, department, full-time status, contact for verification
  • Valid licence or registration from your home country and country of last employment
  • Good standing certificate not older than six months, from the regulator or, where none exists, officially from the last employer
  • Certified legal translation of any document not in English or Arabic
  • The authorisation for DataFlow to contact the issuers, signed as the system requires

Typical causes of delay

  • The issuer does not reply or asks for its own fee or form
  • The university or hospital has been renamed, merged or closed
  • Names spelled differently across passport, diploma and letters
  • Experience letters without exact dates or signed by someone who cannot confirm them
  • Wrong or general contact details for the issuer

Results and what to do

  • Positive: you continue to the assessment and registration
  • Unable to Verify (UTV): the check could not be completed. Under the DHA manual you may request re-verification of the same document twice
  • Discrepancy: the data does not match. Re-verification of the same document is allowed once
  • DHA accepts PSV reports transferred from DOH, SHA and MOHAP, but may ask for more verification

FAQ

How long does DataFlow take for a DHA licence?

Often several weeks, and longer if an issuer is slow to reply. There is no guaranteed deadline, so start as early as possible and warn old employers and your university that a request will come.

What does Unable to Verify mean in DataFlow?

The verification could not be completed, for example because the issuer did not respond or documents were missing. Under the DHA manual you can request re-verification of that document twice; fix the cause first.

Do I need a new DataFlow if I move from Abu Dhabi to Dubai?

Not necessarily. DHA accepts PSV reports transferred from DOH, SHA and MOHAP, but it can ask for additional verification, for example for newer experience.
