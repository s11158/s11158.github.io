How to price services in a Dubai beauty salon

Copying the price list of the salon next door is the fastest way to work hard for no profit. A price has to cover the master, the materials and the hour of the salon, and only then match the market.

Talk to us

Start from cost per hour

Divide monthly fixed costs by booked hours to get the overhead cost of one booked hour. In the illustrative example from our unit economics guide, 110,000 AED of fixed costs over 1,040 booked hours is about 106 AED per hour. Every service must cover that hour plus commission and materials.

  • Materials per service: count them once, properly, for each service
  • Commission or the master's cost per hour
  • Overhead per booked hour, which falls as utilisation grows
  • Target margin on top

Check the market, not the cheapest competitor

  • Compare with salons of the same positioning in the same area: mall, community, street
  • Look at the full offer: duration, brands used, level of master, ambience
  • Price below the market only if you have a clear reason, such as a new location that needs to fill

Positioning and price levels by master

Clients in Dubai accept different prices for different levels of master. A junior, senior and top price for the same service lets you keep new masters busy and pay strong ones well without discounting the brand.

Packages, memberships and promotions

  • Packages and memberships bring cash forward but are a liability: services you still owe
  • Promotions work best on off-peak hours and first visits, not on the busiest days
  • Gift cards: track them in the system, not in a notebook
  • Bundles such as manicure plus pedicure raise the ticket per visit

How commission and discounts change the margin

If commission is paid on the list price, every discount comes only out of the salon's share. Example with illustrative numbers: service 200 AED, commission 30% (60), materials 20, the salon keeps 120 before overhead. A 20% discount on the price with commission still on the list price leaves the salon 80, a third less. Write in the contract whether commission is calculated after discounts and net of VAT. If you are VAT registered, consumer prices are normally shown including VAT: check the rules with your accountant.

FAQ

How do I set prices in my salon in Dubai?

Calculate the cost of each service (materials, commission, overhead per booked hour), add a target margin, then compare with salons of the same positioning in your area. Use price levels by master rather than one price for everyone.

Should commission be paid before or after discounts?

Decide it and write it in the contract. If commission is paid on the list price, discounts come only out of the salon's share, which can wipe out the margin.

Are packages and memberships good for a salon?

They bring cash and loyalty, but they are services you still owe. Track them in the booking system and plan capacity to serve them.
