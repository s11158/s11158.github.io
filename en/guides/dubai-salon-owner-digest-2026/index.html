Salon owner digest 2026 salaries, pricing, staffing and demand in Dubai

This digest is built only on our own recruitment data and what we see in salons we hire for. No market-size claims and no surveys: salary ranges are our market estimates, the rest is practical observation.

Salaries in beauty roles: our 2026 market estimate

Monthly AED, market estimate from our role pages and the Dubai Salary Index 2026. Most beauty roles are paid a base plus commission, so the base alone understates what a strong master earns.

  • Nail technician: entry 3,000 - 4,000, experienced 4,500 - 5,500, senior 6,500+
  • Hair stylist: entry 4,000 - 5,500, experienced 6,000 - 7,500, senior 9,000+
  • Hair colorist: 5,000 - 8,000 + commission, senior 8,000 - 12,000 + commission, color director or educator 12,000 - 18,000
  • Lash and brow technician: 4,000 - 6,000 + commission, senior 6,000 - 9,000 + commission, lead artist or trainer 9,000 - 12,000
  • Makeup artist: 4,000 - 7,000 + commission, senior or bridal 8,000 - 12,000 + commission, brand or lead artist 12,000 - 18,000
  • PMU artist: 5,000 - 8,000 + commission, senior 8,000 - 12,000 + commission, top artist or trainer on a revenue share of 30 - 50%
  • Barber: 3,500 - 6,000 + commission, senior 6,000 - 9,000 + commission, head barber or educator 9,000 - 12,000
  • Esthetician: entry 4,000 - 5,500, experienced 6,000 - 7,500, senior 9,000+
  • Massage therapist: entry 3,500 - 4,500, experienced 5,000 - 6,000, senior 7,000+
  • Spa therapist: 3,000 - 5,000 + commission, senior 5,000 - 8,000 + commission, spa supervisor or manager 10,000 - 20,000
  • Salon administrator: entry 3,500 - 4,000, experienced 4,500 - 5,500, senior 6,000+
  • Salon manager: entry 8,000 - 10,000, experienced 12,000 - 15,000, senior 18,000+

What a hire really costs on top of salary

The salary line is only part of the cost of a person. Budget these items for every employee, the exact amounts depend on the visa route, the insurer and the contract.

  • Work permit, residence visa, medical test and Emirates ID
  • Health insurance, which employers in Dubai must provide
  • Occupational health card for staff who work with clients
  • End of service gratuity accrual: under UAE labour law, 21 days of basic salary per year for the first five years
  • Paid annual leave and, if the contract says so, a ticket home
  • Accommodation or transport, which some salons provide for entry-level staff
  • Recruitment fee or the time and ads spent on hiring yourself

Pricing logic we see in salons that make money

  • Prices follow the master level: junior, senior and top masters have different price lists for the same service
  • Commission is calculated on a clear base (price after discount, or net of VAT and materials), written into the contract
  • Discounts are rare and targeted: first visit, off-peak hours, packages, not a permanent sale
  • The price list is reviewed at least once a year against real cost per hour, not only against competitors

Staffing patterns

Our role pages show first candidates within days for most beauty roles and hiring in about 1-3 weeks; a salon manager usually takes 2-3 weeks. What slows owners down is not the market, it is unclear commission terms and a slow decision.

  • Hire the salon manager first when opening, then masters, then administrators
  • Strong masters move for commission and a full schedule, not for a slightly higher base
  • Check who owns the client relationship: the salon, or the master's personal Instagram
  • Administrators decide how many enquiries turn into bookings, so they are a revenue role, not a cost

Demand through the year: practical observations

These are patterns we and salon owners see in Dubai, not measured statistics. Check them against your own booking data.

  • Cooler months, roughly October to April, are the busy season with events, weddings and tourists
  • July and August are usually slower as many residents travel; good time for staff leave and training
  • During Ramadan demand shifts to the evening, with a peak in the days before Eid
  • Before public holidays and big events, hair, makeup and nails book out first

FAQ

How much do salon staff earn in Dubai in 2026?

By our market estimate: nail technicians 3,000 - 6,500+ AED a month, hair stylists 4,000 - 9,000+, estheticians 4,000 - 9,000+, salon administrators 3,500 - 6,000+ and salon managers 8,000 - 18,000+. Many roles add commission on top of the base.

What does an employee cost a Dubai salon beyond salary?

Visa and Emirates ID, mandatory health insurance, an occupational health card, end of service gratuity accrual, annual leave, sometimes accommodation or transport, and the cost of hiring.

When is the busy season for salons in Dubai?

Usually the cooler months from about October to April, plus peaks before Eid and big events. July and August tend to be slower. Check this against your own booking data.
