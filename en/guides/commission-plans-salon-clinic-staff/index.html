Commission plans for salon and clinic staff

Pay structure decides who you can hire and who stays. In Dubai most beauty and aesthetic staff work on basic plus commission, and the details matter more than the percentage.

Talk to us

Common models

  • Basic salary plus a percentage of the services they perform
  • Commission only, typical for top masters and real estate
  • Tiered commission: the percentage grows after a monthly revenue threshold
  • Guaranteed minimum income for the first 2-3 months while the book fills
  • Separate bonuses for retail sales and rebooking

Mistakes that cause turnover

  • Commission promised verbally but not written in the offer
  • Deductions for consumables that were not explained
  • Thresholds that are impossible in the first months
  • Different rules for different staff without a reason

Administrators and coordinators

Pay a bonus on booked and attended clients, not on calls. For clinics, a bonus on converted treatment plans works well.

FAQ

How much commission do salon masters get in Dubai?

It varies by role and salon; many masters work on a basic salary plus 10-30% of services, and top masters on higher commission or revenue share.

Should the commission be in the contract?

Yes. Unwritten commission promises are one of the main reasons staff leave after probation.

Can you help design a pay plan?

Yes, as part of the search or a team audit.
