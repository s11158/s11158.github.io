One assistant or several business, personal and household: how to split the load in Dubai

An owner in Dubai with a business, a family and one or two properties usually asks the same question: can one person run all of it, or do I need an executive assistant, a house manager and a driver? Both answers can be right. This guide shows how to judge the load, where one hybrid multi-assistant works and where it breaks, how confidentiality changes with each option, what each set-up costs as a market estimate and how to split the work if you go for two or three people.

What the question really is

It is not about titles. It is about three things: how many hours of work there are, how many of them need a physical presence in two places at once, and how much private information one person should hold.

  • Hours: write down the business tasks, the personal tasks and the household tasks separately and estimate hours per week for each list; if the total is above one working week, one person will not cope whatever the title
  • Places: a person cannot be at the office for a board meeting and at the villa for the contractor at the same time; count how often that happens
  • Information: one hybrid person sees your business finances, your family's schedule and your home; decide whether that concentration is acceptable to you
  • Travel: if you travel often, who stays with the house and who comes with you; a hybrid person cannot do both
  • Growth: a set-up that works today may not in a year; choose the option that can add a second person without redesigning everything

One hybrid multi-assistant: when it works

A hybrid assistant runs the business calendar and inbox, your personal errands and the coordination of the household. It works well in a specific situation and badly outside it.

  • Works when: a small business or a professional practice, one household without live-in staff or with one or two, one property, the owner is in Dubai most of the time
  • Works when: the household side is coordination, not hands-on work; the assistant schedules the cleaner and the maintenance, does not clean or cook
  • Breaks when: the business grows to a team that needs daily coordination and the house has several staff, two properties and children's logistics; the person starts dropping one side
  • Breaks when: you need presence in two places on the same day several times a week
  • Breaks when: confidentiality on one side is sensitive enough that you do not want it mixed with the other
  • Market estimate: 10,000 - 18,000 AED per month, senior 18,000 - 25,000, orientation; see the hybrid assistant role page

Two or three people: who does what

When one person is not enough, the usual split follows the three lists. Each role has its own page with duties, salary and how to assess.

  • Executive assistant: business calendar, inbox, meetings, follow-ups, travel, board and partner coordination; market estimate 12,000 - 20,000 AED, 20,000 - 30,000 for C-level or a private family office
  • House manager: the household staff, maintenance, suppliers, inventory, the property calendar, renovations and contractors; orientation 10,000 - 20,000 AED
  • Private driver: the car and the family's movement, school runs, airport, errands that need a car; family driver 3,000 - 5,000 AED, experienced 5,000 - 7,000, VIP chauffeur 7,000 - 10,000
  • Lifestyle manager or personal concierge, when travel, dining, events and personal bookings are a large part of the load; orientation 10,000 - 18,000 AED
  • Chief of staff, when the business side needs someone who runs projects and people for the owner, not only the calendar; orientation 25,000 - 40,000 AED depending on the size of the business
  • A personal assistant can sit between these roles for a smaller household: see our comparison of personal assistant, executive assistant and office manager

Confidentiality and how to split

More people means less concentration of information in one head but more people who know something. Decide the boundaries before the hire, not after.

  • Give each person the information their list needs: the house manager does not need the business bank, the executive assistant does not need the children's medical records
  • One shared calendar for your movements, with private entries visible only as "busy" to people who do not need the detail
  • One person coordinates the others when you are away; usually the executive assistant or the chief of staff, and that is written in both job descriptions
  • Money rules per role: the house manager has a household budget and a limit, the executive assistant prepares business payments for your approval; nobody transfers without a written rule
  • A confidentiality clause in each employment contract and a short written list of what each person may and may not share; see our guide on confidentiality, NDA and access
  • Handover notes for each role from month one, so a holiday or a resignation does not stop the house or the business

FAQ

Can one assistant handle business, personal and household tasks?

Yes, when the business is small or a professional practice, the household has one or two staff and one property, and the owner is in Dubai most of the time. It breaks when the business needs daily team coordination, the house has several staff or two properties, or you need a person in two places on the same day. Then split into an executive assistant and a house manager.

How much does an executive assistant plus a house manager cost in Dubai?

Market estimate: an executive assistant 12,000 - 20,000 AED per month and a house manager 10,000 - 20,000 AED as orientation, so about 22,000 - 40,000 AED in total, total package. Add a private driver at 3,000 - 10,000 AED depending on the level. On top of each package the employer pays visa, health insurance and a gratuity provision. One hybrid person costs 10,000 - 18,000 AED, orientation, but drops tasks when the load is above one working week.
