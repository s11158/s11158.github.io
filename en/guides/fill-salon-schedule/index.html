How to fill the schedule in a beauty salon

A salon with 50% utilisation pays full rent and full salaries for half the work. Filling the empty hours is usually cheaper than finding new clients.

Talk to us

Find the empty hours

Look at utilisation by weekday, hour and master. Most salons are full on evenings and weekends and half empty on weekday mornings.

Fill them

  • Rebook the next visit before the client leaves
  • Reactivate clients who have not visited in 60-90 days with a personal message
  • Off-peak offers for weekday mornings
  • Online booking open 24/7, including Instagram and Google
  • Masters' schedules matched to demand, not to habit

Keep them full

  • Waiting list for cancellations
  • Reminders to reduce no-shows
  • Weekly review of utilisation with the manager

How we help

We set up CRM and online booking, train administrators to rebook and reactivate, and hire masters for the hours that are actually in demand.

FAQ

How can I get more bookings in my salon?

Rebook before the client leaves, reactivate clients who have not returned, offer off-peak slots, open online booking and reduce no-shows.

What utilisation is good for a salon?

It depends on the model; many salons aim for most of the working hours booked. Track it by master and hour, not just in total.
