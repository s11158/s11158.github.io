School of assistants: types and the career ladder who an assistant is in Dubai today and where the profession leads

An assistant in Dubai is not one job but a family of jobs. One person books flights and answers WhatsApp for a founder, another runs the household of a family with staff, a third sits next to a C-level executive and prepares board papers, a fourth works remotely from another country. The titles overlap, the pay differs two or three times, and both candidates and employers mix them up. This is the opening article of our School of assistants series: the types of assistant that exist in Dubai, what each one does in a normal week, how the career ladder is built and what each step pays as a market estimate. Written for the person who is or wants to be an assistant, and useful for the employer who wants to understand whom they are actually hiring.

What an assistant does in Dubai today

Dubai concentrates owners, executives and families who move fast and manage many things at once: companies, licences, travel, children, property, staff. The assistant is the person who turns that stream into a calendar, a task list and finished actions. The common core of every type is the same.

  • Calendar and time: meetings, conflicts, travel, reminders, protecting the principal's time from low-value requests
  • Communication: inbox, WhatsApp, calls, drafting replies, following up with people who did not answer
  • Documents and routine: renewals, contracts, invoices, filing, simple reports, a record of what was decided
  • Logistics: travel, visas for trips, bookings, vendors, gifts, events
  • Confidentiality: the assistant sees money, health, family and business information and keeps it inside
  • Languages: English is the working language; Arabic and Russian are frequently requested on top, depending on the principal's circle

How to tell the types apart in practice

Job titles in Dubai are loose: a vacancy called personal assistant may be an executive assistant role, and an administrative assistant may be asked to run a founder's private life. Read the tasks, not the title.

  • Ask whose time the person protects: one principal (personal, executive, hybrid, chief of staff), a team (administrative), a household (house manager) or a family's wider affairs (family office)
  • Ask what share of the week is business, personal and household; a hybrid multi-assistant usually has all three above 20 percent each
  • Ask where the work happens: office, the principal's home, on the move, remote; virtual roles are remote by definition, lifestyle and house roles are mostly outside an office
  • Ask about access: a chief of staff and an executive assistant see decisions before they are made; an administrative assistant usually works with information that is already public inside the company
  • Ask what good looks like: for an administrative role it is accuracy and speed, for an executive role it is judgement, for a lifestyle role it is a vendor network and taste, for a house manager it is a calm, well-run home

The career ladder: step by step

Most careers in this profession move through similar steps, though people enter at different points and some skip steps when they bring a strong specialism such as finance, legal or medical administration.

  • Step 1, administrative or junior personal assistant, 5,000 - 8,000: learns the tools, the routine and the discipline of checking details; one to two years
  • Step 2, personal assistant or senior administrative assistant, 8,000 - 12,000: owns a calendar and a set of processes, starts to handle a principal's communication
  • Step 3, executive assistant or hybrid multi-assistant, 12,000 - 20,000: protects an owner's or director's time, writes on their behalf, handles confidential matters, manages a few people or vendors
  • Step 4, executive assistant to C-level or family office, senior hybrid, house or lifestyle manager, 18,000 - 30,000: full trust, budgets, staff, projects and travel without supervision
  • Step 5, chief of staff, 25,000 - 40,000: moves from supporting the principal to running parts of the business for them; this is a different job that grows out of a strong executive assistant
  • Side moves at each step: into office management, operations, project coordination, events, HR administration or clinic management; many managers in Dubai started as assistants

What moves a person up the ladder

The ladder is not about years. Two things lift people: scope and trust. Scope grows when the person takes a task from the principal and returns a finished result; trust grows when nothing leaks and nothing is dropped.

  • Written English at business level and at least one more language used in the principal's world
  • Tools: Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, shared calendars, a task tool, travel platforms, basic finance tools, AI assistants used carefully for drafts and research
  • A track record of two years or more with a named principal who will give a reference
  • Confidentiality that can be checked through references, not just promised
  • The habit of closing loops: a message answered, a booking confirmed, a document filed, the principal told
  • For the move to chief of staff: visible ownership of projects with results, not only administration

FAQ

What types of assistant are there in Dubai?

Administrative assistant, personal assistant, executive assistant, hybrid multi-assistant (business plus personal plus household), virtual or remote assistant, family office assistant, clinic owner's or doctor's admin assistant, lifestyle manager, house manager and chief of staff. The core skills overlap; the focus, access and pay differ.

What is the career path for an assistant in Dubai?

A typical path goes from administrative or junior personal assistant (market estimate 5,000 - 8,000 AED per month) through personal or senior administrative assistant (8,000 - 12,000) to executive assistant or hybrid multi-assistant (12,000 - 20,000), then to a C-level or family office role (20,000 - 30,000) and for some to chief of staff (25,000 - 40,000). Side moves into office management, operations and clinic management are common.
