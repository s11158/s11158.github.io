Nurses in Dubai clinics registered nurse vs assistant nurse

Clinic owners often ask for a nurse and mean very different things: someone to assist the doctor, someone to run a laser, or someone to do procedures. In Dubai the title on the DHA licence decides what the person may do.

Talk to us

Two main titles

The Unified Healthcare Professional Qualification Requirements (PQR), used by DHA, DOH and MOHAP, set the education and licensing requirements for each title.

  • Registered Nurse (RN): Bachelor degree in nursing (minimum three years full time). Works independently within the nursing scope and facility privileges
  • Assistant Nurse: Diploma in nursing of at least 18 months. Does not work independently; works under the supervision of a registered nurse

Licensing in Dubai

Every nurse working in a Dubai clinic needs an active DHA licence linked to that facility. The usual route:

  • Eligibility check against the PQR for the title
  • Primary source verification of diploma, licences and experience
  • DHA assessment where required; some applicants are exempt depending on their existing licence and country
  • Valid Basic Life Support (BLS) certificate, as a minimum
  • Experience requirements and exemptions have changed over time: check the current PQR and DHA guidance, not an old forum post

Scope of practice in aesthetic clinics

Whether a nurse may perform a specific aesthetic procedure, for example injectables or laser treatments, depends on DHA rules for that procedure, the nurse's title and training, and the privileges granted by the medical director. Check the current DHA standard for each procedure before you plan the schedule around a nurse.

  • Registered nurses for procedure support, patient preparation, aftercare and, where allowed, specific procedures
  • Assistant nurses for support tasks under RN supervision, not as a cheaper replacement for an RN
  • Documented training for devices and products the clinic uses
  • A head nurse once there are several nurses, to own protocols, stock and infection control

FAQ

What is the difference between a registered nurse and an assistant nurse in Dubai?

Under the unified PQR a registered nurse needs a bachelor degree in nursing and works independently within scope; an assistant nurse needs a nursing diploma of at least 18 months and works under RN supervision.

Can a nurse do aesthetic procedures in a Dubai clinic?

It depends on DHA rules for the specific procedure, the nurse's title and training, and the privileges granted by the medical director. Check the current DHA standard for each procedure.

How long does it take to hire a nurse in Dubai?

With a candidate who already holds a DHA licence, often 2-4 weeks. Licensing a nurse from abroad takes longer because of verification and assessment.
