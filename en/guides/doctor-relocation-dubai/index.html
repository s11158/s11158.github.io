Doctor relocation to Dubai step by step

Most doctors who move to Dubai lose months not on the exam but on the order of steps. The licence, the job and the visa are linked, and each one waits for the previous.

Talk to us

Licence first or job first?

DHA lets you get a professional registration without an employer: verification, assessment and a registration certificate valid for one year. It becomes a licence only when a DHA-licensed facility activates it. Many employers prefer candidates who already have the registration, because the start date becomes predictable.

  • Registration first: more control and a stronger position in the job search, you pay and manage the process yourself
  • Job first: some employers support or pay for licensing, but the offer is usually conditional on getting the licence
  • Either way, start the self-assessment and document collection at once: it costs nothing and shows the blockers early

The sequence

  • 1. Self-assessment against the PQR in Sheryan: which title you can expect
  • 2. Documents: diplomas, internship, experience letters, licences, good standing certificate, legal translations
  • 3. DataFlow primary source verification
  • 4. DHA assessment (computer-based or oral) or an exemption
  • 5. Registration certificate, valid one year
  • 6. Offer and contract: salary, housing, malpractice cover, CME support, notice period
  • 7. Licence activation by the facility, with malpractice insurance
  • 8. Employment visa, medical fitness test and Emirates ID

Family

  • Family can be sponsored once you hold residence, subject to the income and housing conditions set by the immigration authorities (ICP / GDRFA Dubai)
  • A registered tenancy (Ejari) is normally needed
  • Marriage and birth certificates must be legalised for use in the UAE, with official translation
  • School places in popular areas fill early: apply as soon as the offer is signed

Housing and the first months

  • Rent in Dubai is often paid by one or several cheques in advance, plus a deposit and agency fee: plan cash before the first salary
  • Probation is typically up to six months under UAE labour law; the notice terms in the contract matter
  • Keep CME points from the first year: they are needed for licence renewal
  • If pay includes commission, the patient base takes months to build, so compare the fixed part of offers

FAQ

Can I get a DHA licence without a job offer?

You can get a DHA professional registration without an employer: it covers verification and assessment and is valid for one year. It turns into an active licence only when a DHA-licensed facility activates it.

How long does it take a doctor to relocate to Dubai?

It depends on how fast DataFlow verifies your documents, whether you need the assessment and on the employer's visa process. Weeks for each stage is common, so a realistic plan counts in months, not days.

Can I bring my family to Dubai as a doctor?

Yes, once you hold residence, if you meet the income and housing conditions set by the immigration authorities. Documents like marriage and birth certificates need legalisation and translation.
