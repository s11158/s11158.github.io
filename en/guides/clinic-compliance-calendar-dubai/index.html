Clinic compliance calendar in Dubai

Most compliance problems in clinics are not bad intent but a missed date: a nurse's licence expired last week, the malpractice policy lapsed, BLS certificates ran out. A simple calendar with owners and reminders prevents almost all of it.

CME for professionals

DHA requires licensed professionals to complete continuing medical education or professional development before licence renewal, and the required amount differs by profession: doctors and dentists need more than nurses and allied health. Activities should be DHA-approved or internationally recognised and recorded in Sheryan. Check the current requirement for each title on DHA channels rather than an old circular.

Inspections

  • DHA inspections can happen at any time, so compliance must be continuous, not prepared for a visit
  • Keep licences, policies, staff files, training records, consent forms and device logs organised and easy to show
  • After every inspection: list the findings, assign an owner and a deadline, and close them in writing

How to keep track

  • One register for everything: item, document, expiry date, owner, status
  • Reminders at 90, 60 and 30 days before each expiry, in a CRM or task tool, not in someone's head
  • A monthly 15-minute review by the clinic manager, with the owner seeing a one-line status
  • New hires added to the register on their first day; leavers removed and licences updated in Sheryan

FAQ

What licences does a clinic in Dubai need to renew?

The DHA facility licence, the trade licence, every professional's DHA licence, plus insurance policies, staff visas and certificates. Renew each before the expiry date on the document.

How many CME hours do doctors need in Dubai?

DHA sets the requirement by profession and doctors need more than nurses or allied health. Check the current number for your title on DHA channels and log activities in Sheryan before renewal.

How do I keep track of staff licence expiries?

One register with each document, expiry date and owner, reminders 90, 60 and 30 days before expiry in a CRM or task tool, and a monthly review by the clinic manager.
