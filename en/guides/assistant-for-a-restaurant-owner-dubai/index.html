Assistant for a restaurant or cafe owner in Dubai what to delegate, which type fits and what it costs

A restaurant or cafe owner in Dubai runs two businesses at once: the one guests see and the one made of invoices, licences, staff documents, supplier calls and reviews. Most owners carry the second one in their head and in WhatsApp. An assistant can take over the routine part of it, so the owner spends the day on the menu, the team and the guests. This guide is honest about what hurts without an assistant, what the person can and cannot take over, which type of assistant fits a restaurant, what it costs as a market estimate, how to check a candidate and where the risks are.

A typical week without an assistant

The pains below are not dramatic. They are small, they repeat every week, and together they cost the owner evenings, money and reputation.

  • Monday: a supplier invoice from three weeks ago does not match the delivery note, nobody has time to compare them, and the difference is paid anyway
  • Tuesday: the trade licence or the food permit renewal is due in a month, the reminder exists only in the owner's memory, and the PRO asks for documents the day before
  • Wednesday: two staff members ask about their visa or labour card status; the owner forwards screenshots between the PRO and the accountant instead of running the dinner service
  • Thursday: a one-star review about a slow table waits unanswered for three days while a group booking for Saturday asks twice for a confirmation
  • Friday: a private event is booked by phone, the deposit is not written down, and the kitchen hears about the menu on the day
  • Weekend: the family dinner, the car service and the school form are postponed again, because every free hour goes to the restaurant

What the assistant takes over

Split the role into business, personal and household tasks and write it down. The same person can hold all three if the list is clear and the volume is realistic.

  • Business: supplier calendar and order reminders, invoice collection and matching against delivery notes, payment lists for the owner to approve
  • Business: licence and permit calendar for the venue, municipality and food safety inspection dates, collecting the documents the PRO or the consultant asks for
  • Business: staff documents coordination with the PRO: passport and visa expiry list, health cards, contract copies, reminders to the people concerned
  • Business: reservations and reviews triage: large bookings confirmed, deposits recorded, reviews sorted into "reply today" and "reply after the manager checks", drafts for the owner's approval
  • Business: events: a one-page brief for each private event with date, guests, menu, deposit, contact and who in the team is responsible
  • Personal and household: the owner's calendar, travel, medical and school appointments, car and home maintenance, gifts, bills, coordination with a housekeeper or a driver
  • What should NOT be delegated: signing contracts and leases, money transfers without a written rule and a limit, menu and pricing decisions, hiring and dismissals, confidential HR matters, anything the food safety rules reserve for the trained person in charge

How to find and check

Good restaurant assistants come from hotel and restaurant administration, event coordination, office management in hospitality, and from personal assistant roles where the owner also had a business. Test on tasks that look like your week.

  • Email and WhatsApp triage: give 15 real messages (supplier, guest, PRO, family) and ask the person to sort them by urgency and draft three replies
  • Calendar conflict: a supplier visit, a municipality inspection and a tasting for an event land on the same morning; what does the person move and whom do they inform
  • Profession-specific task: build a renewal calendar for the trade licence, the food permit and five staff visas from a messy list of dates, and flag what is due first
  • Numbers: match four invoices against delivery notes with one planted difference and see whether the person finds it
  • Red flags: no questions about your approval limits, promises to "handle the municipality" alone, careless talk about a previous employer's finances
  • Probation plan: weeks 1-2 calendar and supplier reminders with every outgoing message approved, weeks 3-4 licence calendar and reviews drafts, weeks 5-8 events briefs and the first weekly report, then a written scope

Risks and boundaries

An assistant to a restaurant owner sees margins, salaries, supplier prices and the family's life. Set the boundaries before the first day.

  • Confidentiality: a clause in the employment contract or a separate NDA covering recipes, prices, supplier terms, staff salaries and family matters
  • Access: a shared mailbox and delegated calendar instead of your passwords; a password manager; bank access read-only or none, with approvals staying with you
  • Money: a written rule for what the assistant may pay or order without asking, with a monthly limit and a weekly summary
  • Dependence on one person: a handover document with suppliers, renewal dates, PRO contacts, logins by role and recurring tasks, updated monthly
  • Backup: your restaurant manager or the accountant knows where the handover document is and can cover leave and sickness
  • Scope creep: the assistant is not a second manager, a cashier or a PRO; when the venue grows, hire those roles separately

FAQ

Does a restaurant owner in Dubai need an assistant?

If the owner spends several hours a week on invoices, licence and permit dates, staff documents, reviews and event bookings, and family logistics are pushed to late evening, an assistant pays for itself in time and in avoided mistakes such as a slipped renewal or an unpaid deposit. If a restaurant manager already covers the venue's administration, a personal assistant for the owner's own tasks may be enough.

How much does an assistant for a restaurant owner cost in Dubai?

Market estimate: an administrative assistant 5,000 - 8,000 AED per month, a personal assistant 5,000 - 10,000 AED and 12,000+ for a senior one, a hybrid multi-assistant who covers business, personal and household tasks 10,000 - 18,000 AED, senior 18,000 - 25,000 AED.
