Assistants in Dubai: who needs which types, salary estimates and the guide for your profession

The word assistant covers at least ten different jobs in Dubai, from an administrative assistant at 5,000 AED to a chief of staff at 40,000. Employers who hire by the word rather than the job end up with the wrong person, and candidates who call themselves assistant without a type get the wrong offers. This page is the entry point of our assistant series: the types with a one-line definition and a market salary estimate, which type fits a doctor, a founder, a broker, a lawyer, an investor or a relocating family, how to decide between one person and several, in-house and virtual, Russian-speaking and not, and the School of assistants for people who do this work or want to. Every line links to a longer guide.

The types of assistants, with a salary estimate and a page for each

All figures are market estimates in AED per month for the total package, basic salary plus allowances. On top the employer pays the visa, health insurance and the gratuity provision. Ranges overlap on purpose: a senior personal assistant can earn more than a junior executive assistant.

  • Administrative assistant: keeps documents, filing, scheduling and office routine running for a team or department. 5,000 - 8,000, senior 8,000 - 11,000. Role page: /en/administrative-assistant/
  • Personal assistant: runs the private life of one person or family, from appointments and renewals to travel and household vendors. 5,000 - 10,000, senior 12,000 and above. Role page: /en/personal-assistant/
  • Executive assistant: protects the time and decisions of one senior person, owns the business calendar, inbox and follow-ups. 12,000 - 20,000; for a C-level executive or a private family office 20,000 - 30,000. Role page: /en/executive-assistant/
  • Hybrid multi-assistant: one person for business, personal and household tasks of an owner whose life and company are mixed. 10,000 - 18,000, senior 18,000 - 25,000 (orientation). Role page: /en/hybrid-assistant/
  • Virtual assistant: remote, often part-time, for inbox, bookings, research and simple admin. 3,500 - 8,000 part-time or remote, up to 14,000 for a senior full-time remote person (orientation). Role page: /en/virtual-assistant/
  • Family office assistant: works for a wealthy family or its office, handles property, travel, staff coordination and confidential paperwork. 12,000 - 20,000, senior 20,000 - 30,000. Role page: /en/family-office-assistant/
  • Clinic owner's or doctor's assistant: the admin side of a medical practice owner, from licence renewals to insurance paperwork and the owner's calendar. 8,000 - 12,000, senior 12,000 - 18,000. Role page: /en/medical-office-assistant/
  • Chief of staff: a senior operator who runs projects and people on behalf of the owner, not a calendar role. 25,000 - 40,000 (orientation, depends on the size of the business). Role page: /en/chief-of-staff/
  • Lifestyle manager or personal concierge: travel, dining, events, memberships and the family's leisure logistics at a high standard. 10,000 - 18,000 (orientation). Role page: /en/lifestyle-manager/
  • House manager: runs the household staff, maintenance, suppliers and budgets of a large home or several homes. 10,000 - 20,000 (orientation). Role page: /en/house-manager/
  • The full salary table with seniority levels and languages is in our guide /en/guides/assistant-salary-dubai-2026/ and the office staff overview /en/guides/office-staff-salaries-dubai/

Who needs which: by profession

One line per profession: the pain that usually brings the person to us, the type of assistant that fits, and the guide. Each guide describes a typical week without an assistant, what to delegate, what it costs, how to check candidates and the risks.

  • Doctor: patient time is paid, admin time is not, and licence renewals, CME deadlines and insurance queries pile up after hours. Fit: clinic owner's or doctor's assistant, in-house. Guide: /en/guides/assistant-for-a-doctor-dubai/
  • Dentist who owns the practice: treating in the chair all day while suppliers, lab orders, staff visas and the owner's own calendar wait. Fit: doctor's assistant plus a clinic manager if the practice has several chairs. Guide: /en/guides/assistant-for-a-dentist-practice-owner-dubai/
  • Founder or CEO: the calendar and inbox become the bottleneck of the whole company. Fit: executive assistant, later a chief of staff. Guide: /en/guides/assistant-for-a-founder-ceo-dubai/
  • Investor or wealthy family: property, bankers, lawyers, travel and household staff with no single person holding the thread. Fit: family office assistant or executive assistant, often with a house manager. Guide: /en/guides/assistant-for-an-investor-or-hnwi-dubai/
  • Real estate broker: leads go cold while you are at viewings; listings, contracts and follow-ups slip. Fit: personal or virtual assistant with CRM skills. Guide: /en/guides/assistant-for-a-real-estate-broker-dubai/
  • Lawyer: billable hours lost to scheduling, filing and client logistics; confidentiality rules out a casual helper. Fit: executive or administrative assistant with an NDA. Guide: /en/guides/assistant-for-a-lawyer-dubai/
  • Entrepreneur with several businesses: five teams, five calendars, one owner; decisions wait for you. Fit: hybrid multi-assistant now, chief of staff when the group grows. Guide: /en/guides/assistant-for-a-multi-business-entrepreneur-dubai/
  • Content creator: shoots, brand deals, invoices, comments and travel eat the time that makes the content. Fit: virtual or personal assistant who can also coordinate production. Guide: /en/guides/assistant-for-a-content-creator-dubai/
  • Restaurant owner: suppliers, municipality paperwork, staff visas and reservations call you at every hour. Fit: administrative or hybrid assistant. Guide: /en/guides/assistant-for-a-restaurant-owner-dubai/
  • Relocating family: schools, housing, Emirates ID, cars, doctors and a new city all at once. Fit: personal assistant or lifestyle manager for the first months, often Russian-speaking. Guide: /en/guides/assistant-for-a-relocating-family-dubai/
  • Salon chain owner: several locations, rotas, stock, licences and marketing, and you are still doing payroll on Sunday night. Fit: executive assistant or hybrid assistant plus salon managers. Guide: /en/guides/assistant-for-a-salon-chain-owner-dubai/
  • Yacht owner: crew, berths, maintenance, charters and guests, each with its own paperwork. Fit: personal assistant or lifestyle manager working with the captain. Guide: /en/guides/assistant-for-a-yacht-owner-dubai/
  • Clinic or salon owner in general: the earlier guide /en/guides/assistant-for-clinic-or-salon-owner-dubai/ covers the owner who treats clients and manages the place at the same time

How to decide: one person or several, in-house or virtual, Russian-speaking or not

Three decisions settle most of the choice. Make them before you write a job description, otherwise the description becomes a wish list for a person who does not exist.

  • One person or several: write down your top ten recurring tasks and mark each as business, personal or household. If one group is clearly the majority, hire for that type. If the three are evenly mixed and the volume fits one working week, a hybrid multi-assistant works. If the household alone is a full-time job, that is a house manager and the business needs its own person. Guide: /en/guides/one-assistant-or-several-business-personal-household/
  • In-house or virtual: tasks that need presence (errands, documents, visitors, your home) need an in-house person; inbox, bookings, research and CRM work can be remote. Many owners start with a virtual assistant and add an in-house one when the physical tasks grow. Guide: /en/guides/virtual-vs-in-house-assistant-dubai/
  • Russian-speaking or not: if you, your family and your partners communicate in Russian, a Russian-speaking assistant with fluent English removes translation from every task; if your business runs in English and Arabic, language matters less than judgement. Guide: /en/guides/russian-speaking-assistant-dubai/

FAQ

Which type of assistant do I need in Dubai?

Start from where your time goes. Business calendar and inbox: an executive assistant. Private life and family logistics: a personal assistant. Business, personal and household mixed in one week: a hybrid multi-assistant. Remote admin only: a virtual assistant. A large home with staff: a house manager. Projects and people on your behalf rather than a calendar: a chief of staff. The profession list above gives the usual fit for a doctor, a founder, a broker, a lawyer, an investor or a family.

How much does an assistant cost in Dubai?

Market estimates for the total monthly package: administrative assistant 5,000 - 8,000 AED, personal assistant 5,000 - 10,000, executive assistant 12,000 - 20,000 (20,000 - 30,000 for C-level or a family office), hybrid multi-assistant 10,000 - 18,000, virtual assistant 3,500 - 8,000 part-time, chief of staff 25,000 - 40,000, lifestyle manager 10,000 - 18,000, house manager 10,000 - 20,000. On top the employer pays the visa, health insurance and the gratuity provision.
