Cosmetologist in Dubai: doctor or esthetician?

It decides which treatments you may legally offer — and whether a DHA licence is needed.

A "cosmetologist" in Dubai is two different specialists with different rights. Who to hire depends on which services you want to offer legally.

Aesthetic doctor

  • Injectables, lasers, medical procedures
  • Requires a DHA licence + medical degree
  • Pricier, longer to onboard — but unlocks medical services

Esthetician

  • Care treatments: cleansing, peels, device-based care
  • No DHA licence needed (non-medical)
  • Faster and more affordable, but no injectables or lasers
Owner takeaway: first decide what's on your menu. Injectables and medical devices → you need a DHA doctor. Care only → an esthetician is enough. Often a salon needs both.

Frequently asked questions

Is a cosmetologist a doctor or an esthetician?

It depends on the procedures. Injections, device-based and medical procedures may only be performed by a licensed aesthetic-medicine doctor. Non-medical, care procedures can be done by a qualified esthetician or cosmetologist.

Who can perform injections and device procedures in Dubai?

As a rule, only a specialist with a valid DHA medical licence of the relevant profile. Non-medical staff may not perform invasive and certain device-based procedures — this is regulated by law.

Should a salon hire a doctor or an esthetician?

It depends on your service list. Medical aesthetics needs a licensed doctor; care procedures suit a qualified esthetician. TLNT helps match a specialist to your procedure list and licence requirements.
