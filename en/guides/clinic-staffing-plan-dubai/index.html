Clinic staffing plan roles, ratios and hiring order

A small clinic in Dubai usually runs on 6 to 15 people. The mistake is not the headcount but the mix: too many doctors and nobody to book, confirm and bill, or a full front desk and an empty schedule.

Talk to us

Typical roles in a small clinic

  • Medical director: required in both models; in an outpatient clinic often also a practising doctor, in an aesthetic clinic often the lead aesthetic doctor or dermatologist
  • Doctors: GPs and 1-3 specialists in an outpatient clinic; 1-2 aesthetic doctors or dermatologists in an aesthetic clinic
  • Nurses: registered nurses for triage, vitals and procedures; in aesthetics, registered nurses with aesthetic training
  • Laser or device specialist: rare in outpatient clinics, usually 1-2 in an aesthetic clinic
  • Front desk: receptionists who know insurance (outpatient) or who can sell a consultation (aesthetic)
  • Patient coordinator: optional in outpatient clinics, often key in aesthetics because someone must follow up leads and treatment plans
  • Insurance officer: needed once insurance patients are a real share; rare in aesthetics, where most work is self-pay
  • Clinic manager: from opening, or once there are 2+ doctors or the owner steps back from daily operations

Ratios as orientation, not rules

These are rough starting points we use in planning. The real numbers depend on your services, opening hours, DHA requirements for each procedure and how many rooms run at the same time.

  • About one nurse for every one or two doctors who do procedures at the same time
  • About one receptionist for every two or three practitioners working in parallel, plus cover for breaks and long hours
  • One patient coordinator can usually follow up the leads and plans of several doctors if a CRM does the reminders
  • Insurance billing: one officer until volume or rejections show you need a second person or a coder

What to hire first

Hire in the order in which roles block the opening, not in the order you meet candidates.

  • Medical director and the doctors your licensed services depend on: DHA licensing or licence transfer takes the longest
  • Clinic manager, who then helps hire and train everyone else
  • Nurses and device specialists, whose licences also go through DHA
  • Front desk and coordinator 2-4 weeks before opening, so they learn the CRM and scripts before the first patient

FAQ

How many staff does a small clinic in Dubai need?

Often 6 to 15 people: a medical director, one or more doctors, nurses, front desk, and depending on the model a patient coordinator, insurance officer and clinic manager. The mix matters more than the number.

Who should I hire first for a new clinic?

The medical director and doctors your licensed services depend on, because DHA licensing takes the longest. Then the clinic manager, then nurses, then front desk.

How many nurses per doctor does a clinic need?

As an orientation, about one nurse for every one or two doctors doing procedures at the same time. Check DHA requirements for your specific procedures.
