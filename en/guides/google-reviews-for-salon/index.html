Google reviews for a salon in Dubai

In Dubai many new clients choose a salon on Google Maps by rating, number of reviews and how the owner answers. Reviews are a system, not luck.

Talk to us

How to get more reviews honestly

  • Ask every client at the end of the visit, when they are happy with the result
  • Send a short WhatsApp message with the direct review link an hour or two after the visit
  • Put a QR code with the review link at the reception desk and the mirror stations
  • Make it the administrator's routine and track the number of new reviews per week
  • Answer every review, positive too: it shows the salon is alive and encourages others

How to answer a negative review

  • Reply within 24-48 hours, calmly, from the salon's name, not with the owner's emotions
  • Thank the client, acknowledge the experience without arguing about details in public
  • Do not publish any personal details, visit history or photos of the client
  • Offer to fix it offline: "Please message us on WhatsApp, we would like to put this right"
  • After solving it, you can politely ask if the client wants to update the review, but never pressure them
  • Use the complaint internally: which master, which service, what to change

What never to do

These practices break Google's review policies and can lead to removed reviews or restrictions on the listing. Public accusations or disclosing client details can also create legal risk in the UAE, where privacy and defamation rules are strict.

  • No fake reviews from staff, friends or paid services
  • No discounts, gifts or free services in exchange for a review
  • No review gating: do not ask only happy clients and filter out the rest
  • No answering in anger, blaming or threatening the client
  • No buying review packages or "rating boosts"

A simple review routine

  • Daily: administrator sends review links to yesterday's clients
  • Twice a week: the manager answers all new reviews
  • Weekly: count of new reviews and average rating in the owner's dashboard
  • Monthly: review themes discussed at the team meeting

FAQ

How can my salon get more Google reviews?

Ask every happy client at the end of the visit, send the direct review link on WhatsApp shortly after, place a QR code at reception and make it a daily administrator task.

How should a salon respond to a bad Google review?

Within 24-48 hours, calmly, thank the client, acknowledge the experience, share no personal details and offer to solve it offline via WhatsApp. Then fix the cause inside the team.

Can I give a discount for a Google review?

No. Rewards for reviews, fake reviews and asking only happy clients break Google's policies and can get reviews removed or the listing restricted.
