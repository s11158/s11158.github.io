Instagram and TikTok that bring bookings, not likes

A Dubai salon can have beautiful reels and an empty Tuesday. The goal of social media is bookings, so every post should make it obvious what you do, for whom, at what price level and how to book.

Talk to us

Set up the profile to convert

  • Bio: service, area and one reason to choose you, for example "Nails and brows in JLT, Russian and English speaking"
  • One clear booking link or WhatsApp button, tested monthly
  • Highlights: prices, works by category, masters, reviews, location and parking
  • Location tag and area in every post; people search by area in Dubai
  • Reply to direct messages within minutes during opening hours, like WhatsApp

Reels ideas that bring clients

  • Process videos: 15-30 seconds of the most satisfying part of the service
  • "What 250 AED gets you": clear pricing reduces hesitant enquiries
  • Master introductions: who they are, their speciality, how long they have worked
  • Client questions answered in 20 seconds: how long gel lasts, how to prepare for colouring
  • Free slot stories: "Two slots left for tomorrow with Maria" with a booking sticker
  • Seasonal content: before a holiday, wedding season, summer heat care

Masters as faces, salon as owner

People book a person, so show the masters. But keep the content on the salon account, agree in writing how photos of work can be used, and make sure booking always goes through the salon link. This keeps the portfolio with the salon if someone leaves.

Rules for before and after

  • Get the client's written consent before posting any photo, and respect a refusal
  • Same light, angle and background, no filters that change the result
  • Do not promise results the service cannot guarantee
  • If a service is medical (injections, some laser and skin procedures), advertising is regulated by the health authority and may need approval; check the current rules before posting
  • If you pay bloggers, check the current UAE Media Council rules on paid promotion and permits

Measure bookings, not likes

Track how many bookings come from Instagram and TikTok each week in the CRM, with the source recorded by the administrator. Views are only useful if they turn into messages and messages into visits.

FAQ

What content brings bookings to a beauty salon on Instagram?

Short process reels, clear pricing examples, introductions of masters, answers to common client questions and stories with free slots and a booking sticker. Every post should show the service, the area and how to book.

Can a salon post before and after photos in the UAE?

For salon services, yes, with the client's written consent and honest, unfiltered photos. For medical procedures advertising is regulated by the health authority and may need approval, so check the current rules first.

Should salon masters have their own Instagram?

They can, but the salon should feature them on its own account and keep bookings on the salon link, so the portfolio and the client relationship stay with the salon.
