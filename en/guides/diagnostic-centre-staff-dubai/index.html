Staffing a diagnostic centre in Dubai: who to hire

An imaging and laboratory centre depends on a small group of licensed specialists, and a gap in one role can stop a whole modality. This guide lists the roles, the order in which to hire them, what to check on the licence side and the market salary estimates, so the owner can plan the payroll before the first interview. Facility and professional licensing is set by the health authority, so confirm the current requirements before you commit to a date.

Who to hire first

Start with the person who sets the standard for the others, then add the people who generate studies, and only then the support roles.

  • A radiologist or a medical lead who signs the reports: without this person the centre cannot release results
  • Radiographers for each modality you plan to open, with a second person for cover so one absence does not close the room
  • A sonographer if ultrasound is on the service list, ideally with experience in the exam types your referring doctors order
  • A lab technician or a small team if the laboratory is in house; the volume of samples decides whether one person is enough
  • A coordinator who handles insurance approvals and bookings, because refused claims and empty slots cost more than one salary

Licences and safety: what to check

The centre and every clinical professional work under the health authority of the emirate, for Dubai the Dubai Health Authority. Work with ionising radiation is also subject to federal radiation safety rules.

  • Professional licence: diploma and experience verification and the licence category that fits the position; the sequence of steps depends on the case
  • Radiation safety: staff who work with X-ray, CT or similar equipment need to meet the radiation safety rules; confirm the current requirements with the regulator before the start date
  • Equipment and facility licensing is a separate track from the people, so run both in parallel
  • Ask for original documents and verify them at the source, do not rely on copies
  • The employer sponsors the visa; we give a step-by-step checklist

Common mistakes

Most delays are not about finding people, they are about the order of steps and about single points of failure.

  • Buying equipment before the radiologist and radiographers are confirmed
  • Hiring one radiographer per modality with no cover
  • Treating the coordinator as an afterthought, then losing revenue to rejected claims
  • Offering a package below the market and then waiting months, see the salary ranges above
  • Skipping a practical check of the technician on the actual equipment type

FAQ

How many staff does a small diagnostic centre need in Dubai?

It depends on the services. A centre with X-ray, ultrasound and a basic laboratory typically needs a reporting radiologist, one or two radiographers, a sonographer, a lab technician and a coordinator, plus cover for absences. CT and MRI add specialists. Confirm the staffing conditions of your licence with the health authority.

Who has to sign imaging reports in a Dubai diagnostic centre?

Reports are issued by a licensed radiologist under the centre's licence. Many centres work with a radiologist on staff or a reporting arrangement; the exact model should be confirmed with the health authority and set out in the contract.

How much do radiographers and sonographers earn in Dubai?

Market estimate: radiographers about 8,000 - 25,000 AED per month and sonographers about 10,000 - 25,000 AED, depending on level, modality and the clinic. See our role pages for the ranges by level.
