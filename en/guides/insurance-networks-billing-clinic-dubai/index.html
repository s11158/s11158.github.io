Insurance networks and billing for a clinic in Dubai

Health insurance is mandatory for residents in Dubai, so for many outpatient clinics insurance patients are the main flow. Getting on the networks is only half of it: the money is made or lost in approvals, coding and rejections.

Talk to us

Getting on insurance panels

Each insurer or third-party administrator (TPA) signs its own provider agreement. There is no single application for all of them.

  • Have the basics ready: active DHA facility licence, trade licence, licensed doctors, services list and your price list
  • Apply to insurers and TPAs one by one; each decides whether your clinic fits their network and in which category
  • Negotiate the price list, covered services and payment terms, and read the audit and recovery clauses
  • Expect the process to take weeks to months and not every insurer to accept a new clinic at once
  • Aesthetic and cosmetic services are usually excluded from cover, so an aesthetic clinic may not need panels at all

Prior approvals and eClaimLink

In Dubai, eligibility checks, prior authorisation requests, claims and remittances go electronically through the DHA-managed platforms (eClaimLink and the DHA Health Post Office, DHPO). Registration requires a valid facility licence.

  • Check eligibility and cover before the visit, not at the payment desk
  • Send prior authorisation for services that require it, with the diagnosis and clinical justification written clearly
  • Record the patient's approved services so doctors do not perform unapproved extras
  • Submission and response deadlines are set by DHA policy and change over time: keep the current circulars at hand

Coding and rejection management

Most rejections come from preventable causes: wrong or missing codes, services not approved, eligibility not checked, documentation that does not support the claim.

  • Diagnoses and services must be coded correctly (ICD and CPT codes as required by DHA) and match the medical record
  • Read every remittance: group rejections by reason, by doctor and by insurer
  • Resubmit corrected claims within the allowed window; appeal where the clinical record supports it
  • Feed the reasons back to doctors and front desk weekly, otherwise the same mistakes repeat

Numbers to track

  • Rejection rate by insurer and by reason
  • Time from service to submission and from submission to payment
  • Receivables by age, and how much is written off
  • Share of prior approvals granted on first request

FAQ

How does a clinic get on insurance networks in Dubai?

By signing a provider agreement with each insurer or TPA separately. You need an active DHA facility licence, licensed doctors and a price list; each insurer decides on acceptance and network category.

What is eClaimLink?

The DHA-managed electronic platform through which Dubai providers and insurers exchange eligibility checks, prior authorisations, claims and remittances. A valid facility licence is needed to register.

Why are my clinic's insurance claims rejected?

Usually wrong or missing codes, services without prior approval, eligibility not checked or documentation that does not support the claim. Track rejections by reason and fix the process, not just the claim.
