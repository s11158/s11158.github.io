What if the new hire leaves? The replacement guarantee

A bad hire costs a salon or clinic in Dubai far more than the agency fee: lost clients, a visa already paid and weeks of searching again. Here is how we share that risk.

Talk to us

How our guarantee works

If the employee leaves or you decide they do not fit within 2 months of starting, we find a replacement for free. You do not pay a second fee.

The real cost of a bad hire

  • Visa, Emirates ID, medical test and insurance already paid
  • Clients lost to a weak master or receptionist
  • Management time spent on training and then on the exit
  • Weeks without the role covered

How we reduce the risk before the start

  • Reference calls to previous employers
  • Trial shifts or practical tasks for hands-on roles
  • Licence checks for medical staff
  • An honest talk about salary, schedule and commission before the offer

FAQ

How long is the guarantee?

2 months from the employee's first working day.

Is the replacement really free?

Yes, there is no second fee for the replacement.

What is not covered?

Changes to the role or salary after the hire, and dismissals caused by the employer's restructuring. We agree the details in the contract.
