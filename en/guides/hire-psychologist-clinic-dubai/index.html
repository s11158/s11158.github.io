How to hire a psychologist for your clinic in Dubai

Clinic owners add psychology to a medical or aesthetic centre to serve clients who ask for it, and then find that the hire is different from a doctor or a nurse: the profile, the licence and the pay model all need a decision first. This guide walks through those decisions. Salary figures are market estimates, and licensing is described as steps because requirements are set by the health authority and can change.

Decide the profile first

Psychologists work in different ways, and a vague job description brings vague candidates. Write down who the clients are and what the clinic will treat before you search.

  • Counselling psychologist: support for stress, relationships, adjustment and life changes
  • Clinical psychologist: assessment and treatment of diagnosed conditions, often working next to a psychiatrist
  • Child and family work, couples therapy or a specific method each need their own experience
  • Language matters: English is the base, and Russian or Arabic is in high demand among clients
  • Decide whether you need in-person sessions, online sessions for clients in the UAE, or both

Licensing in Dubai: the steps

Psychology is a licensed profession in Dubai and the category depends on the degree and supervised experience. A candidate who does not yet hold the licence can still be hired, but the timeline then includes the licensing steps.

  • Primary source verification of the degree, licence and experience through DataFlow
  • An assessment by the health authority for the category
  • The licence, issued once the file and assessment are cleared
  • Linking the licence to your facility, so the psychologist can work under your clinic
  • Online practice for UAE clients also requires proper licensing, so do not start sessions before the licence is active

Pay models and market estimates

Market estimate, AED per month: counselling psychologist about 12,000 - 20,000, clinical psychologist about 18,000 - 30,000, senior or lead psychologist about 30,000 - 40,000. The figure depends on experience, languages and the package.

  • Fixed salary gives stability and suits clinics that want the psychologist to build the service
  • A revenue share per session is common in private practice; write down how a cancelled session is counted
  • A mixed model, a lower base plus a share, is a middle path for a new service
  • The employer covers the work permit, residence visa, medical test, Emirates ID and mandatory health insurance; by UAE law these costs cannot be deducted from the employee's pay
  • Exact visa and insurance costs depend on the company category, emirate and plan

What to check before you hire

A psychologist is trusted with sensitive information, so the checks matter more than speed.

  • Degree, licence status and supervised hours, confirmed from documents
  • Experience with the client group you plan to serve, and with the language they speak
  • Approach to confidentiality, record keeping and referral to a psychiatrist when needed
  • A short practical assessment on a model, for example a described case and a written plan of the first three sessions
  • References from a previous manager, and the candidate's visa status

FAQ

Does a psychologist need a DHA licence in Dubai?

Yes, psychology is a licensed profession in Dubai, and online sessions for clients in the UAE also require proper licensing. The category depends on degree and supervised experience.

How much does a psychologist earn in Dubai?

Market estimate: about 12,000 - 20,000 AED per month for a counselling psychologist, 18,000 - 30,000 AED for a clinical psychologist and 30,000 - 40,000 AED for a senior or lead psychologist. Private practices often use a revenue share per session.

How long does it take to hire a psychologist for a clinic?

First candidates usually appear in one to two weeks and hiring takes about three to six weeks. If the candidate still needs the DHA licence, the licensing steps add to the timeline.
