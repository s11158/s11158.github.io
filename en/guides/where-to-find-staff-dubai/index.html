Where to find staff in Dubai

Sourcing channels, agency vs DIY — and why the latter usually takes longer.

There are several ways to find a candidate in Dubai. The question is how much of your time and nerves it costs.

Main channels

  • Job portals and listings
  • Professional networks and niche chats
  • Referrals and word of mouth
  • A recruitment agency with a ready base

DIY vs an agency

On your own it's dozens of applications, screening, interviews and paperwork on your shoulders; strong candidates leave while you deliberate. An agency takes sourcing, first screening and part of the paperwork off you — you see only suitable people.

An agency fee is usually a percentage or a fixed sum (ours is one month's salary of the placed employee, 50/50). Using an agency is optional, but it saves the most expensive thing — your time.

Frequently asked questions

Where do you find Russian-speaking staff in Dubai?

The main channels are specialised agencies, community referrals, niche Telegram chats and social media. For beauty and medicine, pre-screening by qualifications and licences matters, so many owners delegate hiring to an agency.

Why is an agency better than searching yourself?

An agency handles screening, experience checks and interviews, saving the owner dozens of hours. TLNT also helps with paperwork and onboarding and offers a 2-month replacement guarantee, reducing the risk of a bad hire.

How long does it take to find the right candidate?

On average around 2 weeks, but it depends on the role and requirements for licence and language. For niche medical positions requiring a DHA licence the search can take longer.
