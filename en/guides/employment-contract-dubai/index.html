Employment contract & salary in Dubai

MOHRE, WPS, probation and gratuity — what hiring can't be done properly without.

Once the employee starts, the relationship must be formalised by law. Here's what matters to an owner.

MOHRE contract and WPS

  • On the mainland the contract is registered with MOHRE and must comply with federal labour law
  • Salary is paid through the WPS (Wage Protection System)
  • The contract sets out the role, salary and terms

End-of-service gratuity

21 days of basic salary per year for the first 5 years, then 30 days per year; on basic salary, with a cap. It's payable at the end of the contract within a short statutory window.

Exact rules (probation, termination, payment deadlines) depend on the contract type and the law, and norms are updated periodically — confirm the details via official sources. We help formalise the relationship correctly and design the motivation.
