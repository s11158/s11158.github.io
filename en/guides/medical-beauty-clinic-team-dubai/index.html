Medical beauty clinic team in Dubai who to hire first, who next and what to avoid

An aesthetic clinic sells trust before it sells treatments, and the team shows that trust in every step from the first WhatsApp message to the follow-up after an injection. This guide answers the question owners ask most: who should the clinic hire first, what comes next, how licences affect the start date and which mistakes cost the most. Salaries are market estimates and match our role pages.

The founding team

A clinic cannot open its treatment room without a licensed doctor, and it cannot grow without someone who turns enquiries into bookings. The founding team therefore combines one clinical lead and one front-line person.

Sequence: what to add and when

Add people in the order that protects revenue and quality.

  • Step 1: the aesthetic doctor or a dermatologist as the clinical lead, plus a patient coordinator who answers enquiries the same day
  • Step 2: a nurse injector once the doctor's schedule is limited by consultations rather than by injections
  • Step 3: a laser technician or laser nurse when laser packages are in your price list; they bring repeat visits and steady revenue
  • Step 4: a dermatologist when you want medical skin treatments and a stronger reputation, or when acne, pigmentation and skin disease patients keep arriving
  • Step 5: a clinic manager when the owner can no longer control the schedule, suppliers and standards personally
  • Step 6: further doctors by service line, such as a plastic surgeon (/en/plastic-surgeon/), a hair transplant surgeon (/en/hair-transplant-surgeon/) or a longevity physician (/en/longevity-physician/), only after the core services run smoothly
  • An aesthetic nurse (/en/aesthetic-nurse/) can support treatment rooms, consultations and aftercare; the exact scope depends on the licence and the supervising doctor

Licence timing

Doctors, nurses and laser staff who treat patients need a licence from the regulator: DHA in Dubai, DOH in Abu Dhabi, MOHAP in the northern emirates. The scope of what each title may do is set by the regulator and by your facility licence; we describe the steps, not the rules by heart.

  • Documents go through DataFlow primary source verification
  • The regulator checks eligibility against its requirements for the title
  • An assessment follows where it is required
  • The licence is activated under the employing facility, and the person treats patients only after that
  • A doctor or nurse who already holds a Dubai licence transfers to your facility faster than a person coming from abroad
  • The patient coordinator and the manager need no clinical licence, so hire them first and let them prepare bookings, price list and patient scripts while the clinical licences are in process
  • Check that your facility licence covers the services you plan to sell; a salon licence does not allow medical treatments, and our guide on aesthetic clinic versus beauty salon explains the difference

Mistakes that cost the most

Aesthetic clinics lose money in a small number of predictable ways.

  • Hiring a famous injector with a patient base but no written rule on who owns the base if the person leaves
  • Paying commission only, so the doctor sells procedures and nobody builds consultations and follow-up
  • No patient coordinator: enquiries wait for hours, and the lead goes to the competitor
  • A laser technician hired before the facility licence and equipment are confirmed
  • Advertising treatments or doctors in a way the regulator does not allow; check the rules before the first post
  • Choosing by a portfolio of photos instead of by a practical skills assessment on a model and a check of the licence route
  • Skipping the nurse protocols: the doctor's scope, consent forms and aftercare must be written, not remembered

FAQ

Who should I hire first for an aesthetic clinic in Dubai?

An aesthetic doctor or a dermatologist as the clinical lead, and a patient coordinator who turns enquiries into bookings. Add a nurse injector when the doctor's time is limited, a laser technician when laser packages are on your price list, and a clinic manager when the owner can no longer run the daily work.

How much does an aesthetic doctor earn in Dubai?

Market estimate: about 15,000 - 35,000 AED per month, with senior doctors above that; a specialist dermatologist about 35,000 - 50,000 AED. Many clinics combine a fixed salary with a share of revenue. The exact range depends on experience, licence status and the compensation model.
