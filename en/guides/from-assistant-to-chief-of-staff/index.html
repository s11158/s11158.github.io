From assistant to chief of staff how an assistant's career grows in Dubai

After three to five years as an executive or personal assistant, many people in Dubai hit the same ceiling: the work is trusted and well paid, but it is still a support role, and the next step is not obvious. This article maps the realistic routes out of the assistant seat: chief of staff, business or operations manager, house or family office manager. It explains what actually changes in the work, what to learn before you ask, how to negotiate the step with your current employer, and what the market pays, as orientation ranges rather than promises.

What actually changes in the work

The title is the smallest part of the step. The real change is what you are responsible for and who waits for your answer.

  • From tasks to outcomes: an assistant is asked to book, prepare and confirm; a chief of staff is asked to make sure the project happens, and decides which tasks that requires
  • From one person to the team: you start following up with department heads, finance, lawyers and partners on the owner's behalf, and they have to take you seriously without the owner in the room
  • From the calendar to the agenda: you help decide what deserves the owner's time this quarter, not only where the meeting fits this week
  • From information to judgement: you prepare a one-page summary with a recommendation, not a folder of documents
  • From supporting to managing: an operations or house manager has people reporting to them; hiring, feedback and dismissal become part of the week
  • From availability to boundaries: paradoxically, the senior roles need you less reachable at midnight and more reliable on the things you own

What to learn before you ask for the step

Employers rarely promote an assistant into a chief of staff role because of loyalty alone. They do it when the person already does parts of the job. Build the evidence first.

  • Project follow-up: take one real project from the owner, keep a tracker of owners, deadlines and status, and send a weekly one-page update without being asked. Three months of this is your portfolio
  • Numbers: read a monthly P and L and a cash flow, understand the key figures of the business and be able to spot when something looks wrong. A short finance-for-non-finance course is enough to start
  • Writing for decisions: the one-page memo with context, options and a recommendation. Practise on real questions the owner has to decide
  • Meetings: prepare the agenda, run the follow-up list and chase it. Ask to attend management meetings as the person who tracks actions
  • People basics: how to give feedback, how to onboard a new hire, how a UAE employment contract and probation work in general terms; see our guide to probation and notice in the UAE
  • Tools beyond the calendar: a task board for the team, a shared document system, a simple CRM or finance tool; our tools article covers the toolkit
  • For the household route: vendor contracts, property maintenance calendars, staff schedules and payroll basics, security and privacy routines

How to negotiate the step with your employer

The best time to ask is after you have already carried a project to the end, not after a hard week. Bring facts, a proposal and a timeline.

  • Open with evidence, not a request: in the last six months I ran these three things end to end; here is what changed for you. Then: I would like to formalise this
  • Propose the role in writing: title, the areas you will own, what you will stop doing (and who takes it over), how success will be measured after 90 days
  • Separate the two conversations: first the scope and the title, then the package. Agreeing the scope first makes the salary discussion about the market, not about you
  • Anchor the package with the market, as orientation: chief of staff about 25,000 - 40,000 AED per month depending on the business, senior EA to C-level or a family office 20,000 - 30,000. Ask for a review date if the employer wants to see the role work first
  • Suggest a transition: a three-month period with the new scope and a clear review, so the employer takes a smaller risk and you get a fair test
  • If the answer is no and there is no path, say so calmly and start looking while still employed; your notice period follows your contract. A clean exit keeps the reference you will need
  • If you are moving to a new employer for the step, the same evidence works in the interview: three projects, numbers, the memo you wrote, the team you coordinated

Salary anchors and how to read them

Two chief of staff roles in Dubai can differ by half in pay. Here is what usually decides where in the range a person lands.

  • Size and complexity of the business: a group with several companies and a family office pays at the top; a single small company pays near the bottom or keeps the senior EA title
  • Scope you actually own: projects and department follow-up pay more than an extended calendar role with a bigger title
  • People reporting to you: a manager with a team is paid for management, not for support
  • Languages: working English is the base; Arabic for government and local partners, or Russian for Russian-speaking owners and their circle, usually lifts the offer
  • Total package: basic salary plus allowances; the employer also pays the visa, health insurance and the end-of-service gratuity provision. Compare offers on the whole package, not the headline

FAQ

What is the difference between an executive assistant and a chief of staff?

An executive assistant manages the executive's calendar, inbox, travel and communication and is judged on reliability. A chief of staff runs the owner's priorities and projects, follows up with department heads and prepares decisions, and is judged on outcomes. The chief of staff usually has a smaller operational load and a much larger share of responsibility.

How much does a chief of staff earn in Dubai?

Market estimate about 25,000 - 40,000 AED per month as a total package, as orientation: the top depends on the size and complexity of the business. A senior executive assistant to a C-level executive or a private family office earns about 20,000 - 30,000 AED. The employer also pays the visa, health insurance and gratuity provision.
