Nurse salaries in Dubai by clinic type, 2026

What a clinic in Dubai pays a nurse depends on the type of work first: a general clinic, an aesthetic clinic, an IV therapy service or care at home. This guide puts the main nurse roles side by side as market estimates in AED per month, shows what moves the number and what an employer pays on top of the salary.

Why the clinic type changes the number

Two nurses with the same experience can sit several thousand dirhams apart because the job is different. These are the usual drivers.

  • General clinic: the broadest pool of candidates and the most predictable pay; the salary follows the DHA title and years of experience
  • Aesthetic clinic: the nurse supports doctors with injectables and devices and, where the rules allow, performs procedures; a trained nurse with a client following is paid at the upper end, often as a fixed base plus commission
  • IV therapy and wellness: a service model with shifts, often home or hotel visits; a bonus tied to the number of sessions is common
  • Home care: lower fixed salary in many cases, but transport, flexible hours and live-in or visit-based models change the package
  • Day surgery and specialised units: experience in a theatre or a monitored environment is scarce and priced higher

Registered nurse or assistant nurse

The DHA licence title decides what the person may do in your clinic, and the pay follows the title. A registered nurse has a bachelor degree in nursing and works independently within scope. An assistant nurse holds a nursing diploma and works under the supervision of a registered nurse. An assistant nurse is not a cheaper replacement for an RN: plan the schedule so that a registered nurse is always on duty where the rules require one.

  • Check the title on the DHA licence, not only on the CV
  • Whether a nurse may perform a given aesthetic procedure depends on the current DHA rules for that procedure, the nurse's training and the privileges granted by your medical director
  • A nurse who already holds an active DHA licence starts faster; a nurse from abroad goes through DataFlow verification and, if required, an exam, so the start date moves by weeks or months
  • Valid Basic Life Support (BLS) is a minimum; ask for training records for the devices and products you use

What else moves the salary

Beyond the clinic type, these points usually decide where a particular nurse sits in the range.

  • Years of experience in Dubai or the Gulf and the facility types worked in
  • Languages: Arabic for local patients, Russian for the Russian-speaking community, Hindi or Tagalog depending on the clinic
  • Procedure skills: IV cannulation, laser, injectables, wound care, sterilisation protocols
  • Housing, transport and flights: some clinics add an allowance or provide housing, which changes the comparison between offers
  • Shift pattern: evenings, weekends and 24-hour home visits are paid differently from a fixed daytime schedule

The cost on top of salary

Salary is not the full cost of a nurse. Add these items when you compare candidates and plan the team.

  • Visa and Emirates ID: paid by the employer; the amount depends on the company type and the free zone
  • DHA licensing for the facility: the nurse's licence is activated when your DHA-licensed facility hires them; amounts depend on the case
  • Health insurance for every employee; the premium depends on the plan and the person's age
  • Gratuity provision: about 5.83% of basic salary per month in the first five years of service, see our gratuity guide
  • Annual leave of 30 calendar days after the first year and the cost of covering the shift
  • Training for devices and continuing education if you want the nurse to take on new procedures

FAQ

How much does a nurse earn in Dubai per month?

Market estimate: a registered nurse in a clinic earns about 7,000 - 12,000 AED per month, an aesthetic nurse about 9,000 - 14,000 AED, an IV therapy nurse about 7,000 - 11,000 AED plus bonus and a home care nurse about 6,000 - 9,000 AED. A head nurse earns about 18,000 - 25,000 AED. The exact figure depends on the DHA title, experience, languages and the facility.

Which nurses earn the most in Dubai?

Market estimate: head nurses and nurse managers at about 18,000 - 25,000 AED per month, senior nurse injectors with their own client base at about 14,000 - 20,000 AED often as a fixed base plus commission, and specialised theatre or day-surgery nurses at about 12,000 - 18,000 AED.

Does a small clinic need a registered nurse or is an assistant nurse enough?

It depends on the services and on the current DHA rules for your facility. A registered nurse works independently within scope; an assistant nurse works under registered nurse supervision. Check the rules for each service and decide with your medical director before you plan the schedule.
