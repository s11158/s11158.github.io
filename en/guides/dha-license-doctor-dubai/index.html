DHA licensing when hiring a doctor in Dubai: what owners need to know

A short breakdown for clinic owners and managers: what a DHA licence is, how it affects your launch timeline and budget, and where weeks are most often lost.

You've found a good doctor — but before they can see patients in your Dubai clinic, there's one thing that quietly breaks timelines: the DHA licence. Formally it sits "with the doctor", but in practice it directly concerns you as the owner. Let's be honest about it.

Why it's your concern

In Dubai a doctor can't legally practise without an individual professional licence from the Dubai Health Authority (DHA) and without linking that licence to your licensed facility. What that means for you:

  • Hiring "an experienced doctor" without an active licence means they can't start right away.
  • Seeing patients without linking to your clinic's licence risks fines and regulator issues.
  • Unplanned processing time easily pushes your launch or capacity back by weeks.

The path to a licence — 4 stages

1

3
DHA licence issuedThe doctor gains the right to practise in Dubai in their specialty.
4
Linking to your clinicThe licence is activated under your facility's licence — only then can the doctor legally see patients.

How long it takes — and how it affects your plan

This is where the main fork for an owner lies:

  • A candidate with an active DHA licence can start quickly, in around two weeks (mostly facility linking left).
  • A candidate without a licence means several weeks, sometimes months for DataFlow, the exam and processing. A great doctor, but not "tomorrow".

The takeaway is simple: if the launch or replacement is urgent, prioritise candidates with an active licence. If you have time, you can take a strong doctor and guide them through the process.

Where owners most often lose time

  • Making an offer without checking licence status first — then learning about months of processing.
  • Assuming "any experienced doctor starts immediately" — and not budgeting for DataFlow.
  • Not matching the licensed specialty with what the doctor will actually do.
  • Hiring without knowing who will run the licensing.
Note: exact requirements depend on the specialty, country of the degree and the candidate's experience, and DHA rules change periodically. Abu Dhabi uses DOH (formerly HAAD), the federal level MOHAP; each emirate has its nuances. We always confirm the specifics for your case.
