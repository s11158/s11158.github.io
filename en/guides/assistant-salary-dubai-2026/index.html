Assistant salary in Dubai market estimate by assistant type, 2026

"Assistant" in Dubai covers very different jobs: a person who keeps the office diary and the paperwork, a personal assistant who runs an owner's day, an executive assistant to a director, a family office assistant who handles private matters, a virtual assistant working remotely, and an assistant to a clinic owner. They are paid very differently. This guide gives market estimates in AED per month for each type, explains what moves the number and how to read a package, so you can set a realistic budget before you start the search.

What moves the number

Two assistants with the same title can differ by 50% or more. These factors usually decide where a person sits in the range.

  • Who they support: an assistant to the owner of a family business is paid for trust and availability, not for typing speed
  • Languages: English at business level is the base; Arabic for government and Arabic-speaking contacts, or Russian for Russian-speaking owners and clients, usually adds to the offer
  • Hours and availability: evening calls, travel with the boss and weekend cover raise the package; a strict office day lowers it
  • Scope: one person who is assistant, PRO support and basic bookkeeping coordination costs more than a pure diary assistant, but less than three hires
  • Systems: calendar and email management, Zoho, Notion, Excel, expense and booking tools; a person who can run them from day one sits higher
  • Sector: clinics, real estate, finance and family offices often pay more than small trading or retail companies

Package versus basic salary

In the UAE the package is usually split into basic salary and allowances such as housing and transport. The split matters for the employer, because end-of-service gratuity is calculated from basic salary only.

  • Write basic salary, each allowance and any bonus as separate lines in the offer and the contract
  • Ask the candidate for the current package split the same way, so you compare like with like
  • A very low basic next to large allowances is noticed by candidates and lowers trust in the offer
  • Commission is rare for assistants; if you pay a bonus, describe the rule in writing
  • On top of the package the employer pays visa and Emirates ID, health insurance and a gratuity provision, see our cost of hiring guide

Where a virtual assistant fits in the comparison

A virtual assistant is not a cheaper copy of an office assistant. The scope is narrower: tasks that do not need a physical presence. A remote assistant is often paid by fixed monthly hours or by task, so compare the cost per useful hour, not the monthly number alone.

  • Good fit: diary and inbox management, travel booking, research, social media scheduling, supplier follow-ups
  • Poor fit: receiving guests, handling originals and parcels, anything that needs to be in the room
  • Before you hire, check the working arrangement for a remote person with the right authority; this guide does not replace that check

Consistency with office salaries

If you hire several office roles, keep the grades coherent. An administrative assistant sits next to an office administrator, an executive assistant above an office manager in trust level but in a similar money range, and a family office assistant at the top. Paying an assistant far above the office manager they report to usually creates friction, so decide the structure first and the numbers second.

  • Office administrator / coordinator: about 5,000 - 8,000 AED
  • Office manager: about 8,000 - 14,000 AED
  • Executive assistant: about 12,000 - 20,000 AED

FAQ

How much does a personal assistant earn in Dubai?

Market estimate: about 5,000 - 7,000 AED per month at entry level, 8,000 - 10,000 AED for an experienced personal assistant and 12,000 AED and above for a senior one. An executive assistant to an owner or director is usually 12,000 - 20,000 AED, and 20,000 - 30,000 AED for a C-level or family office role.

Is a virtual assistant cheaper than an office assistant in Dubai?

Often the monthly number is lower, as an orientation 3,500 - 8,000 AED depending on hours, but the scope is narrower because a remote person cannot receive guests or handle originals. Compare the cost per useful hour and check the working arrangement before you decide.
