DHA licence for nurses step by step

For nurses coming to Dubai the licence is the gate. Here is the path most candidates follow, and how to shorten it.

Talk to us

The steps

  • Check eligibility for your category on the DHA portal (Sheryan)
  • Primary source verification through DataFlow: degree, licence, experience
  • The DHA nursing exam (Prometric)
  • Eligibility letter once the file and exam are cleared
  • Job offer from a DHA-licensed facility, which activates the licence

What usually slows it down

  • Experience letters without exact dates and duties
  • Gaps in recent practice
  • Documents in a language other than English without translation

Tips

  • Prepare experience letters before you start DataFlow
  • Book the exam early; some months are busy
  • Send your CV to Dubai clinics as soon as you have the eligibility letter

Free for candidates

TLNT never charges candidates. Send your CV and we will match you with clinics that hire licensed or eligible nurses.

FAQ

How long does it take to get a DHA licence as a nurse?

Usually a few months from starting DataFlow to the eligibility letter, depending on document checks and exam dates.

Do I need a job offer before the DHA licence?

You can obtain eligibility first; the licence is activated when a DHA-licensed facility hires you.

Does TLNT charge nurses?

No, it is free for candidates.
