Licensed or not: who to hire for your timeline

A simple fork that decides when the doctor starts and how much choice you'll have.

When hiring a doctor in Dubai you almost always choose between two candidate types. Each has its own logic, and it comes down to what matters more: speed or breadth of choice.

Candidate with an active DHA licence

  • Pro: starts fast — around two weeks (just facility linking left)
  • Con: fewer of them, and pricier
  • For whom: when the launch or replacement is urgent

Strong unlicensed candidate

  • Pro: wider choice, you can take a very strong doctor
  • Con: weeks for DataFlow, exam and paperwork
  • For whom: when you have time and quality matters
How to decide: start with the timeline. Urgent → licensed. A month or two to spare → take the strong one and guide the paperwork. We'll help weigh it for your case.
