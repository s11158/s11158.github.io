Beauty salon salaries in Dubai by role, 2026

A salon owner in Dubai usually pays a base salary plus commission, and the base differs a lot between roles. This guide puts the main salon roles side by side as market estimates in AED per month, explains what moves the number and what the employer pays on top of the salary.

Base plus commission: how the pay is usually built

Most masters in Dubai salons are not paid a flat salary only. A base covers the person's living costs and commission rewards the work they bring in. The split is the owner's decision and should be written in the contract before the first day.

  • A lower base with a higher commission suits an experienced master with a client following and carries more risk for the master in a quiet month
  • A higher base with a lower commission suits a new master who is building a client list
  • Commission can be tied to services, to retail sales of care products or to both; agree the rule in writing
  • A fixed minimum base for the first months helps a newcomer settle without turning the base into the main cost
  • More on structuring plans in our guide on commission plans for salon and clinic staff

What moves the salary of a master

Two masters with the same title can sit several thousand dirhams apart. These are the usual reasons.

  • Experience in Dubai or the Gulf and the level of the salons worked in
  • A personal client base and a booking calendar that fills without the salon's help
  • Narrow skills with demand: colour correction, extensions, bridal makeup, lash lamination, laser devices
  • Languages: Arabic for local clients, Russian for the Russian-speaking community, English as a base
  • Portfolio and reviews that the salon can use in its own marketing
  • Housing, transport and flights: some salons add an allowance or provide housing, which changes the comparison between offers

Front desk and management

The front desk and the manager decide how many calls become bookings and how many clients come back, so the salary of these roles repays itself quickly when the person is good.

  • Salon receptionist or administrator: bookings, payments, confirmations and first contact with clients; pay is lower than for masters but the role shapes the client experience
  • Salon manager: schedule, stock, quality, promotions and the team; the upper part of the range fits a manager who runs several masters and watches the numbers
  • A manager can be hired or the owner can run the salon alone for a while; see our guide on whether to hire a salon manager or manage it yourself

The cost on top of salary

Salary is not the full cost of a master. Add these items when you plan the payroll and compare offers.

  • Visa and Emirates ID: paid by the employer; the amount depends on the company type and the free zone
  • Health insurance for every employee; the premium depends on the plan and the person's age
  • Gratuity provision: about 5.83% of basic salary per month in the first five years of service, see our gratuity guide
  • Annual leave of 30 calendar days after the first year and the cost of covering the master's appointments
  • Training on products and devices if you want the master to take on new services
  • Product and consumables used per service, which belong in the service price rather than in the salary

FAQ

How much does a hairdresser or nail technician earn in a Dubai salon?

Market estimate: a hair stylist earns about 4,000 - 7,500 AED per month and a colorist about 5,000 - 8,000 AED, with a senior colorist at 8,000 - 12,000 AED. A nail technician earns about 3,000 - 5,500 AED. Many roles are paid as a base plus commission, so the total depends on how many clients the master serves.

How much does an esthetician or cosmetologist earn in Dubai?

Market estimate: an esthetician in a salon earns about 4,000 - 7,500 AED per month, and a senior one can reach 9,000 AED or more. A laser technician earns about 6,000 - 9,000 AED plus commission. A licensed aesthetic physician is a different category and is paid much more, see our doctor salary guide.

Should I pay my salon masters a salary or a percentage?

Most salons combine both: a base that covers living costs and a commission on services or on retail sales. Pick the split by the master's experience and client base, and write the rule in the contract before the start date.
