Clinic manager salary in Dubai: monthly pay and full cost

How much to offer a clinic manager in Dubai, and what the role really costs the clinic each month. The ranges below are market estimates that we use when advising clinic owners; the exact figure depends on the size of the clinic and on what the manager is responsible for.

What moves the number up or down

Two managers with the same title can differ by ten thousand dirhams a month. The difference usually comes from the following.

  • Size: number of doctors, rooms and front desk staff the manager is responsible for
  • Insurance or cash: a clinic with insurer contracts, approvals and rejections needs a manager who understands billing
  • Compliance record: DHA inspections, facility and staff licence renewals they have handled themselves
  • Scope: only administration, or also revenue targets, marketing and hiring
  • Opening experience: a manager who has launched a clinic in Dubai costs more and saves months
  • Languages for your patient base: English is expected, Arabic or Russian is often a plus

What the package includes besides the basic salary

Candidates compare whole packages, not one number. These are the usual parts.

  • Basic salary and allowances (housing, transport): the split matters, because end-of-service gratuity is calculated on the basic salary
  • Bonus: usually tied to revenue or margin against plan, or to a short list of KPIs; put the formula in writing
  • Work permit, residence visa, medical test and Emirates ID: paid by the employer and cannot be deducted from the employee's pay
  • Health insurance: mandatory, paid by the employer
  • Paid annual leave: 30 calendar days after a full year of service
  • Flight ticket home: a common practice rather than a legal duty, so it depends on the contract
  • End-of-service gratuity after at least one year: 21 days of basic salary for each of the first five years and 30 days for each year after that

Full monthly cost for the employer

The salary is not the whole cost. A worked example helps to plan the budget: a manager on 20,000 AED per month, of which 12,000 AED is basic salary.

Gratuity accrues at 21 days of basic salary per year, which here is 8,400 AED a year or about 700 AED a month. On top of that come health insurance, visa and work permit costs spread over the visa term, and the bonus if targets are met. Exact visa and insurance amounts depend on the company category, the emirate and the insurance plan, so ask your PRO or accountant for the figures for your licence.

  • Fixed part: monthly package agreed in the contract
  • Accruals: end-of-service gratuity on the basic salary
  • Mandatory extras: health insurance, visa and work permit
  • Variable part: bonus on revenue, margin or KPIs
  • Hidden cost of a vacancy: the owner or the medical director running operations between patients

Clinic manager, operations manager or medical director

The title changes the pay, so decide first which role you need. A clinic manager runs the daily business of one clinic: schedules, front desk, billing oversight, staff, suppliers. An operations manager owns processes, compliance and KPIs, often across branches. A medical director is a licensed doctor responsible for clinical governance, and that pay follows the clinical title, not the management scale.

  • One clinic, owner still present: a clinic manager is usually enough
  • Insurance billing, several branches or frequent inspections: add or hire an operations manager
  • Every licensed facility needs a medical director; this role is not replaced by a manager

FAQ

How much does a clinic manager earn in Dubai per month?

Market estimate: about 12,000 - 15,000 AED per month at entry level, 18,000 - 22,000 AED for an experienced manager and 28,000 AED or more for a senior one. Operations managers of a single clinic earn about 15,000 - 22,000 AED, group operations directors 32,000 - 50,000 AED.

Is a clinic manager's salary in Dubai tax free?

There is no personal income tax on salaries in the UAE, so the manager receives the amount stated in the contract. The employer separately pays for the visa, work permit and health insurance; these costs cannot be deducted from the employee's pay.

What bonus is normal for a clinic manager in Dubai?

There is no single standard. Common models are a quarterly or annual bonus tied to revenue or margin against plan, or to a few KPIs such as utilisation, show-up rate and insurance rejections. Whatever you choose, write the formula into the contract or a bonus letter.
