Golden visa for doctors in the UAE

The UAE golden visa is a long-term residence visa that is not tied to an employer's sponsorship. Doctors are named among the eligible groups, but conditions differ by route and change over time. This page explains the logic; the decision is made by the authorities, not by agents.

Routes as published

According to the UAE government portal (u.ae), the golden visa is issued in several categories. The ones that most often matter for doctors:

  • Exceptional talents and specialised talents: doctors are explicitly listed; this route gives a 10-year visa, and requirements differ by subcategory (recommendations, experience, accredited degrees)
  • Skilled professionals: a salary-based route with conditions on education, profession and salary, published by ICP
  • Investors: for example through property or capital, with thresholds published on the official portals (the investor category starts from AED 2 million)
  • Some health regulators have nominated or recommended eligible healthcare professionals: ask DHA, DOH or MOHAP whether a route applies to you

What doctors usually prepare

Exact lists depend on the route and are set by ICP or GDRFA. Commonly relevant:

  • Valid UAE licence to practise (DHA, DOH or MOHAP)
  • Degree certificates, attested or equivalency-recognised where required
  • Proof of experience and current employment contract or salary certificate
  • Passport, current residence details and any recommendation letters the route asks for

What it changes and what it does not

  • Benefits named on u.ae: sponsoring family members and staying outside the UAE longer than the usual six months without losing residence
  • It does not replace the DHA licence: a doctor still needs an active licence linked to the facility to practise
  • The clinic still hires the doctor under an employment contract; work permit steps can differ from a standard employment visa, so check the current MOHRE procedure
  • For recruitment, a doctor on a golden visa can be easier to hire because residence does not depend on the new employer

Official channels and a warning

  • ICP (Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security): eServices and eligibility checker
  • GDRFA Dubai: golden visa services for Dubai residents
  • Amer centres in Dubai for help with the application
  • Be careful with intermediaries who guarantee approval or ask for large fees upfront: the authorities decide, and official fees are published on official channels

FAQ

Can doctors get a golden visa in the UAE?

Yes, doctors are listed among the eligible groups on the UAE government portal, mainly under the talents category with a 10-year visa. Conditions depend on the route and are set by ICP and GDRFA.

Does a golden visa let a doctor work in any clinic in Dubai?

No. The visa gives residence, but to practise the doctor still needs an active DHA licence linked to the facility and an employment arrangement with the clinic.

Where do I apply for a golden visa as a doctor?

Through ICP eServices (which also has an eligibility checker) or GDRFA Dubai, and Amer centres can help. Ask DHA whether a health-sector nomination route applies to you.
