Cost of opening a beauty salon in Dubai: the budget structure

Any single total for a Dubai salon is misleading: a 4-station nail bar in a community centre and a full-service salon in a mall differ many times over. What is useful is the list of lines, so nothing is forgotten.

Talk to us

Licence and government costs

These depend on the licensing authority, the activities, the location and the number of visas. Get a written quote from DET or the free zone, or from a setup consultant, for your exact case.

  • Trade name, initial approval and trade licence
  • Approvals from Dubai Municipality and Civil Defence, signage permit
  • Establishment card and visa quota
  • Annual renewal: it is a recurring cost, not a one-off

Premises

  • Annual rent, in Dubai often paid in a small number of cheques, which means a large payment up front
  • Security deposit and agent commission
  • Service charges, and in malls often marketing fees or a turnover component
  • Ejari registration, utilities connection and deposits
  • Rent-free fit-out period: negotiate it, it directly reduces the opening budget

Fit-out and equipment

  • Design, fit-out works and contractor approvals
  • Plumbing for wash units and pedicure chairs, ventilation, electrics for equipment
  • Stations, chairs, pedicure chairs, wash units, trolleys, storage
  • Sterilisation equipment and first stock of consumables and professional products
  • Reception, POS terminal, booking software, sound and lighting

Team before revenue

Payroll starts before the salon is full. An illustrative opening team, using the experienced level of our market estimates: 1 salon manager (12,000 - 15,000), 2 administrators (9,000 - 11,000 together), 4 nail technicians (18,000 - 22,000), 2 hair stylists (12,000 - 15,000), 1 esthetician (6,000 - 7,500) and 1 lash technician (4,000 - 6,000). That is roughly 61,000 - 76,500 AED a month in base salaries before commission, visas, insurance, health cards and any allowances. Replace it with your own team plan.

  • Visas, Emirates ID, medical tests and health insurance for each person
  • Occupational health cards
  • Recruitment fees or hiring costs
  • Training weeks before opening, when staff are paid but not yet earning

Marketing, launch and working capital

A new salon rarely fills its schedule in the first months, so the cash reserve is part of the opening budget, not an extra. A common rule of thumb is to hold enough to cover several months of fixed costs (rent, base payroll, software, marketing), and to plan it on the slow-fill scenario of your financial model.

  • Launch marketing: content, ads, Google Business Profile, opening offers
  • Software and CRM subscriptions
  • VAT registration and accounting once you reach the threshold
  • Reserve for the months before break-even

FAQ

How much does it cost to open a beauty salon in Dubai?

There is no honest single number: it depends on size, location, fit-out level and team. Build it from the lines: licence and approvals, rent and deposit, fit-out and equipment, payroll and visas before revenue, marketing and a cash reserve.

What is the biggest cost when opening a salon in Dubai?

Usually the premises (rent paid up front plus fit-out) and payroll in the first months, when the team is paid but the schedule is not yet full.

How much working capital does a new salon need?

Enough to cover fixed costs until break-even on a slow-fill scenario. Many owners plan several months of rent and base payroll as a reserve.
