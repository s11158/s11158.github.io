eClaimLink for clinic owners in Dubai: how it works and who does what

eClaimLink is where a Dubai clinic's insurance money is approved or lost. This page is for the owner, not the coder: what the platform does, what happens to one insurance visit, who in your team should own each step and which numbers to look at every week.

Talk to us

What eClaimLink is

eClaimLink is the DHA-managed electronic platform through which Dubai healthcare providers and insurers (or their TPAs) exchange eligibility checks, prior authorisation requests, claims and remittance advice. Transactions pass through the DHA Health Post Office (DHPO). Health insurance is mandatory for residents in Dubai, so for most outpatient clinics that accept insurance this is a daily working tool, not an IT detail.

Abu Dhabi and the Northern Emirates use their own systems (for example Shafafiya under DOH in Abu Dhabi), so a clinic in several emirates needs people who know each of them.

  • Who uses it: DHA-licensed facilities, insurers and TPAs
  • What passes through it: eligibility, prior authorisations, claims, resubmissions, remittances
  • What it needs: a valid DHA facility licence and contracts with the insurers you work with
  • What it does not do: it does not code the visit and does not fix weak documentation; people do

The path of one insurance visit

Every rejected claim can be traced back to one of these steps. When an owner knows the order, it is easy to see where the money leaks.

  • Before the visit: check eligibility and network for the patient's card and plan
  • Before the service: send prior authorisation for services that require it, with the diagnosis and clinical justification
  • During the visit: the doctor documents enough to support every code that will be billed
  • After the visit: diagnoses and procedures are coded (ICD-10-CM and CPT, plus the codes the payer requires) and the claim is submitted
  • Remittance: the insurer pays in full, in part or rejects, with a reason code
  • Resubmission: rejected lines are corrected and resubmitted with the missing information, within the insurer's timelines

Why claims get rejected

Most rejections come from preventable causes, and the same causes repeat. Look at rejection reasons, not just the total.

  • Eligibility not checked: the card was inactive or the service is outside the patient's plan or network
  • No prior authorisation, or the service delivered differs from what was approved
  • Coding errors: missing, wrong or mismatched diagnosis and procedure codes
  • Documentation does not support the claim: medical necessity is not visible in the notes
  • Pricing outside the agreed price list with that insurer
  • Late submission or resubmission without the information the insurer asked for

What the owner should see every week

You do not need to log in to eClaimLink yourself. Ask for a short weekly report, ideally from your CRM or billing system, with these lines.

  • Rejection rate by insurer and by reason, compared with the previous weeks
  • Rejections by doctor: shows where documentation needs a word with a specific doctor
  • Receivables by age: what the insurers owe for 30, 60 and 90+ days
  • Open prior authorisations and how long approvals take
  • Resubmissions sent and recovered, and what was written off and why

Getting set up

Registration on eClaimLink is tied to the facility licence and is done through DHA; the exact steps, documents and any fees should be checked with DHA and your insurers for your case. Before the first insurance patient, make sure the following is in place.

  • A valid DHA facility licence and the contracts and price lists agreed with each insurer or TPA
  • Access to eClaimLink and DHPO, and a practice management or billing system that connects to it
  • At least one person with Dubai eClaimLink experience, and a coder or a coding service for the volume you expect
  • Clinic rules for eligibility checks at reception and prior authorisation before services
  • A weekly rejection and receivables report agreed with the clinic manager

FAQ

What is eClaimLink in Dubai?

The DHA-managed electronic platform through which Dubai healthcare providers and insurers exchange eligibility checks, prior authorisations, claims and remittances, with transactions passing through the DHA Health Post Office (DHPO). A valid DHA facility licence is needed to register.

Who in a clinic should work in eClaimLink?

Usually an insurance officer handles prior authorisations, remittances and resubmissions, and a medical coder codes the visits and submits claims. Reception checks eligibility before the visit, doctors document the services, and the clinic manager reviews rejections and receivables every week. In a small clinic one person may combine the officer and coder roles.

Why are claims rejected in eClaimLink?

Most often because eligibility was not checked, prior authorisation was missing or did not match the service, codes were wrong or missing, documentation did not support the claim, or the price was outside the agreed list. Track rejections by reason and by doctor and fix the step where they start.
