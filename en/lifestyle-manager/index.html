Hire a lifestyle manager in Dubai

Lifestyle managers and personal concierges for high-net-worth individuals and busy families in Dubai. The role is the private side of life done well: travel, restaurants and events, gifts, memberships and clubs, the admin behind property and cars, medical appointments and the hundred small things that otherwise eat the owner's evenings. Unlike a PA, the lifestyle manager rarely touches the business. We assess taste, network, discretion and the ability to deliver on a Friday night, not only to plan.

Who we help you hire

A lifestyle manager who gets the table, the flight and the specialist appointment when it looks impossible, remembers every preference and never talks about the family outside the house.

  • Travel: itineraries, private and commercial flights, hotels and villas, visas, transfers, a plan B when the plan changes mid-trip
  • Restaurants, events and tickets: bookings in Dubai and abroad, hosting at home, concerts, sport, school and charity events
  • Gifts and occasions: a calendar of birthdays and anniversaries, gifts chosen and delivered, thank-you notes sent
  • Memberships and clubs: golf, beach and private clubs, gyms, loyalty programmes, renewals and benefits actually used
  • Property and cars: registration and insurance renewals, service, Salik and fines, tenancy and community paperwork, coordination with the house manager or housekeeper
  • Medical and personal appointments: specialists, dental, wellness, school meetings; a preferences file for every family member (allergies, sizes, favourite seats, dislikes)

What to check before hiring

Lifestyle manager is not a licensed role. The person will know where the family is, what they spend and whom they see, so the checks are about discretion, references and real delivery.

1
Impossible-request task: a realistic brief: a table for eight at a fully booked restaurant tomorrow, a last-minute flight change for the family and a gift for a business partner whose tastes are unknown. The candidate explains step by step how they would get each done, whom they would call and when they would tell you it cannot be done.
2
Preferences and detail check: we give a short family profile and ask the candidate to plan a weekend trip. We look for the details: allergies respected, flight times that work for children, a back-up hotel, costs shown clearly.
3
Confidentiality and spending scenario: a journalist or an acquaintance asks where the family will spend the holidays; a supplier offers the candidate a commission for bringing your business. We listen for a clear refusal in both cases and ask how the candidate reports spending.
4
References, background, access and probation: calls to previous principals or families; a background check within what the law allows; a written confidentiality clause; access rules for cards, accounts and travel documents with limits and receipts; a three-month probation with agreed deliverables (preferences file, renewals calendar, first trips). Own visa, transferable or new sponsorship (the employer sponsors the work permit and residence visa).

How it differs from neighbouring roles. A personal assistant covers diary and errands and often some business admin. A family office assistant handles the family's documents, schools and staff with a strong admin component. A house manager runs the home and its staff. The lifestyle manager owns the private calendar of experiences and the admin around it: travel, dining, events, gifts, memberships, cars, property paperwork and appointments. Hire one when the family travels and entertains a lot and the owner wants nothing to do with the arrangements. Common hiring mistakes: hiring a hotel concierge who can book but cannot run a household budget or renewals; hiring someone with a network but no discipline on receipts; no clear line between the lifestyle manager and the house manager, so both wait for the other.

FAQ

How much does a lifestyle manager or personal concierge earn in Dubai?

Orientation, market estimate: about 10,000 - 18,000 AED per month total package for a lifestyle manager or personal concierge, and 18,000 - 25,000 AED for a lifestyle manager to a very wealthy family with frequent travel and events. The employer also pays visa, health insurance and gratuity provision.

What is the difference between a lifestyle manager and a personal assistant?

A personal assistant looks after one person's diary and errands and often helps with business admin. A lifestyle manager works only on the private side: travel, dining, events, gifts, memberships, cars, property paperwork and appointments for the whole family, with a network of suppliers and a file of preferences. If you need both business and private support in one person, look at the hybrid assistant role.
