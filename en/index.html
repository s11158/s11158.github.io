FAQ

Common questions

How much does it cost?

One month's salary of the person we place. 50% upfront and 50% once they start. 15% off your first order, up to 20% for several vacancies.

What's covered by the guarantee?

If within two months the hire isn't a fit, we find a replacement free of charge. The guarantee applies to every placed role.

Do you only do sourcing?

No. We also help with the contract, salary and motivation, the onboarding plan, and adaptation. All turnkey.

We're a small business — is this for us?

Absolutely. Most of our clients are salons, clinics, galleries, retail and families with no in-house HR. You describe the task, we handle the rest.

How fast can you fill a role?

On average around two weeks. Simpler roles faster; complex roles with qualification checks a little longer. We give an honest timeline on the call.
