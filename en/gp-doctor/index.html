Licensing in Dubai (DHA)

To practise legally in Dubai a doctor needs a Dubai Health Authority (DHA) licence. We know the path and take it off your plate.

1
DataFlow (PSV) — primary source verification of degree, licences and experience.
2
DHA assessment — specialty exam / evaluation (for some candidates).
3
DHA licence — the right to practise in Dubai is issued.
4
Facility linking — the doctor is registered under your clinic's licence.

We place candidates who already hold a DHA licence — or guide the process so you don't have to navigate it yourself. Abu Dhabi uses DOH/HAAD, the federal level MOHAP; for other emirates we account for their requirements.
