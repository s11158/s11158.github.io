Who we work with

We work with companies outside the UAE and with intermediaries who have clients that need staff here.

  • Recruitment and HR firms in other countries whose clients are opening or growing in the UAE
  • Relocation, business set-up and immigration consultants who help clients move to Dubai
  • Clinic, salon, restaurant and retail brands entering the UAE, including franchises

What we do for your clients

We do the part that needs local presence: knowing the market, finding people who are actually in the UAE or ready to move, and checking them in person.

  • Search in our database of 10,000+ specialist profiles in the UAE and on the open market
  • Assessment of every finalist, including a practical task on the client's own case
  • First shortlist in 2 days on average for most roles
  • Local salary benchmarks for 130 roles from the Dubai Salary Index 2026
  • A clear checklist of what the employer needs for the visa and the employment contract
  • Russian and English, calls across time zones, every day 08:00-21:30 Dubai time

Roles we cover

Medical and aesthetic staff (doctors, nurses, cosmetologists, clinic managers), beauty and spa specialists, hotel and restaurant teams, office and sales roles, personal and executive assistants, and private household staff such as nannies, governesses, housekeepers, drivers and chefs.

Two ways to work together

Start with one role and see how we work, or agree a long-term framework from the beginning.

  • One-off request: you send the brief, we agree a fixed fee for the role before the search starts and work on our standard terms
  • Long-term partnership: a written agreement for partners who bring clients regularly, with partner terms, the role of each side, confidentiality and how we communicate with your clients agreed individually

How a project runs

  • An introductory call with you, and with your client if you wish
  • A brief for each role, built around the real working day
  • A fixed fee agreed before the search starts
  • A shortlist with our assessment notes
  • Remote interviews; the employer makes the hiring decision and agrees the terms directly with the candidate
  • Support until the person starts work

FAQ

Can you help a client that has no company in the UAE yet?

Yes, we can start the search early. The person is employed by a company licensed in the UAE, either the client's own entity or another legal arrangement the client chooses with its advisers, and that employer arranges the visa. We give a clear checklist of what is needed.

How is a partner rewarded?

Partner terms are agreed individually and written into the partnership agreement, depending on the number of roles and how we share the work with you.

Do you work with partners outside the Russian-speaking market?

Yes. We work in Russian and English and take calls across time zones, every day 08:00-21:30 Dubai time.
