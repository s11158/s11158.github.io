Hire a house manager in Dubai

House managers (household managers) for villas and large family homes in Dubai. The house manager runs the home as a small operation: the household staff and their schedules, suppliers and contractors, maintenance and the pool, the household budget, inventories and the preparation for guests. The owner stops being the person who knows where the spare keys are and why the air conditioning contractor has not come. We assess candidates on how they run a home, not on how they clean one.

Who we help you hire

A house manager who keeps the villa, its staff and its suppliers working quietly, with a budget the owner can read and a home that is ready for guests at short notice.

  • Household staff: hiring support, schedules, rotas and leave for the housekeeper, nanny, driver, chef and gardener; daily standards and gentle correction
  • Suppliers and contractors: maintenance, air conditioning, pool, garden, pest control, cleaning of facades and tanks; quotes compared, work supervised, access controlled
  • Maintenance calendar: a planned schedule for the villa's systems, community and DEWA matters, warranties and service contracts, snagging after works
  • Budget and records: monthly household budget, petty cash, receipts and a simple report; purchases within agreed limits
  • Inventories: linen, tableware, wine, art and valuables, consumables; stock levels and a reorder rule; keys and access cards
  • Guests and events: preparing rooms, catering with the chef, extra staff for events, arrival and departure logistics; security and privacy of the family

What to check before hiring

House manager is not a licensed role. The person has the keys to the home and manages the people inside it, so the checks are about management, honesty and references.

1
Villa walk-through case: a floor plan and a short description of a home with five staff: the candidate lists what they would check in the first week, which rotas, inventories and supplier contracts they would set up and what they would report to the owner at the end of the month.
2
Supplier and budget task: three quotes for the same repair, one obviously padded, and a monthly budget sheet with gaps. The candidate chooses, explains, fills the gaps and says how they would prevent the next overspend.
3
Staff conflict scenario: the housekeeper and the nanny disagree about duties, and the driver has been late twice. The candidate describes the conversations they would have and what they would bring to the owner. Managing through people, not complaining to the owner.
4
References, background, access and probation: calls to previous principals, families or estate managers; a background check within what the law allows; a written confidentiality clause; access rules for keys, alarm codes, cards and petty cash with limits and receipts; a three-month probation with agreed deliverables (staff rotas, maintenance calendar, inventories, first budget report). Own visa, transferable or new sponsorship (the employer sponsors the work permit and residence visa).

How it differs from neighbouring roles. A housekeeper cleans and runs the laundry and the kitchen and may supervise one or two helpers; a house manager manages the whole household team, the suppliers and the budget and usually does little hands-on work. A lifestyle manager runs the family's travel, events and private admin, not the home. A family office assistant handles the family's documents and coordination from an office. Hire a house manager when the home has three or more staff, regular contractors and guests, or when there are several properties. Common hiring mistakes: promoting a good housekeeper into the role without management skills or budget discipline; no written authority over the other staff, so nobody listens; no inventories at the start, so losses cannot be seen later.

FAQ

How much does a house manager earn in Dubai?

Orientation, market estimate: about 10,000 - 20,000 AED per month total package for a house manager, with 10,000 - 15,000 AED for a villa with three to five staff and 15,000 - 20,000 AED for several properties or a large team. The employer also pays visa, health insurance and gratuity provision; live-in roles often include accommodation.

What is the difference between a house manager and a housekeeper?

A housekeeper does the cleaning, laundry and kitchen and may supervise one or two helpers. A house manager manages the whole household: staff rotas, suppliers and contractors, maintenance, budget, inventories and guests, and does little hands-on work. If the home has up to two staff and no regular contractors, a senior housekeeper is usually enough; with three or more staff, several properties or frequent guests, hire a house manager.
