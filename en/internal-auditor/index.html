Hire an internal auditor in Dubai

Internal auditors for family businesses, holding groups, trading companies and retail, clinic or salon groups in Dubai. The internal auditor works for the owner or the board, not for the finance team: they test whether processes and controls work in reality, find leakage in stock, cash, discounts and purchasing, and write findings that management can act on. In a family business with several companies and managers the owner rarely sees day to day, this is often the owner's most direct view of what really happens. We assess candidates on a real audit finding write-up.

Who we help you hire

An internal auditor who finds the real issue behind a control failure, writes it in a way a busy owner reads to the end and follows up until it is fixed.

  • Audit plan: a yearly plan by risk across companies, branches and processes, agreed with the owner or the audit committee
  • Process reviews: purchasing and supplier selection, sales and discounts, stock and warehouses, payroll, petty cash, cash handling at branches and clinics
  • Testing: samples of transactions, surprise stock and cash counts, system access and approvals, reconciliation of POS to bank
  • Findings and reports: each finding with what was found, why it matters in money or risk, the root cause and an agreed action with an owner and a date
  • Follow-up: tracking open actions, retesting, a short status for the owner every quarter
  • Special reviews: suspected fraud or conflict of interest, a new acquisition, a branch that underperforms without an obvious reason

What to check before hiring

Many candidates come from external audit and know the methodology, but have not worked inside a business where findings land on people they see every day. Test judgment, writing and tact.

1
Audit finding write-up: we give the candidate test results from a purchasing review: one supplier always wins, quotes are missing, approvals come after the invoice. The candidate writes one finding with condition, impact, root cause and a practical action, in plain English, on half a page.
2
Process walk-through case: a description of how a salon or clinic group handles cash, discounts and stock. The candidate designs five tests they would run in the first month and explains why.
3
Stakeholder scenario: a branch manager who is a relative of the owner disagrees with a finding and asks to remove it. We listen to how the candidate keeps independence without starting a war.
4
Structured reference calls: calls to a previous head of audit, CFO or owner: quality of findings, follow-through, discretion.

How it differs from neighbouring roles. The external auditor signs the annual financial statements; the internal auditor works all year on processes and controls for the owner. A financial controller designs and runs controls inside finance; the internal auditor tests them independently and should not report to the person being audited. Typical hiring mistake: putting the internal auditor under the finance manager, so finance findings never reach the owner; or hiring a pure accountant who checks invoices but does not look at processes, stock and people.

FAQ

How much does an internal auditor earn in Dubai?

Market estimate for 2026: about 9,000 - 13,000 AED per month for an internal auditor, 13,000 - 20,000 AED for a senior internal auditor and 20,000 - 30,000 AED for an internal audit manager or head of audit. Allowances and a bonus change the full package.

Does a family business in Dubai need an internal auditor?

It is worth it when the owner can no longer see every branch, warehouse or company personally: several entities, stock, cash at branches, many suppliers, managers who are relatives or long-time staff. A smaller business can start with a periodic external review and hire an in-house internal auditor when the group grows. Whatever you choose, the auditor should report to the owner or the board, not to finance.
