What it is

One goal, several different technologies

“Body contouring” is not a single procedure. It is a set of methods that solve different problems — and they are often combined.

Loose skin, stubborn fat and weak muscle tone are three separate issues, and no single device fixes all three well. That is why the plan starts with a diagnosis, not with a machine: the doctor establishes what actually needs changing, and only then selects the technology — or a combination of them.

All the methods below are non-invasive: no incisions, no anaesthesia and no recovery period. You return to your usual routine the same day.

Suitability

Indications and contraindications

Body contouring is a medical procedure, not a spa treatment — which means it is not right for everyone.

This is for you if you have

— Localised fat deposits on the abdomen, flanks, thighs, knees or arms
— Cellulite at any stage, uneven skin texture
— Loss of skin tone after weight loss, pregnancy or with age
— Weak core, abdominal or gluteal muscles, including after childbirth
— Swelling and a “heavy legs” feeling, poor lymphatic drainage
— Unclear body contours you want sharpened without surgery
— Preparation for, or recovery after, invasive procedures

We will not treat you if there is

— Pregnancy or breastfeeding (some drainage programmes by doctor's decision only)
— Oncological disease or serious systemic illness
— A pacemaker, metal implants or electronic devices in the body
— Epilepsy, thrombosis, phlebitis, active varicose veins
— Acute inflammation, infection or skin damage in the treatment area
— Decompensated diabetes

Contraindications differ slightly between platforms — the full list for your chosen protocol is checked by the doctor at the consultation.

Honest answers

Questions people actually ask

How much does body contouring cost in Dubai?

At SOLAR one zone starts from 1,500 AED and the “abdomen + flanks” combination from 3,500 AED. Course programmes on Tesla Former, Icoone and Beautylizer are priced after the consultation, because the number of sessions depends on the zone and your starting point. The consultation itself is free.

Will body contouring make me lose weight?

No, and we would rather say so upfront. These methods change your shape — they reduce local fat deposits, tighten skin and build muscle tone. They are not a substitute for weight loss, and they work best when your weight is already stable.

Which method do I need?

That is exactly what the consultation decides. Loose skin, fat and weak muscle are three different problems: Ulfit and Accent Prime work on fat and skin, Tesla Former on muscle, Icoone and Beautylizer on lymphatic drainage and skin quality. Most plans combine two or three.

Does it hurt?

No. Tesla Former is completely painless — you feel intense muscle contractions. Icoone and Beautylizer feel like a deep massage. Accent Prime and Ulfit give a sensation of warmth. None of them require anaesthesia.

Is there downtime?

None. All the methods are non-invasive — no incisions, no anaesthesia, no recovery period. You can return to work and normal activity the same day.

How many sessions will I need?

Body methods work cumulatively, so a course is the norm rather than a one-off. The exact number depends on the method and your starting point, and the doctor confirms it at the consultation.

Is it safe for darker or tanned skin?

The methods on this page are not light-based fat treatments that depend on melanin, so tone is not the limiting factor it is with some lasers. As with any procedure, there are contraindications — which is why a consultation is required.

Is it a replacement for liposuction?

It is a non-surgical alternative, not an equivalent. Ulfit is positioned as an alternative to liposuction and facelift for people who do not want surgery: the change is more gradual and less dramatic, but without the risks and the recovery.

How soon will I see results, and how long do they last?

It depends on the platform. Accent Prime and Beautylizer often show a visible change after the first one or two sessions, Tesla Former after two or three, and Ulfit gives a light tightening straight away with the maximum effect at one to two months as collagen builds. Fat cells destroyed by ultrasound do not come back, so the volume result holds as long as your weight and activity stay stable; muscle tone and drainage effects are kept up with maintenance sessions.

Can it be done after childbirth?

Yes, once you are no longer breastfeeding and your doctor has no objection. Tesla Former in particular is used to restore abdominal and pelvic floor tone after pregnancy, and Icoone for post-pregnancy skin tone and drainage. Your individual situation is checked at the consultation.

What should I do after a session?

Nothing complicated, and there are no restrictions. We ask you to drink plenty of water and keep light activity — walking or swimming — for a day or two, so the body clears the broken-down fat efficiently.
