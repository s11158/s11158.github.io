Who we are

One clinic for your whole aesthetic routine

SOLAR Beauty & Aesthetics is a doctor-led aesthetic clinic in Sobha Hartland, Dubai. Rather than a single machine or a single treatment, we take a 360° approach — combining advanced hardware technologies, injectables and premium salon care so your face and body are cared for in one place, by people who plan the result together.

That means no bouncing between salons for lifting, skin, hair and injectables. You get one considered plan, one team, and honest advice on what will actually move the needle for you — with an option to be cared for at the clinic or, for selected treatments, at home across Dubai.

What we do

Our services

Explore each treatment on its own page, or message us and the doctor will build a personalised plan across several of them.

SMAS lifting (Ultraformer MPT)

Non-surgical lifting and tightening of the face and neck, working at the deeper SMAS layer for a firmer, more defined contour.

RF microneedling (Virtue RF)

Radiofrequency microneedling to smooth texture, tighten skin and improve pores and scarring, with minimal downtime.

Laser hair removal

Medical laser hair removal with settings matched to your skin phototype — safe and effective on tanned and darker skin.

HydraFacial

Deep cleansing, exfoliation and hydration in one gentle session for fresh, glowing skin with no downtime.

Botox & anti-wrinkle injections

Doctor-administered anti-wrinkle injections to soften expression lines and refresh the face, tailored to your features.

LED / Heleo4 light therapy

Light therapy to calm, brighten and support the skin — a gentle add-on that pairs well with other treatments.

What treatments do you offer?

SOLAR is a 360° face & body clinic. Each treatment has its own page above, and the doctor builds a personalised plan for you at a free consultation.

Do I need to be an existing patient, and where are you located?

No — new visitors are always welcome. We are in Sobha Hartland (Sobha Hartland Waves), Dubai, about 10 minutes from Business Bay and close to Downtown, Ras Al Khor, Meydan and Jumeirah. Just message us on WhatsApp to arrange a free consultation.

Is there a doctor on site?

Yes. SOLAR is a doctor-led, licensed aesthetic clinic — not a back-room salon. A qualified specialist assesses your skin, plans your treatment and stands behind every session.

Do you offer home visits?

Yes. Alongside the clinic we offer a genuine at-home and mobile service across Dubai for selected treatments, so you can be cared for in the comfort of your own home. Ask us on WhatsApp which treatments are available as a home visit.
