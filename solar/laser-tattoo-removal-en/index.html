The method

What laser tattoo removal is

A laser sends very short, high-energy pulses into the skin. The ink particles absorb that energy, heat up and shatter; the surrounding skin, which does not absorb the same wavelength, is left largely untouched. Over the following weeks your immune system removes the fragments, and the tattoo fades. That is why tattoo laser treatment is always a course: each session removes a share of the pigment, not all of it.

1

The laser targets the pigment, not the skin

Different wavelengths are absorbed by different ink colours, which is why the settings change from tattoo to tattoo and sometimes within one tattoo.

2

Your body does the removal

The laser only fragments the ink. The fading you see between visits is your lymphatic system clearing those fragments, and it takes weeks.

3

The tattoo fades in layers

Amateur tattoos with shallow, sparse ink usually clear faster than dense professional work with layered colour.

How many sessions and why

A typical tattoo needs 5 to 10 sessions, sometimes more. Each pass fragments only the pigment the laser can reach; the layer underneath becomes reachable once the top one is cleared. Nobody can promise a clean result in one visit, and a clinic that does is selling you the first session, not the outcome.

Colour and ink density decide the pace

Black pigment absorbs almost every wavelength and leaves the fastest. Blue and green are slower. Red, yellow, white and pastel shades are the hardest, and white ink can darken instead of fading. Complete removal without a trace is not always achievable - the honest answer is sometimes significant lightening, not disappearance.

Intervals of 6-8 weeks, and why they cannot be shortened

The fading happens between visits, not during them. Your lymphatic system needs those weeks to clear the fragmented ink, and the skin needs them to recover fully. Coming back sooner does not speed anything up and raises the risk of blistering, prolonged redness and changes in skin tone.

Sun protection between sessions, critical in Dubai

The treated area must stay covered and out of direct sun, with SPF 50 reapplied through the day. Tanned or sun-irritated skin changes how it absorbs the laser, forces us to lower the settings or postpone the session, and increases the risk of pigmentation.

Evidence base

What the research says

Laser tattoo removal is a well-studied method with decades of clinical use behind it.

Nano vs pico

Comparative studies

Controlled comparisons of Q-switched nanosecond lasers and newer picosecond systems report that both clear tattoo ink effectively, with picosecond devices generally reaching a comparable result in fewer sessions for some colours. The difference is in the number of visits rather than in whether removal is possible.

Colour

Clearance studies

Prospective series consistently find black and dark blue ink clearing fastest, green and red slower, and complete clearance not achieved in every case, with the number of sessions varying widely by tattoo type, ink density and skin phototype.

Safety

Reviews of adverse effects

Reviews describe most side effects as temporary (redness, swelling, blistering, crusting) and identify sun exposure, incorrect settings and short intervals as the main factors behind pigment changes and textural scarring.

The studies refer to the method (laser tattoo removal), not to a specific clinic. Results are individual and depend on the ink, its depth and age, and your skin type.

FAQ

Honest answers

How many sessions will I need?

Usually 5 to 10, with 6-8 weeks between them. Amateur and faded tattoos can take fewer, dense coloured professional work can take more. At the consultation you get an estimated range for your specific tattoo, and it is updated as we see how your ink responds.

Does it hurt, and is there anaesthesia?

It is uncomfortable: clients describe short snaps, like an elastic band against the skin. Sessions are brief, cooling is used throughout, and a topical anaesthetic cream can be applied beforehand for larger or more sensitive areas.

Will there be a scar or a mark?

The laser is designed to work through intact skin, and scarring is uncommon when the intervals are respected and the aftercare is followed. Temporary lightening or darkening of the skin can occur and usually settles over months. Picking crusts and sun exposure are the two things that genuinely cause marks.

How much does laser tattoo removal cost?

The price depends on the size, the colours and the number of sessions your tattoo needs. The doctor estimates the course at the free consultation and the price is agreed with you before the first session, with no hidden extras.

What should I avoid between sessions?

Sun and tanning on the area, sauna, hammam, pool and sea until the skin has healed, scrubs and aggressive cosmetics on the site, and picking the crusts. Keep SPF 50 on the area for the whole course - in Dubai this is what decides how smoothly the course goes.
