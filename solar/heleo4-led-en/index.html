The method

What LED and photodynamic therapy are

LED therapy treats the skin with light at precisely defined wavelengths. It works through photobiomodulation: photons are absorbed by the mitochondria of skin cells and boost the production of ATP — the cell's fuel. Cells then repair, divide and synthesize collagen more actively. It isn't heat or damage, but a gentle biological signal to renew.

Combined with photosensitizing gels, the HELEO PRO LED device performs true photodynamic therapy (PDT): the gel makes the skin more responsive to light, so the anti-inflammatory, antibacterial and rejuvenating effect goes deeper and more targeted. That's why HELEO4 works where home LED masks can't — on acne, rosacea, post-acne and visible dullness.

How light treats acne

The 415 nm blue spectrum excites porphyrins inside acne bacteria, leading to their destruction and reduced inflammation, while the photosensitizing gel amplifies the effect. No antibiotics and no aggressive impact on the skin.

How light rejuvenates

The 633 nm red spectrum reaches the dermis and activates fibroblasts, raising collagen and elastin synthesis, while 830 nm near-infrared improves microcirculation and speeds regeneration — skin becomes denser, smoother and more radiant.

How is HELEO4 LED therapy different from a home LED mask?

HELEO PRO LED is a medical-grade device with high power and precisely calibrated wavelengths (415/633/830 nm, up to 6). Paired with photosensitizing gels it performs true photodynamic therapy, not just surface lighting. That's why it treats acne, rosacea and photoageing, while a mask gives only a light cosmetic effect.

Does it hurt?

No. The treatment is completely painless and needle-free — you only feel gentle warmth as you relax under the light panel. It suits even sensitive skin.

When will I see results?

Freshness and glow are visible after the first session. For acne, rosacea and ageing concerns a course is needed — the effect builds cumulatively from session to session.

Is it safe for darker and tanned skin?

Yes. LED light contains no ultraviolet, so it does not cause pigmentation and is safe for every skin tone, all year round — which matters especially in Dubai.

Can it be combined with other procedures?

Yes, and it's recommended. LED therapy speeds recovery and enhances results after SMAS lifting, RF microneedling, lasers, peels and injectables.

How many sessions are needed?

A single session is enough for glow. For acne, rosacea or rejuvenation a course of 5–10 sessions is usually prescribed — the doctor confirms the exact plan at consultation.
