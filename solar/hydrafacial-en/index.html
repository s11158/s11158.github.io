FAQ

Honest answers

Is it suitable for sensitive skin?

Yes, it's gentle with no aggressive mechanics; intensity and ingredients are tailored to you.

Is there downtime?

None. A light flush may last a few minutes; makeup can go on right away.

How often should I do it?

One-off before an event, or a course every 3–4 weeks for lasting skin quality and glow.

How is it different from a regular facial?

HydraFacial combines cleansing, peeling, extraction and hydration in one gentle protocol, without painful manual squeezing.
