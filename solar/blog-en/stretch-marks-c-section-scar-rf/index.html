Stretch marks and a C-section scar: what RF microneedling can really do

During pregnancy the skin of the abdomen goes through an extreme test: stretching, a hormonal shift and sometimes surgery. Stretch marks and a caesarean scar are the two most common postpartum requests. Here is an honest look at what device-based treatments can do, and what nobody should promise.

SOLAR Beauty & Aesthetics Clinic · Sobha Hartland, Dubai · updated 5 Aug 2026

Let us start with the main point: no method erases stretch marks or a scar completely, not creams, not lasers, not RF. The realistic outcome is that stretch marks become significantly less visible: narrower, smoother and closer in colour to the surrounding skin. That is exactly what RF microneedling does well, and here is why.

Why creams do not work

Stretch marks are not surface marks but torn fibres in the dermis, the middle layer of the skin. Cosmetics simply do not reach that layer: they hydrate the epidermis, and that is where their range ends. To change the structure of a stretch mark you have to trigger collagen remodelling exactly where the tear happened.

What Virtue RF does to stretch marks

The device combines microneedles with radiofrequency heating: 36 insulated needles deliver RF energy precisely into the dermis, warming the tissue in the affected zone to a therapeutic 41 to 43 degrees. The skin responds with remodelling: damaged fibres are replaced by new collagen, and the marks become denser and closer to the level of the surrounding skin.

  • Fresh pink stretch marks respond fastest, so it is better not to postpone them;
  • mature white ones respond too, but need a longer course and patience: the result builds over one to three months after each session;
  • the surface evens out, the dips of the marks become shallower and the skin feels denser.

The caesarean scar

The logic is the same: microneedles loosen dense scar tissue while RF heating triggers its remodelling, so the scar gradually lightens, flattens and becomes more elastic. Two conditions matter:

  • only a fully healed scar can be treated, as a rule not earlier than 6 to 12 months after surgery and only after the doctor has examined it;
  • a tendency to keloid scarring is a contraindication: the consultation comes first, and the doctor assesses the scar type.

More about the treatment

Postpartum recovery is not only about skin

The postpartum abdomen is three separate tasks, and they are solved with different tools:

  • skin quality, meaning stretch marks, laxity and the scar: this is what Virtue RF is for;
  • muscles, meaning a weakened core: electromagnetic stimulation with Tesla Former helps here;
  • swelling and fluid retention: robotic lymphatic drainage massage Icoone takes care of that.

This is why a recovery plan is put together individually at the consultation, and it is often a combination of methods in the right order.

When you can start

RF microneedling is not performed during pregnancy or breastfeeding. The best window is after breastfeeding ends, once hormones have settled: the skin responds better to stimulation then. There is no need to rush, as both white stretch marks and a mature scar remain treatable later.

Frequently asked questions

Can I have the treatment while breastfeeding?

No, breastfeeding is a contraindication. You can start once you have finished breastfeeding, and the doctor will confirm this at the consultation.

Will the stretch marks disappear completely?

No, and do not trust anyone who promises that. The realistic outcome is that they become significantly less visible: narrower, smoother and closer in colour to the surrounding skin.

How many sessions are needed?

Usually three to four with an interval of four to six weeks; mature white stretch marks may need more. The doctor gives the exact plan after examining the area.

When can the C-section scar be treated?

Only after it has healed completely, as a rule not earlier than 6 to 12 months after surgery and with the doctor's approval. A tendency to keloids is discussed separately.

Does it hurt?

A numbing cream is applied first, and the needles are fine and insulated, so most patients find the session comfortable. Redness lasts one or two days afterwards.

Is it suitable for darker skin?

Yes. RF energy is not absorbed by melanin, so Virtue RF is safe for any skin phototype, unlike many laser methods.

Want a postpartum recovery plan?

A SOLAR doctor will examine the area, assess how mature the stretch marks and the scar are and put together a realistic plan: how many sessions, in what order and what to expect.
