HydraFacial or a Regular Facial: Which Works Better

A mechanical facial traumatises the skin; HydraFacial is a vacuum-based, atraumatic treatment. We explain how the vortex technology differs from a classic facial and why the skin glows immediately afterwards.

SOLAR Beauty & Aesthetics Clinic · Sobha Hartland, Dubai · updated 13 July 2026

"Facial" is a broad word for very different treatments. A classic mechanical facial is traumatic and needs recovery time. HydraFacial works more gently: a special vortex technology cleanses, exfoliates and hydrates the skin with serums all at once - without any squeezing.

How HydraFacial works

At its heart is vortex technology. The handpiece creates a swirl of fluid that:

  1. softens and flushes out the contents of the pores (rather than squeezing them);
  2. exfoliates dead cells with an acid peel;
  3. simultaneously delivers hydrating and antioxidant serums.

The skin is cleansed and instantly saturated with actives - hence the immediate glow with no redness.

More about HydraFacial

Who it suits

  • Dull, dehydrated skin - both "for an occasion" and on a regular basis.
  • Enlarged pores and blackheads without pronounced inflammation.
  • A first step before LED/photodynamic therapy or a course of treatments.
  • Sensitive skin for which a traumatic facial is not advisable.

How often to have it

To maintain the result - roughly every 3-4 weeks. A one-off treatment is a great way to refresh the skin before an important day.
