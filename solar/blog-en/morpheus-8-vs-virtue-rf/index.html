Morpheus 8 or Virtue RF: an honest comparison of two RF devices

Morpheus 8 is a well-known name in RF microneedling: Hollywood names talk about it. But brand recognition and the result for your skin are two different things. Here is a comparison of the two platforms on the substance.

SOLAR Beauty & Aesthetics Clinic · Sobha Hartland, Dubai · updated 5 Aug 2026

Let us start with what these devices share, because there is more of it than of the differences. Both platforms perform fractional RF microneedling: microneedles deliver radiofrequency energy into the skin, the dermis heats up and the body responds by producing new collagen. The indications overlap almost completely: lifting, skin density, pores, acne scars and fine lines.

Morpheus 8: strengths and nuances

Morpheus 8 by InMode is a solid, widely used platform. Its trump card is depth: up to 4 mm on the face, and deeper still with the Body handpiece, which is why it is popular for aggressive remodelling on the body. The nuances come from the needle design: in most configurations they are not insulated along their full length, so part of the energy is released on the way to the target, including the superficial layers. In practice that means a more noticeable treatment, more visible redness afterwards, and the need for careful settings on darker and tanned skin, which describes most people in Dubai.

Virtue RF: a bet on insulation and predictability

Virtue RF solves the same task with different engineering. The Smart RF handpiece carries 36 microneedles with 24K gold plating and full insulation: energy is released only at the tip, at a set depth of up to 3.5 mm. The epidermis is not overheated, so the risk of burns and post-inflammatory pigmentation is minimal on any phototype. The second feature is micro-pulsed delivery: RF is applied in short controlled pulses, heating the dermis evenly to a therapeutic 41 to 43 degrees. Histology studies report new collagen fibres and fibroblasts in the dermis 14 to 21 days after treatment, which means real tissue renewal rather than temporary swelling.

So what should you choose?

Here is what clinics rarely say out loud: the result of RF microneedling is defined by the protocol and the doctor's hands, not by the logo on the device. Depth, energy and pass density chosen for your skin decide the outcome. In competent hands both platforms give comparable results on the main facial goals.

We at SOLAR chose Virtue RF deliberately: our core work is the face, neck and delicate areas, our patients have every skin phototype, and most of them need to be back at work tomorrow. Insulated needles and gentle energy delivery give a predictable result here, without surprises. A detailed look at the technology is in our article on how RF microneedling works.

RF microneedling at SOLAR is performed on Virtue RF. A course is three to four treatments with an interval of four to six weeks; the plan and the cost are set by the doctor at the free consultation.

Frequently asked questions

Do you have Morpheus 8 at SOLAR?

No. We work on Virtue RF, and we chose it deliberately: insulated needles and gentle energy delivery are safer for darker skin and more comfortable for the patient. This article is here so you can compare the technologies and decide for yourself.

Morpheus 8 is more famous, so is it better?

Fame is the result of marketing and celebrity mentions, and that is normal. But the clinical result comes from the protocol and the doctor, not from brand recognition.

Will the Virtue RF result be worse?

On the main facial goals, meaning lifting, density, pores and scars, the result is comparable. Histology after Virtue RF shows new collagen as early as two to three weeks after treatment.

What is safer for darker skin?

Platforms with fully insulated needles such as Virtue RF: no energy is released in the epidermis where melanin sits, so the risk of pigmentation is minimal.

How much does it cost?

The cost depends on the area and the scope of work, and it is quoted at the free consultation after the doctor has examined your skin and mapped out the course.

How many treatments are needed?

A standard course is three to four treatments with an interval of four to six weeks. The result builds over one to three months as the new collagen matures.
