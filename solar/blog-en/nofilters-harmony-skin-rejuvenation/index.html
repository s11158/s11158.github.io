NoFilters: how rejuvenation without filters works on the Harmony platform

The trend of recent years is naturalness: even tone, glow and invisible pores. Alma, the manufacturer of the Harmony platform, built a signature protocol around exactly this request and named it literally, NoFilters. Here is what the effect is made of.

SOLAR Beauty & Aesthetics Clinic · Sobha Hartland, Dubai · updated 5 Aug 2026

The core idea of NoFilters is that perfect skin in a photograph is made of three independent parameters: even tone, smooth texture and glow. No single technology covers all three. That is why NoFilters is not one treatment but a protocol: a combination of Harmony modules, each responsible for its own part.

What the effect is made of

The Alma Harmony platform is modular: dozens of handpieces run on one base. Three of them are key in rejuvenation protocols:

  • ClearLift, a non-ablative Q-switched 1064 nm laser, the weekend laser: photoacoustic pulses stimulate renewal in the dermis without damaging the surface. No downtime at all, so it fits into a lunch break;
  • Dye-VL, narrow-spectrum light based on AFT technology: it clears pigment spots and vascular threads, evening out the tone, which is what ages skin most in photos;
  • ClearSkin, a non-ablative fractional 1540 nm laser with cooling: it works on texture, meaning pores, fine irregularities and acne marks, and stimulates collagen.

According to the manufacturer, combined ClearLift and ClearSkin protocols trigger new collagen production, so the skin becomes denser and smoother from the inside rather than being masked from the outside.

Who it suits

The NoFilters approach is for people who do not have one big problem but a sum of small ones: dullness, sun damage which after a summer in the UAE means almost everyone, uneven tone, enlarged pores, first lines and a tired look on camera. A separate audience is event preparation: a wedding, a shoot, an important week of meetings.

How a course is built

A programme is usually three to four visits with an interval of three to four weeks, and at each one the doctor combines modules according to the state of your skin. A session takes 30 to 60 minutes and there is no downtime: light pinkness fades within a couple of hours. Glow is visible after the first visit, while tone and texture even out gradually, towards the end of the course.

One important note for Dubai: light-based treatments are not performed on a fresh tan, and SPF 50 afterwards is mandatory. This is not a ban on summer, it simply means the course is planned with the doctor around your holidays and beach days.

The Harmony platform is already running at SOLAR and the clinic performs light-based treatments on it. Which modules to combine, and in what order, is decided by the doctor at the free consultation.

More about the platform

How this differs from skincare

A fair question: how is this better than a good facial or HydraFacial? The answer is the level of action. Skincare works with the surface: cleansing, hydration and a glow that lasts a week or two. Light-based protocols change the skin itself: they stimulate collagen, clear pigment from the dermis and tighten pores structurally. The ideal scheme is a combination: skincare maintains, device-based protocols renew.

Frequently asked questions

Does it hurt?

No. ClearLift is done without any numbing and feels like light warm clicks. The Dye-VL flashes and the fractional ClearSkin come with cooling; the most you feel is a short tingling.

How many treatments are needed?

Usually three to four visits with an interval of three to four weeks. Glow shows after the first one, while even tone and texture build towards the end of the course.

Can it be done in summer in Dubai?

Yes, with two conditions: not on a fresh tan, and with mandatory SPF 50 afterwards. The doctor will plan the schedule around your time in the sun.

Is there any downtime?

No. The light pinkness after a session fades within a few hours: these treatments are designed for going straight back to the office.

Will NoFilters remove deep wrinkles and a sagging jawline?

No, and that is honestly not its job. For contour lifting there are SMAS technologies, and for deep collagen stimulation there is RF microneedling. NoFilters is about surface quality: tone, texture and glow.
