Good to know

Aesthetic & beauty clinic — FAQ

Where is SOLAR located in Dubai?

SOLAR is based in Sobha Hartland, Mohammed Bin Rashid City — the geographic heart of Dubai, minutes from Downtown, Business Bay and Meydan and an easy drive from Palm Jumeirah, Dubai Marina, Jumeirah, Dubai Hills and DIFC.

Which areas of Dubai does SOLAR serve?

Clients come to SOLAR from across the city — Downtown, Business Bay, Palm Jumeirah, Dubai Marina and JBR, Jumeirah, Dubai Hills Estate, DIFC and City Walk, and the wider MBR City. The central location keeps every neighbourhood a short drive away.

What treatments does the clinic offer?

A full aesthetic and beauty menu led by a doctor: HIFU/SMAS lifting, RF microneedling, injectables (botulinum toxin, fillers, mesotherapy), Hydrafacial, LED therapy Heleo4, laser hair removal, peels, body contouring and skincare.

Is SOLAR a clinic or a salon?

A clinic. Treatments are planned and performed by a doctor on certified medical equipment, with sterile single-use supplies and full clinic hygiene standards — not sold as one-off salon services.

Do you offer home visits in these areas?

Yes. For many skincare, injectable and light-based treatments a specialist can visit your home, villa or hotel in the areas we serve. Device treatments (HIFU/SMAS, RF microneedling, laser) are performed at the Sobha Hartland clinic. We confirm the format when you book.
