When people in Dubai search for a "beautician near me" or a specific treatment marked near me — Hydrafacial, SMAS lifting, RF microneedling, LED therapy, Botox or a beauty drip — they usually want one thing: convenient and close. SOLAR solves this differently from an ordinary clinic: we come to you. Our doctor or specialist travels to your home, villa or hotel anywhere in Dubai — with professional portable equipment and sterile supplies.

Let's be honest: SOLAR is a full clinic in Sobha Hartland (central Dubai) and, at the same time, a mobile service. So you get both the convenience of "near me" and the safety of a real clinic: licensed specialists, certified equipment and medical hygiene standards — at your home, with no drive across the city and no waiting in line.

At-home visit FAQ

Do you really do home visits across Dubai?

Yes. SOLAR is a clinic in Sobha Hartland and a mobile service. A doctor or specialist comes to your home, villa or hotel anywhere in Dubai with professional portable equipment and sterile single-use supplies. Most skincare, injectable and LED treatments are done right at your place.

Which treatments can be done at home and which only in clinic?

At home: Hydrafacial, LED therapy Heleo4, injectables (Botox, fillers, mesotherapy), IV drips, peels and skincare, massage and lymphatic drainage, manicure, pedicure, hair, brows and lashes. In clinic (or by arrangement with a doctor visit): HIFU/SMAS lifting, RF microneedling, laser hair removal — these need stationary certified hardware. We always state the format clearly when you book.

How fast can you arrive?

It depends on the area and time. For central areas — Downtown, Business Bay, Meydan, Jumeirah, DIFC, Dubai Creek Harbour — we arrive fastest, often the same day. For the rest of Dubai we agree a convenient slot when you book.

Is it as safe and hygienic as a clinic?

Yes. Visits are carried out by licensed specialists; we use sterile single-use supplies and certified equipment and follow the same medical hygiene standards as in the clinic. Convenience of format — with no compromise on safety.

Which areas of Dubai do you cover?

All of Dubai: Downtown, Business Bay, DIFC, Dubai Marina, JBR, Palm Jumeirah, Jumeirah 1/2/3, Meydan, Nad Al Sheba, Dubai Hills, Emirates Hills, Meadows, Springs, JVC, JVT, Al Barsha, Dubai Creek Harbour, Mirdif, Silicon Oasis, Arjan, Sports City and more. Dubai is one service zone for us.

What do I need to prepare at home?

The minimum: a clean, calm space, a table or couch and access to a power socket. Everything else — equipment, supplies, materials — we bring. We'll share details when we confirm the booking.

How much does the visit and treatments cost?

The price depends on the treatment, the number of zones or sessions, and the visit area; it's confirmed at a free consultation. Fully transparent, nothing pushed.

How do I book a home visit?

Message us on WhatsApp at +971 50 734 5432 with the treatment, your area and a convenient time. The first consultation is free and with no obligation.
