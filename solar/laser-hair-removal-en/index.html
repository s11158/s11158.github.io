The method

How laser hair removal works

Laser hair removal targets the root, not the skin surface, for long-lasting results.

1

Melanin heating

The laser is absorbed by pigment in the hair and heats the follicle, disabling the growth zone without harming surrounding skin.

2

Long-lasting

After a course, hair grows finer, lighter and less often until the area is smooth.

3

Comfort

A built-in cooling system protects the skin, so treatment is easy even on sensitive areas.

FAQ

Honest answers

Does it hurt?

A modern laser with cooling is comfortable: you feel light warmth and a slight snap, without significant pain.

Is it safe for darker and tanned skin?

Yes. The medical laser is tuned to your skin type. On an active tan the doctor picks safe settings or reschedules.

How many sessions are needed?

Usually 6–8, 4–6 weeks apart, because the laser targets hair in its active growth phase.

Is it permanent?

It's long-term reduction: most hair is gone; occasional regrowth is handled with maintenance sessions.

How do I prepare?

Shave the area a day before, no plucking or waxing for 3–4 weeks, and avoid strong sun before and after.
