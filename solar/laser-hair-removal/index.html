The method

How laser hair removal works

A laser emits a precisely calibrated beam of light that is absorbed by the melanin (pigment) inside the hair. The light energy converts to heat and disables the hair follicle at its growth zone — without harming the surrounding skin. Because only actively growing hairs respond, a course of sessions is needed to catch every hair in its growth phase.

The result is a steady, permanent reduction in hair: with each session hair grows back finer, lighter and sparser, until the treated area stays smooth for the long term.

How the visit goes

Your session step by step

Consultation & skin test

The specialist assesses your skin and hair, sets the laser to your phototype and, if needed, does a small test patch. Free and with no obligation.

The treatment

The area is treated pass by pass with the cooled handpiece. From a few minutes for the upper lip to around an hour for full body.

Back to your day

No downtime. Mild warmth or slight redness may appear and settles within hours. Avoid direct sun and use SPF.

Your course

Sessions are repeated every few weeks. Over 6–8 visits hair becomes finer and sparser until the area stays smooth.

Common questions

Honest answers

Is it safe for tanned or darker skin?

Yes. The laser and its settings are chosen for your skin phototype, which is why we assess your skin first. This makes the treatment safe and effective on tanned and darker skin. We may advise avoiding fresh tanning right before a session for the best, safest result.

How many sessions will I need?

Hair grows in cycles, so a course of about 6–8 sessions a few weeks apart is typically needed to treat every hair in its active phase. Some areas and hair types need more or fewer; the doctor gives you a realistic plan at consultation.

Does it hurt?

Modern lasers with contact cooling make it comfortable — most people feel only a light, warm snap. Sensitive areas may feel a little more, but there is no numbing needed and no downtime.

How should I prepare?

Shave the area a day before (do not wax or pluck, as the root must stay intact), avoid heavy sun and self-tan, and come with clean skin free of lotions. The specialist will confirm details for your area.

Is there downtime?

None. You can return to your day immediately. Keep the area out of strong sun for a day or two and use sunscreen.
