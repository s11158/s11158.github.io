The method

How ULFIT works

One device solves two body-shaping problems at once — it reduces localized fat and tightens skin, without damaging the surface.

ULFIT (Classys) is a 3rd-generation HIFU device. It focuses high-intensity ultrasound at a precise depth (6, 9 and 13 mm cartridges). The energy passes through the skin surface without harming it and concentrates in the target layer: within subcutaneous fat it locally heats and destroys a portion of the fat cells, while in the deep skin layers it triggers collagen production and tightens tissue. Circular energy delivery (Macro-Focused Circular Ultrasound) spreads it quickly and evenly — which is what makes ULFIT well suited to the body.

1

Fat-cell destruction

At the focal point the ultrasound locally heats subcutaneous fat until cells break down. The body then clears them naturally over time.

2

Skin tightening

At the level of the deep skin and SMAS, heat stimulates new collagen and elastin — the skin over the area tightens instead of sagging.

3

Surface stays intact

The energy works pointwise inside the tissue. The skin surface is left whole — no incisions, no needles, no downtime.

Honest about the name

"Non-surgical liposuction" — what it really is

It is an established marketing term, not surgery. Understanding the difference keeps expectations aligned with the result.

ULFIT — without surgery

  • Ultrasound destroys a portion of fat cells; the body clears them gradually
  • Suited to localized pockets and lax skin
  • No incisions, anaesthesia or downtime
  • Gentle, gradual result over weeks
  • Bonus — skin tightening

Surgical liposuction

  • Fat is suctioned through incisions under anaesthesia
  • Removes a larger volume in one session
  • Requires recovery, compression garments, and carries surgical risks
  • Does not tighten skin — excess skin may need separate correction
  • Performed by a surgeon in an operating room
The key point: ULFIT is body contouring for localized areas, not a way to lose weight. The method is designed for people close to their normal weight who have specific fat pockets or skin laxity. With significant excess weight, weight loss comes first. Whether the method suits you is determined by a doctor at the consultation.

The process

The procedure step by step

Doctor consultation (free)

The doctor assesses the areas, skin quality and weight, and discusses a realistic result, indications and contraindications.

Area mapping & depth setup

For each area, the cartridge (6 / 9 / 13 mm) and parameters are selected — for fat, for skin, or both layers.

ULFIT session

The applicator glides over the skin delivering ultrasound. You feel warmth and mild tingling. Small areas 30–60 min, large areas 60–90. No anaesthesia.

Results over 8–12 weeks

Contour and firmness improve gradually as fat clears and collagen renews. Repeat sessions if needed, 8–12 weeks apart.

Good to know: for 24 hours after the procedure, avoid intense workouts, sauna and alcohol. Hydration and activity help the body clear the broken-down fat.

FAQ

Straight answers

Is this real liposuction?

No. "Non-surgical liposuction" is an established name for the method, not surgery. ULFIT does not suction fat through incisions: focused ultrasound heats and destroys a portion of the fat cells in a localized area, and the body then clears them gradually. It is body contouring, not a weight-loss method.

Who is it for, and who is it not for?

The method targets localized fat pockets (abdomen, flanks, thighs) in people close to their normal weight, as well as mild skin laxity. It is not a substitute for weight loss in cases of significant excess weight. A doctor determines suitability and contraindications at the consultation.

Does it hurt?

ULFIT is a 3rd-generation device with circular energy delivery designed for reduced discomfort. Most people feel warmth and mild tingling. No anaesthesia or preparation is required.

How many sessions and when do results show?

Many people see noticeable results after 1–3 sessions per area, spaced 8–12 weeks apart. Skin tightens and the contour refines gradually over 8–12 weeks, with continued improvement for several months.

Is there any downtime?

There is no downtime. Temporary redness or mild tenderness of the area may occur and settles quickly. For 24 hours it is advised to avoid intense workouts, sauna and alcohol.

How is ULFIT different from Ultraformer MPT?

Both are HIFU devices by Classys. We use Ultraformer MPT primarily for facial SMAS lifting; ULFIT is a dedicated device with macro-focused ultrasound and 6/9/13 mm cartridges for body contouring (fat + skin). The doctor will advise which suits you.

Is there evidence it works?

Yes, for the method. HIFU for non-invasive body contouring reduces waist circumference by ablating subcutaneous fat and tightens skin — supported by clinical studies and a systematic review (see the research section). Results are individual.
