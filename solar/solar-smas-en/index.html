The method

What SMAS lifting is and why it works

SMAS (Superficial Musculo-Aponeurotic System) is the musculo-aponeurotic framework of the face that plastic surgeons tighten during a full facelift. It is precisely the weakening of this layer with age that leads to a “sagging” oval, jowls and downturned corners of the mouth. SMAS lifting on the Ultraformer MPT device lets us act on this same framework with high-intensity focused ultrasound (HIFU), without damaging the skin surface.

The ultrasound creates thermal coagulation points (TCP) in the tissue at precisely defined depths — 1.5, 2.0, 3.0 and 4.5 mm. The body reads them as a signal to renew and launches neocollagenesis — the production of new type I collagen and tissue remodelling. As a result the skin densifies, the facial oval tightens and the contour becomes sharper — naturally and gradually, with no “stretched mask” effect.

Common questions

Honest answers

How is SMAS lifting different from a surgical facelift?

The Ultraformer MPT acts on the same musculo-aponeurotic layer (SMAS) as a surgeon, but it does so with focused ultrasound through the skin — without incisions, anesthesia or recovery. The result is softer and shorter-lived than surgery, but without the risks or downtime. It’s a way to postpone surgery for years.

When will I see the result?

A light lifting effect is noticeable immediately thanks to tissue contraction. The main result develops gradually over 8–12 weeks while new collagen is produced.

How many procedures are needed?

In most cases one procedure a year is enough. According to clinical data, more than 80% of patients reported a marked improvement after a single session (p<0.000005). The doctor will tailor the frequency to your skin.

Is there a recovery period?

There is no downtime. Brief redness, mild swelling or sensitivity are possible and resolve within a few hours or days. You can return to work and your usual routine right away.

How is your device different from “HIFU for 499 AED”?

“HIFU” is often used to sell outdated or unlicensed devices with unpredictable results. We work on the original Ultraformer MPT with certified cartridges and a protocol grounded in clinical data. Safety and a predictable result are not where you should cut corners when it comes to your face.
