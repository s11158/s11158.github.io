The treatment

What a HydraFacial actually does

A HydraFacial is a medical-grade hydradermabrasion facial. Instead of harsh scrubbing or manual squeezing, it uses gentle vortex and serum technology to cleanse, exfoliate, painlessly extract and hydrate the skin — all in a single, comfortable session. There are no needles and no downtime, so you leave looking instantly refreshed.

It works in three signature steps that work with the skin rather than against it, which is why it suits almost every skin type and gives a visible glow you can see the same day.

1

Cleanse & peel

A gentle cleanse and light peel loosen dead skin cells and excess oil, uncovering fresh, smoother skin underneath.

2

Extract & hydrate

Painless vortex extraction clears out pores and blackheads while the skin is flooded with intense, soothing hydration.

3

Fuse & protect

The skin is infused with antioxidants, peptides and hyaluronic acid to nourish, plump and seal in that radiant glow.

The result: instantly brighter, smoother, hydrated skin with cleaner, less visible pores. It is comfortable, quick and suitable for all skin types — ideal as a monthly reset or a pre-occasion treat. Optional boosters can be added to target a specific concern such as brightening, deep hydration or an anti-ageing lift; your specialist will suggest what fits your skin.

Common questions

Honest answers

How is HydraFacial different from a regular facial?

A HydraFacial uses a medical-grade device to cleanse, exfoliate, extract and hydrate the skin in one controlled session with vortex serum technology. Unlike a manual spa facial, extractions are painless and gentle, and hydrating serums are infused deep into the skin — so the result is more consistent and visible immediately.

Is there any downtime?

None. Your skin looks refreshed and glowing straight away, so you can return to your day, apply make-up or attend an event immediately. That is exactly why so many clients book it before a flight or occasion.

How often should I do it?

For a healthy glow, many people enjoy a HydraFacial about once a month, in line with the skin's natural renewal cycle. For congestion or a specific concern, a short course can help. Your specialist recommends a realistic rhythm at consultation.

Does it hurt?

No — there are no needles and the extraction is painless. Most clients describe it as a cool, gentle massage. It is one of the most comfortable facials available and is suitable for sensitive skin.

Can I do it before an event or wedding?

Yes — it is one of the best pre-event treatments because there is no downtime and skin looks instantly brighter and smoother. For a big occasion we suggest booking a day or two before so your glow is at its peak.
