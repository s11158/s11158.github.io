The method

How Botox works

Botox smooths lines caused by active expression, without affecting the surrounding tissue.

1

Muscle relaxation

Micro-doses of botulinum toxin temporarily reduce a muscle's activity, so the crease above it smooths out.

2

Natural

The goal isn't to freeze the face but to soften excess movement while keeping it expressive.

3

Prevention

Regular treatments stop dynamic lines from becoming fixed, static wrinkles.

FAQ

Honest answers

Will my face look frozen?

No. With correct dosing your expression stays — only the excess creases go, and the face stays alive.

Does it hurt?

The needles are very fine and feel like light pricks. Topical numbing is available on request.

When will I see results and how long do they last?

Smoothing builds over 3–14 days and lasts 3–4 months; then the treatment is repeated.

Is it safe?

Yes, when performed by a doctor by indication — botulinum toxin has been used in aesthetics for decades. It has contraindications, so a consultation is required.
