The method

How Botox works

Botox smooths wrinkles caused by active facial expressions, without affecting the surrounding tissue.

Botulinum toxin type A is a purified protein that temporarily blocks the signal from nerve to muscle. The muscle stops over-contracting and the crease above it relaxes. The effect is reversible: over time signalling recovers and expression returns fully.

1

Muscle relaxation

Micro-doses of botulinum toxin temporarily reduce the activity of a facial muscle, and the crease above it smooths out.

2

Natural

The goal is not to freeze the face but to remove excess movement while keeping liveliness and emotion.

3

Prevention

Regular treatments stop dynamic wrinkles from turning into permanent static ones.

FAQ

Honest answers

Will my face look frozen?

No. With the right dosing your expressions stay natural — only excess creases go, the face remains alive and expressive.

Does it hurt?

The needles are very fine and feel like light pricks. Topical anaesthetic is applied on request.

When do results show and how long do they last?

Smoothing builds over 3–14 days and lasts about 3–4 months; then the procedure is repeated.

Is it safe and is there evidence?

Yes, when performed by a doctor by indication. The efficacy and safety of botulinum toxin type A for glabellar lines are confirmed by randomized trials and meta-analyses (see "What the research says"). The procedure has contraindications — a consultation is required.

How is Botox different from fillers?

Botox relaxes the muscle and removes dynamic (expression) wrinkles. Fillers restore volume and static folds. They are often combined, but they are different products for different goals.

Who performs the procedure?

A certified GP doctor with over 15 years of experience, at a DHA licensed clinic in Sobha Hartland.

Can I have it before an important event?

Yes, but with a buffer: the result unfolds by day 10–14. Plan the procedure at least 2 weeks before the event.
