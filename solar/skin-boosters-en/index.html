The method

What skin boosters are

A skin booster is an injectable or transdermal treatment that improves the quality of the skin: hydration, elasticity, tone and density. It does not create shape and it does not add volume. That is the main difference from everything else on an aesthetic menu, and it is worth being precise about it. Pure hyaluronic boosters suit dryness and dullness; polynucleotides suit thin, sensitive, post-inflammatory skin; exosomes are for repair and for hair; needle-free options are for those who do not want injections at all. The doctor chooses after examining the skin, not from a price list.

1

Booster is not a filler

A filler is a dense gel placed under the skin to build volume and contour: cheeks, jawline, chin. A booster is a fluid hyaluronic acid or a bioactive complex distributed within the skin to make it moist, dense and light-reflecting. A filler changes the shape of a face; a booster changes its texture.

2

Booster is not classic mesotherapy either

Mesotherapy delivers a cocktail of vitamins, amino acids and trace elements as a course of frequent small sessions. A booster is a concentrated product with a longer working time, usually one to three sessions. In practice the two are often combined: mesotherapy feeds the skin, a booster rebuilds its structure.

3

Profhilo is bio-remodelling, not filling

Profhilo is a high-concentration hyaluronic acid that is not cross-linked into a stiff gel. Instead of holding a shape it spreads through the tissue from a small number of injection points and stimulates the skin to produce its own collagen and elastin. The result is firmer, better hydrated skin with no added volume at all - which is exactly why it suits the neck, the decolletage and skin that looks tired rather than deflated.

FAQ

Honest answers

How is Profhilo different from fillers?

A filler is a dense gel that stays where it is placed and builds volume or contour. Profhilo is a fluid, highly concentrated hyaluronic acid that spreads through the tissue and stimulates your own collagen and elastin. It changes the quality and firmness of the skin without changing the shape of the face, which is why it works on the neck and hands where fillers have little to offer.

How many sessions do I need?

The standard Profhilo protocol is two sessions four weeks apart, then a repeat course after six to nine months. Mesotherapy and Dermapen protocols are usually planned as a short course of three to four sessions, and PRX-T33 can be done as a single pre-event treatment. The number of sessions, the product and the area are what the price depends on, and it is agreed with you at the free consultation.

When will I see the result?

Hydration and glow appear within the first week. Firmness and density build over four to eight weeks, because the skin needs that time to produce new collagen. This is a treatment that rewards patience rather than delivering everything on the day.

Does it hurt?

Profhilo uses a small number of injection points, and most clients describe it as brief discomfort rather than pain. For Dermapen protocols a topical anaesthetic is applied first. Small papules at the Profhilo points are expected and settle within a few hours.

How long does it last?

The effect of a completed course generally holds for six to nine months and fades gradually. Skin condition, age, sun exposure and daily care all influence how long the result stays, and in the Dubai climate consistent sunscreen makes a measurable difference.
