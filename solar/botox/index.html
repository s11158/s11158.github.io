The treatment

How Botox works

Botox is a purified botulinum toxin that gently relaxes the specific muscles responsible for dynamic wrinkles — the lines that form when you frown, squint or raise your brows. By calming just those muscles, the overlying skin smooths out and stops creasing, while the rest of your face keeps moving and expressing naturally.

The treatment itself is quick: a few precise injections with a very fine needle, and a typical session takes only about 10–15 minutes. There is no cutting and no downtime — you walk out and carry on with your day.

Common questions

Honest answers

Will I look frozen?

No. Our doctor uses conservative, natural-looking dosing to soften lines while keeping your expressions. The goal is a fresher, more rested version of you — not a frozen or overdone look. We would rather do a little and add later than do too much.

Does it hurt?

Very little. The injections use a very fine needle, so most people feel only a tiny pinch. The whole session takes around 10–15 minutes and needs no numbing for most areas.

When will I see results and how long do they last?

First effects appear in about 3–5 days and the full result settles at around two weeks. It typically lasts about 3–4 months, and with regular treatments results can last longer over time.

Is it safe?

Botox is one of the most studied and widely used aesthetic treatments. At SOLAR it is always performed by a licensed doctor using a certified product, after a proper assessment of your health and suitability. The procedure has contraindications, which is why a consultation comes first.

What's the difference between Botox and fillers?

Botox relaxes muscles to soften dynamic lines caused by movement, such as forehead and frown lines. Fillers add volume to restore contours or plump areas that have lost fullness. They solve different things and often work well together — the doctor helps you choose the right option at your consultation.
