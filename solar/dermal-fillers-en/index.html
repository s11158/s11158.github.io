The method

What dermal fillers are

A dermal filler is a soft gel based on hyaluronic acid, a substance the skin already produces. Injected at the right depth, it adds volume exactly where the face has lost it and lifts or defines the contour. The gel gradually breaks down and is absorbed by the body, so the result is reversible and the treatment can be repeated.

1

Volume where it is missing

With age, cheeks, temples and the under eye area lose fat and bone support. The filler restores that support and the face looks rested again.

2

Contour and proportion

Jawline and chin fillers build a cleaner lower-face line and balance the profile, which is why they are the fastest-growing filler requests in Dubai.

3

Smoothing of folds

Nasolabial folds and marionette lines soften once the volume above them is restored and the crease itself is filled with a lighter gel.

Evidence base

What the research says

Hyaluronic acid fillers are among the most studied products in aesthetic medicine. Their efficacy and safety for facial volume loss and folds are supported by randomized controlled trials and long-term follow-up studies.

RCT

Randomized controlled trials

Split-face and controlled studies of hyaluronic acid fillers for nasolabial folds show a significant improvement against untreated or comparator sides at the primary endpoint, with good tolerability.

Long-term

Follow-up studies

Prospective studies of cheek and mid-face augmentation report that improvement in volume is maintained for many months, with a gradual return towards baseline as the gel is absorbed.

Safety

Reviews of adverse events

Systematic reviews describe most side effects as mild and temporary (swelling, bruising) and stress that anatomical knowledge and proper technique are the main factors in preventing rare vascular complications.

The studies refer to the method (hyaluronic acid fillers), not to a specific clinic. Results are individual and depend on anatomy, product and the doctor's skill.

FAQ

Honest answers

Will it look natural?

Yes, if the volume is right. The doctor works in half-millilitre steps and stops when the face looks rested, not when the syringe is empty. Overfilled faces come from too much product, not from fillers as such.

Does it hurt?

A topical anaesthetic is applied first and most gels contain lidocaine. Clients describe pressure rather than pain; the under eye and nose areas are done with a cannula, which is gentler.

How long do dermal fillers last?

From 6 to 18 months depending on the zone, the gel and your metabolism. Firmer contour gels in the jawline and chin tend to last longer than soft gels in mobile areas.

Can fillers be removed?

Hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved with a special enzyme within a day or two if you want to adjust the result. This is one of the reasons we work mainly with HA fillers.

Fillers or Botox - which do I need?

They solve different problems. Botox relaxes the muscles behind expression lines; fillers restore volume and contour. Many clients combine them, and the doctor will tell you honestly at the consultation which one your face actually needs.
