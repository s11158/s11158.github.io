The method

What hair loss treatment is

Hair loss treatment is not one procedure but a course. First the doctor finds out why the hair is falling, then works on the environment the hair grows in: blood supply to the follicle, the condition of the scalp and the nutrients the growth phase needs. Hair grows in cycles, so the aim of a course is to move more follicles back into the active phase and keep them there.

1

The scalp comes first

Hair grows out of skin. Inflammation, excess sebum, flaking or dryness weaken the follicle long before you notice thinning, and this is where treatment starts.

2

Nutrition of the follicle

Scalp mesotherapy delivers vitamins, amino acids and trace elements directly into the skin around the follicle, where a tablet or a shampoo simply does not reach in that concentration.

3

A signal to the growth phase

Exosome therapy and low-level light therapy work on the signalling environment of the follicle, supporting the shift from the resting phase back to growth.

Why SOLAR

Hair loss has causes, and they are not all on the scalp

Hair is one of the first things to react to what happens in the body, so treating only the surface rarely holds.

Find the cause before the course

Thyroid and hormonal shifts, iron, ferritin and vitamin D deficiency, a strict diet, illness or a stressful few months, and in Dubai also hard water, chlorine and constant air conditioning. The doctor will ask about all of it and may recommend blood tests before starting.

Treatment starts with the scalp

A close examination of the scalp shows the state of the skin, the sebum balance, inflammation and how dense the hair actually is between the visible parting and the rest of the head. HELEO4 LED therapy is used here both to assess the scalp and as part of the treatment.

A course, and time

The hair cycle is measured in months, not weeks. A realistic course is 6 to 8 sessions, and the result is judged at around three months, with maintenance afterwards. Anyone promising new hair in two weeks is selling something else.

Where our methods stop

With pronounced androgenetic alopecia, a receding hairline or visible bald areas, the follicle is no longer there to support, and the method of choice becomes a hair transplant. We do not perform hair transplants or PRP. If that is your case, we will say so at the consultation and you will not spend money on a course that cannot deliver.

Evidence base

What the research says

The methods we use are studied in dermatology, though the evidence differs in strength for each of them.

Mesotherapy

Clinical studies of scalp injections

Clinical studies of scalp mesotherapy with vitamin and amino acid cocktails report reduced shedding and improved hair density in people with diffuse hair loss, while noting that protocols differ between studies and larger controlled trials are still needed.

Exosomes

Early clinical evidence

Research into exosome preparations for hair growth is recent: pilot clinical studies and case series describe improvement in hair density and thickness, and the field is moving towards larger controlled trials.

LED

Low-level light therapy

Randomized controlled trials of low-level light therapy for pattern hair loss show an increase in hair count compared with sham devices, with a good safety profile and effect that depends on regular, repeated sessions.

The studies refer to the methods, not to a specific clinic. Results are individual and depend on the type of hair loss, its cause and how far it has progressed.

The process

Step by step

Doctor's consultation (free)

The doctor examines the scalp and hair, asks about health, medication, diet and stress, and decides whether blood tests are needed before treatment.

Scalp diagnostics

A close look at the scalp and hair shafts: skin condition, sebum, inflammation, density in the affected zone against a control zone. This is the baseline everything is measured against later.

The course

Scalp mesotherapy, Dermapen exosome therapy or LED sessions, alone or combined, usually every one to two weeks. Each session takes 30 to 60 minutes.

Review and maintenance

At around three months the doctor compares the scalp with the baseline and agrees a maintenance schedule with you.

Good to know: do not wash your hair for 24 hours after a scalp session, skip sauna, pool and intense sport for a day, and avoid aggressive colouring in the days around a session.

FAQ

Honest answers

How soon will I see a result?

The scalp feels better within the first month and shedding usually slows within 4 to 6 weeks. Visible density takes 2 to 3 months, because that is how long the hair cycle needs. Judge the course at three months, not at the third session.

How many sessions do I need?

Usually 6 to 8 sessions, every one to two weeks, followed by maintenance. The exact number and the cost of the course are agreed at the free consultation and depend on the cause and how long the hair has been falling.

Will this help if I already have bald patches?

Honestly, no. Our methods support follicles that are still alive: they work on density, hair quality and scalp health. Where the hairline has receded or the scalp shows through, the follicle is gone and no injection brings it back. In that situation the method of choice is a hair transplant, and the doctor will tell you so directly.

Do you do hair transplants or PRP?

No. SOLAR does not perform hair transplants and does not offer PRP. We work with early and moderate hair loss, with hair density and with scalp health, and we will tell you at the consultation if your case needs something we do not do.

What should I do at home between sessions?

Wash your hair as often as it needs, with a gentle shampoo and a filter or softener if your water is hard. Avoid tight styling and hot tools, correct any deficiency the blood tests show, and give it protein and sleep. Home care will not replace the course, but neglecting it can cancel it out.
