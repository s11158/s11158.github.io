The method

What is mesotherapy

Mesotherapy is a method, not a single product. A tailored cocktail of hyaluronic acid, vitamins, amino acids, peptides or polynucleotides is delivered into the upper layers of the skin in very small doses, spread over many points. Two things happen at once: the skin receives the actives directly, and the micro-channels themselves trigger the natural repair response that thickens and firms the tissue. Mesolifting is the same method used with lifting-oriented cocktails and a denser point pattern, most often on the face and neck.

1

The actives go where they work

A serum applied on top of the skin stops at the barrier. Mesotherapy crosses that barrier, so hydration and nutrition reach the living layers instead of evaporating from the surface.

2

The skin repairs itself

Each micro-channel is a controlled signal. In response the skin produces its own collagen and elastin over the following weeks, which is why the result keeps improving after the session, not only on the day.

3

With needles or without

Injectable mesotherapy uses a Dermapen device with fine sterile needles. Needle free mesotherapy uses Dermadrop TDA, which pushes the actives through the barrier with pressure and oxygen instead. SOLAR offers both, so a fear of needles is not a reason to skip the method.

Evidence base

What the research says

Mesotherapy and microneedling have been studied for skin quality and for the scalp. The evidence base is smaller than for fillers, and the honest summary is that results are consistent but moderate.

Controlled trials

Skin quality

Randomized and split-face studies of intradermal hyaluronic acid report improvement in hydration, elasticity and skin roughness compared with untreated skin or with topical application alone.

Microneedling

Texture and scars

Controlled studies of microneedling describe improvement in skin texture, fine lines and atrophic scars after a course of sessions, with the effect building over the weeks that follow.

Scalp

Hair density

Trials of scalp mesotherapy and microneedling, alone or combined with standard hair loss therapy, report increases in hair count and density, with the combination generally performing better than either approach on its own.

The studies refer to the method (mesotherapy and microneedling), not to a specific clinic. Results are individual and depend on the initial condition, the cocktail and the course.

FAQ

Honest answers

Does it hurt, and is there an option without needles?

The Dermapen route is done under topical anaesthetic and feels like light vibration and scratching rather than pain. If needles are a hard no for you, Dermadrop TDA delivers the same kind of actives with pressure and oxygen instead, with no injections at all. Having both under one roof is the reason most needle-averse clients still get a result here.

How many sessions do I need?

Usually 4-6 sessions every 2-3 weeks, then maintenance every three to six months. One session before an event gives glow; skin quality changes over a course. The cost of the course depends on the cocktail and the zones and is agreed at the free consultation.

How is mesotherapy different from skin boosters?

Skin boosters are a few deep deposits of a dense hyaluronic acid gel that hydrate over months. Mesotherapy is many shallow points of a lighter cocktail that also stimulates repair through the micro-channels. Boosters are about reservoir hydration, mesotherapy is about skin quality and can be tuned with different actives, including for the scalp.

When will I see the result?

Freshness within two to three days, collagen-driven firmness from around three to four weeks, the full change after the course.

Can it be done in summer?

Yes, with sunscreen and sensible sun habits for the following week. Mesotherapy is not a peel and does not remove the outer layer, so it is one of the safer options for the Dubai summer.
